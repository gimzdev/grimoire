Fish where the zone's fishing level is at or below your skill. Start in the starting zones and the zones beside them, whose levels are below zero, and move to the next table each time your skill reaches its level. In Classic, a cast in a zone below its fishing level catches nothing. The level-330 waters sit above the skill cap of 300 the client shows.

Fishing is a secondary profession, learned beside your two main ones. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 16, a Fishing Rack gives its lures to everyone, and the Bloated Fish caught with them drop more often.

## Where to fish

Each table is one fishing level. A subzone with a level of its own is named before its zone. Fishing levels, catches and their chances are Classic’s (patch 1.12, from VMaNGOS): Forever may have changed them. Forever's new zones are not in this data.

## Fishing's camp crafts

Fishing has three camp crafts in the launch client: the Fishing Bowl at skill 20, the Fishing Rack at 140 and the Fishing Hut at 300. Blizzard's sample gives the Fishing Bowl 8% to all stats for everyone at the camp, in place of Blessing of Kings. [Professions in Forever](/guides/professions) has how camps work.

## Sources

Fishing levels and catches come from the VMaNGOS world database (skill_fishing_base_level and the fishing loot, patch 1.12); the rule that a cast below the level catches nothing is VMaNGOS's server code. Forever's changes come from Blizzard's [hotfix list](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281) and the launch client's data as [Professions in Forever](/guides/professions) records them.
