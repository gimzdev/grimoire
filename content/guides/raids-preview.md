Forever's first raid tier opens on **December 9, 2026**, five weeks after launch. It has three raids of three different sizes, all for level 60 and all resetting weekly, and tier pieces can drop in all of them, so a ten-player group and a forty-player guild are both gearing toward the same sets.

| Raid | Size | Bosses | Where | Legacy point for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Barrow Deeps](/dungeons/barrow-deeps) | 10 players | 8 | Inside Mount Hyjal | Defeating all 8 bosses |
| [Hyjal Summit](/dungeons/hyjal-summit) | 20 players | 13 | Summit of Mount Hyjal | Defeating all 13 bosses |
| [Onyxia’s Lair](/dungeons/onyxias-lair) | 40 players | 1 | Dustwallow Marsh | Defeating Onyxia |

Only Onyxia’s Lair needs an attunement, the Drakefire Amulet below; any level-60 group can walk into the other two. All three reset once a week. The two new raids open on December 9, so the names of their bosses below come from previews, the raids' Legacy challenges as Warcraft Tavern, Warcraft Wiki and Blizzard Watch list them (read on November 14). Grimoire prints a name only when two of them spell it the same, in the order two of them agree on. No open database has their abilities or loot.

## Barrow Deeps

The night elves' sacred barrow, hidden beneath Kalimdor inside Mount Hyjal, held Illidan Stormrage prisoner for ten thousand years. It is a ten-player raid with eight bosses and several ways in. Blizzard has said every raid tier will have at least one ten-player raid, so small groups will always have one to run.

**Entrances:** through Mount Hyjal, Winterspring and Blackmaw Hold. No open source gives their map coordinates.

**Bosses:** Deepscar Matriarch, Khalith the Dreadspinner, Amethrax, Ravus and Darlissa, Elder Tangleclaw, Well of Sorrow, Del'lynar Songwood and Sonya Darkhallow. All three sources give the same eight.

## Hyjal Summit

A new twenty-player raid at the summit of Mount Hyjal, near the World Tree Nordrassil, where something is draining the mountain's power. It opens with a fight against an ancient guardian, then lets the raid choose its route across the mountain, through ruins above and below ground, before the final encounters. Thirteen bosses make it the largest raid of the tier, and it holds Forever's first legendary weapon. The zone around it, [Mount Hyjal](/world/616), is the endgame leveling zone, so you will know the terrain before you step inside.

**Entrance:** at the summit of Mount Hyjal. No open source gives its map coordinates.

**Bosses:** Bandalar, Time-Lost Battalion, Old Gloomlurker, Kathris the Haunted, Elder Minderel, Council of Thorns, The Wild King, Ancient of Decay, Sylvestris Dusksong, Gharalis the Abyssal, Anara Chillwind and Tracker Stillwind. The thirteenth, the last of the raid, is Nythus, whose title the three sources spell three ways.

## Onyxia’s Lair

Deep in Dustwallow Marsh, the broodmother returns as the forty-player raid of the first tier, and she drops additional tier pieces. Her fight has three phases and a lot of fire, so collect some fire resistance before your first night. Every raider needs the **Drakefire Amulet** to get in; it only has to be in your bags. Her [page](/dungeons/onyxias-lair) has her abilities and loot as Classic had them (patch 1.12, from VMaNGOS); Forever may have changed them.

**Entrance:** Dustwallow Marsh, 52.6, 76.8, as QuestieDB places it.

## The Drakefire Amulet

Each player earns the amulet through a questline for their faction. Both end in [Blackrock Spire](/dungeons/blackrock-spire), where you take the Blood of the Black Dragon Champion from General Drakkisath. Start early, bring friends for the elite quests and plan several trips into Blackrock Mountain.

**Alliance**

1. In the Burning Steppes, take **Dragonkin Menace** from Helendis Riverhorn, then the six quests named **The True Masters**.
2. They lead to Marshal Maxwell at Morgan's Vigil, who sends you into [Blackrock Depths](/dungeons/blackrock-depths) to find Marshal Windsor (**Marshal Windsor**, then **Abandoned Hope**).
3. In Blackrock Depths, loot **A Crumpled Up Note** and bring it to Windsor in his cell, recover his notes from Golem Lord Argelmach and General Angerforge (**A Shred of Hope**), then break him out (**Jail Break!**).
4. Meet Squire Rowe at Stormwind's gates to call Windsor (**Stormwind Rendezvous**) and play your part in **The Great Masquerade**.
5. Highlord Bolvar Fordragon sends you on **The Dragon's Eye**, and **Drakefire Amulet** takes you to General Drakkisath in Upper Blackrock Spire.

**Horde**

1. In Kargath, in the Badlands, take **Blood and Thunder** from Warlord Goretooth, then **Warlord's Command** in Lower Blackrock Spire.
2. Goretooth sends you to Eitrigg and Thrall in Orgrimmar, and **For the Horde!** sends you back for Warchief Rend Blackhand's head.
3. **What the Wind Carries** and **The Champion of the Horde** lead to Rexxar on the roads of Desolace, who sends **The Testament of Rexxar** to Myranda the Hag in the Western Plaguelands.
4. Myranda's **Oculus Illusions** needs Black Dragonspawn Eyes. With the Amulet of Draconic Subversion, find the drake **Emberstrife** in Dustwallow Marsh.
5. Bring back the skulls of Scryer, Somnus, Chronalis and Axtroz in four quests named **The Test of Skulls**. Emberstrife then gives you the Dull Drakefire Amulet (**Ascension...**) to take to Rexxar.
6. **Blood of the Black Dragon Champion** sends you to General Drakkisath in Upper Blackrock Spire, and Rexxar gives you the Drakefire Amulet.

## Before December 9 (as of November 14)

The raids open in 25 days.

1. **Reach 60 by questing.** Since November 13 a kill in a dungeon gives no experience to anyone in the party when the enemy is trivial to one member, so a high-level friend can no longer pull you through. The [leveling guide](/guides/leveling) has the route.
2. **Start the Drakefire Amulet** if your guild will raid Onyxia: every raider needs one, and its questline runs through Blackrock Depths and Upper Blackrock Spire.
3. **Trade your Blessed Band of Light.** Since November 13 it has no hit. Until the raids open, Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay trades it for a Channeler's Ring, which gives spell power. The trade cannot be undone.
4. **Run Pix Xizzix's weekly dungeon quests** at 60. Since November 13 they leave your quest log at the weekly reset.
5. **Set up a raid build.** Every class has raid builds on the [builds page](/builds), each with what it was checked against. Secondary Talents, a second set of talents bought at level 40 for 50 gold, keeps your leveling or dungeon build beside it.
6. **Get your hit up.** Against a raid boss, spells stop missing at 17% hit and special attacks at 8%. Tanks stop taking crits at 440 Defense. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has every cap.
7. **Collect fire resistance** for Onyxia.

## What comes after

Blizzard's roadmap for 2027:

- **Spring 2027:** a new ten-player raid, a new twenty-player raid, new dungeons and new quests.
- **Summer 2027:** a revamped version of an iconic raid and another new raid.

The level cap stays at 60, so each new tier adds gear and challenges instead of resetting your character.

## Building the raid

Forever changes raid composition in two big ways.

**Debuffs are personal.** Shadow Weaving, Improved Scorch, Winter's Chill and Improved Shadow Bolt now buff only the player who applies them. Stacking Shadow priests to buff warlocks, or a wall of Fire mages sharing Scorch stacks, no longer pays off.

**Smaller raids need every role covered.** The [raid planner](/tools/raid) checks which buffs and debuffs your roster brings, such as Blessing of Kings, Trueshot Aura, Leader of the Pack, Moonkin Form, Power Infusion, Mana Tide Totem and Curse of the Elements, and shows what is missing.

New group tools help as well: Light’s Vigil and Wild Growth heal whole parties, Prayer of Mending bounces through the raid, and every paladin can bless Kings.
