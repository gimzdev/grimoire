The Forever druid fills every role and finally has the tools for each. Balance gets Eclipse and a Moonkin Form worth standing in, Feral splits into a cat with real burst and a bear with high-threat area tanking, and Restoration gets Wild Growth. Every druid can now resurrect out of combat with Revive, and both Skyborne factions can be druids, with forms of their own.

## What changed from Classic

- **Revive** is an out-of-combat resurrection from level 12, so a druid no longer waits for Rebirth to bring back a party member between pulls.
- **Omen of Clarity and Nature's Grasp are trainer spells,** free for every druid.
- **Naturalist** in Restoration now adds 5% to all damage you deal and takes half a second off Healing Touch. All four builds take it.
- **Eclipse** links Balance's two nukes: each Wrath takes half a second off your next two Starfires, storing up to four charges.
- **Moonkin Form** doubles Omen of Clarity's proc chance and gives your party 3% crit.
- **Feral gets new buttons.** [[talent:druid:Shifting Power]] converts mana into 40 Energy, [[talent:druid:Primal Bite]] is a high-threat bite, and Berserk ties them together.
- **Furor remembers your Energy.** Shifting back into Cat Form restores the Energy you had when you left, plus 10 per second you spent out of form, up to 100.
- **[[talent:druid:Thick Hide]]** now adds base armor per level and per point of defense in your forms instead of a flat percentage.
- **Wild Growth** caps Restoration: a front-loaded heal over time on the target's whole party.
- **Removed:** Natural Weapons, Feline Swiftness, Feral Aggression, Improved Shred, Primal Fury, Improved Enrage, Faerie Fire (Feral), Improved Healing Touch, Improved Mark of the Wild, Improved Thorns and Improved Nature's Grasp.

## Balance

[[build:druid-balance-raid]]

Balance alternates Wrath and Starfire and keeps two damage-over-time effects up. [[talent:druid:Moonkin Form]] arrives at 45.

**Single target:**

1. Moonkin Form for the whole fight.
2. Moonfire and Insect Swarm, both longer with [[talent:druid:Nature's Splendor]].
3. Wrath to bank [[talent:druid:Eclipse]] charges. Each Wrath shortens your next two Starfires.
4. Starfire while you have charges.
5. Spend Clearcasting procs on Starfire, the most expensive spell you cast.

[[talent:druid:Vengeance]] doubles the critical damage bonus of Arcane and Nature spells, [[talent:druid:Moonfury]] adds 10% to both schools, and [[talent:druid:Nature's Grace]] speeds up your casting after every crit. [[talent:druid:Nature's Reach]] adds 4% hit. The Restoration points are [[talent:druid:Naturalist]] for 5% damage, [[talent:druid:Reflection]] for mana, and a few ranks of Nature's Focus, Furor and Gift of Nature.

## Feral cat

[[build:druid-feral-raid]]

The cat is a bleed spec with a big cooldown. [[talent:druid:Berserk]] arrives at 44.

**Single target:**

1. Keep Rake and Rip on the target. [[talent:druid:Rend and Tear]] adds 10% to all your melee damage on a bleeding target.
2. Build combo points with Shred from behind, 18 Energy cheaper with [[talent:druid:Shredding Attacks]], or Claw when you cannot get behind.
3. Spend extra combo points on Ferocious Bite.
4. Shifting Power when you run dry: 40 Energy for a little mana.
5. Berserk with Tiger's Fury. For 15 seconds your combo point builders gain 100% crit, and [[talent:druid:Blood Frenzy]] adds an extra combo point on each of those crits.

[[talent:druid:Predatory Instincts]] adds 20% critical damage to your melee abilities, and [[talent:druid:Leader of the Pack]] gives your party 3% crit. From Restoration the build takes Furor, Naturalist, [[talent:druid:Natural Shapeshifter]] to make shifting and Shifting Power cheaper, and three points of Gift of Nature.

## Feral bear

[[build:druid-feral-bear]]

The bear tanks with threat to spare and stays alive through dodge and armor.

**Threat:**

1. Feral Charge in, Faerie Fire, then Demoralizing Roar on packs.
2. Primal Bite on cooldown for single-target threat.
3. Lacerate to stack a bleed that also generates high threat.
4. Swipe for packs, 30% stronger with [[talent:druid:Feral Instinct]].
5. Maul with spare Rage.
6. Berserk on big pulls: Primal Bite hits three targets with no cooldown for 15 seconds.

[[talent:druid:Natural Reaction]] adds 5% dodge and 5 Rage every time you dodge, and Blood Frenzy adds 5 Rage on each crit. [[talent:druid:Heart of the Wild]] gives 20% Stamina in bear form. Frenzied Regeneration and Barkskin are your cooldowns.

## Restoration

[[build:druid-restoration-heal]]

Restoration heals with heal-over-time effects and has the best party healing of any healer through Wild Growth. [[talent:druid:Swiftmend]] arrives at 32 and [[talent:druid:Wild Growth]] at 45.

**How it plays:**

1. Rejuvenation on the tank and anyone taking steady damage.
2. Regrowth on targets that need a direct heal. [[talent:druid:Improved Regrowth]] adds 50% crit to its initial heal.
3. Wild Growth when a whole party takes damage.
4. Swiftmend on a target with Rejuvenation or Regrowth for an instant heal.
5. Nature's Swiftness into Healing Touch for emergencies.

[[talent:druid:Gift of the Earthmother]] shortens the global cooldown on Rejuvenation, Swiftmend and Wild Growth by half a second, which speeds up your whole rotation. [[talent:druid:Genesis]] from Balance adds 5% to all your periodic healing, and [[talent:druid:Living Spirit]] adds 15% Spirit.

## Stat priorities

Forever converts 10 Hit Rating to 1% hit, 14 Critical Strike Rating to 1% crit and 12 Dodge Rating to 1% dodge. Blizzard has not published raid hit caps for Forever yet.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Balance | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect, Spirit |
| Feral cat | Hit, Expertise, Agility, Strength, Critical Strike, Attack Power |
| Feral bear | Stamina, Armor, Agility, Defense, Dodge, Hit and Expertise for threat |
| Restoration | Healing power, Spirit, Intellect, Critical Strike, Haste |

Weapon damage does not matter in your forms, so a feral weapon is just a stat stick. Compare sets in the [gear planner](/tools/gear?class=druid).

## Leveling

Level as a cat. The Feral order puts [[talent:druid:Feral Swiftness]] at 17 and 18, for 30% more movement speed in Cat Form, then Shifting Power at 27, Primal Bite at 28 and Berserk at 44.

- Bear Form at 10 for anything dangerous, Cat Form at 20 for speed.
- Omen of Clarity from 20 now procs from spells as well as attacks, so you get a free Shred, Maul or Wrath every so often.
- Revive at 12 brings your party back between pulls without a ritual.
- Heal yourself between fights in caster form, then shift back. Furor gives back the Energy you left with.
- With the Talented Legacy perk, Berserk comes at 39.

## Races

- **Night Elf:** [[racial:Shadowmeld]] and [[racial:Quickness]], which makes you harder to spot while Prowling and adds 1% dodge. [[racial:Elune's Light]] adds 10% crit for 15 seconds.
- **Tauren:** [[racial:Endurance]] adds 5% health and 1% hit, and War Stomp stuns up to five enemies. [[racial:Plainsrunning]] speeds you up the longer you keep moving.
- **High Order Skyborne:** new to the class, with forms made for the race, [[racial:Read Ley Line]] for regeneration and 1% haste.
- **Windshaper Skyborne:** new to the class, with forms made for the race, [[racial:Skysight]] for movement speed and 1% haste.
