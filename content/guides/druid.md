The Forever druid fills every role. Balance gets Eclipse and a cheap, fast Wrath, Feral splits into a cat that turns mana into Energy and a bear with more threat than ever, and Restoration ends in Wild Growth. Every druid can resurrect out of combat with Revive, and both Skyborne factions can be druids, with forms of their own. The builds here are worked out from the game's tooltips, with Blizzard's hotfixes up to November 13.

## What changed from Classic

- **Revive** (12) resurrects out of combat, so you no longer wait for Rebirth between pulls.
- **Omen of Clarity and Nature's Grasp are trainer spells.** Omen procs from spells too, and Nature's Grasp works in any form.
- **Forms** still free you from Polymorph, roots and snares, are now immune to Disarm and let you drink mana potions. Bear Form and Dire Bear Form add 115% threat (50% before 2 November, raised because Blessing of Salvation is gone), and crits in them give 100% more Rage.
- **Cat damage:** Shred deals 200% weapon damage, Claw 140% and Ravage 435%, and Rip, Rake, Ferocious Bite and Pounce gained about 25% base damage and Attack Power scaling. Ferocious Bite has a new first rank at 24 and Shred comes at 20. Tiger's Fury is gone.
- **Feral talents:** [[talent:druid:Lacerate]] is now a talent with two new low ranks. The last row has a capstone per form: [[talent:druid:Primal Bite]] for the bear, [[talent:druid:Shifting Power]] for the cat behind [[talent:druid:Predatory Discipline]], which lets Ferocious Bite keep the spare Energy it adds damage for. [[talent:druid:Furor]], now Feral, restores the Energy you left Cat Form with, and [[talent:druid:Thick Hide]] adds armor per level and per point of Defense.
- **Balance:** [[talent:druid:Eclipse]] links Wrath to Starfire, the beta raised Wrath's base damage by about 50% and Starfire's by about 40%, and [[talent:druid:Genesis]] adds up to 10% periodic damage and healing. [[talent:druid:Moonkin Form]] and Leader of the Pack each add 3% to every kind of crit for the raid, but they do not stack.
- **Restoration:** [[talent:druid:Wild Growth]] heals the target's whole party (3 sec cast), [[talent:druid:Swiftmend]] heals for the full duration of the heal over time it consumes but only takes your own, and [[talent:druid:Bloom]] heals for 100% of your Spirit when your Rejuvenation or Regrowth runs out. [[talent:druid:Naturalist]] adds up to 10% damage and speeds up Healing Touch and Wild Growth, Heart of the Wild is now a Restoration talent, and Rejuvenation, Tranquility and Wild Growth can crit.
- **Removed:** Natural Weapons, Feral Aggression, Improved Enrage, Faerie Fire (Feral), Improved Mark of the Wild, Improved Thorns and Improved Nature's Grasp; Berserk and Gift of the Earthmother left during the beta. Feline Swiftness, Improved Shred, Improved Healing Touch and Primal Fury live on as Feral Swiftness, Shredding Attacks, Naturalist and Blood Frenzy.

## Balance

[[build:druid-balance-raid]]

Balance weaves Wrath into Starfire under two damage-over-time effects, and mana decides how much of it is Starfire. [[talent:druid:Moonkin Form]] arrives at 40.

1. Moonkin Form for the whole fight.
2. Moonfire and Insect Swarm, 15 and 14 sec long with [[talent:druid:Nature's Splendor]].
3. Wrath to bank [[talent:druid:Eclipse]] charges: each takes 0.5 sec off your next two Starfires, up to four charges.
4. Starfire while you have charges: 2.5 sec with [[talent:druid:Improved Starfire]] and a charge. Clearcasting goes on Starfire too.
5. Low on mana: more Wraths, Innervate and a mana potion.

[[talent:druid:Improved Wrath]] makes this work: a 1.5 sec Wrath takes 1 sec off your next two Starfires, so it costs half a second of casting and adds its own damage. With spell damage gear Wrath also does far more damage per point of mana, so Improved Wrath, [[talent:druid:Moonglow]] and [[talent:druid:Reflection]] decide how long the Starfires last. [[talent:druid:Vengeance]] doubles your crit bonus, [[talent:druid:Moonfury]] and Naturalist add 10% each and [[talent:druid:Nature's Reach]] adds 4% hit. [[talent:druid:Heart of the Wild]] replaces Nature's Focus: you are rarely hit in a raid, and 10% Intellect is more mana.

## Feral cat

[[build:druid-feral-raid]]

The cat is a bleed spec that turns mana into Energy. [[talent:druid:Shifting Power]] arrives at 40.

1. Open from Prowl with Ravage; Poised takes 10 Energy off it.
2. Keep Rake and Rip up: [[talent:druid:Rend and Tear]] adds 15% to your melee abilities while the target bleeds, Pounce's bleed included since November 11, and Genesis 10% to the bleeds.
3. Shred from behind ([[talent:druid:Shredding Attacks]] takes 18 Energy off it), Claw when you cannot.
4. With five combo points and Rip ticking, Ferocious Bite at high Energy: Predatory Discipline adds damage for the spare Energy without spending it.
5. Shifting Power when you run low: 40 Energy, 8 sec cooldown, not usable at full Energy.

On cooldown, Shifting Power would add half again the Energy you regenerate, but each one costs 55% of your base mana, so mana runs out first. [[talent:druid:Natural Shapeshifter]] makes it 30% cheaper, about 43% more Shifting Powers from the same mana, and Heart of the Wild's Intellect and mana potions turn into Energy too. [[talent:druid:Natural Instinct]] adds 20% to your crit bonus, [[talent:druid:Blood Frenzy]] a combo point when a builder crits, and Furor keeps your Energy when you leave Cat Form to Innervate or resurrect. Faerie Fire is free in Cat Form.

## Feral bear

[[build:druid-feral-bear]]

With the 115% threat bonus, threat is rarely the bear's problem. [[talent:druid:Lacerate]] arrives at 25 and [[talent:druid:Primal Bite]] at 40.

1. Feral Charge in (it interrupts), then Faerie Fire, which is free and now generates threat.
2. Primal Bite on cooldown (6 sec).
3. Lacerate to five stacks and keep it up: it causes high threat, and while the target bleeds Rend and Tear adds 15% to your other attacks.
4. Maul with spare Rage, 10 with Ferocity.
5. Packs: Demoralizing Roar, Swipe (three targets, 30% harder with [[talent:druid:Feral Instinct]]), and Lacerate or Primal Bite on the targets that matter.

[[talent:druid:Feral Swiftness]] (now 4% dodge in any form) and [[talent:druid:Natural Reaction]] (5% dodge) add about 9% dodge, and every dodge gives 5 Rage. [[talent:druid:Thick Hide]] adds 3 base armor per level, about 180 at 60, plus 2.00 for every point of defense skill beyond five times your level, and Dire Bear Form multiplies it. Heart of the Wild adds 20% Stamina in Bear Form. [[ability:druid:Frenzied Regeneration]] turns up to 10 Rage a second into 1% of your health per point for 10 sec: your emergency button.

## Restoration

[[build:druid-restoration-heal]]

Restoration is a really strong PvE pick: every raid size needs healers, and Wild Growth, Swiftmend and Bloom give it a complete kit. [[talent:druid:Swiftmend]] arrives at 29 and [[talent:druid:Wild Growth]] at 40.

1. Rejuvenation on the tank and anyone taking steady damage.
2. Wild Growth when a party is hurt: 2.5 sec cast with Naturalist. Since November 13 you cannot cast it on the move, and it gains 6.6% of your healing power, twice what it did; the client of that day also shares one cooldown across its three ranks, which Blizzard has not posted.
3. Regrowth for direct healing, 50% more crit with [[talent:druid:Improved Regrowth]].
4. Swiftmend on one of your own Rejuvenations for an instant heal worth its whole duration.
5. Let the rest run out: [[talent:druid:Bloom]] heals for 100% of your Spirit when they do.
6. Nature's Swiftness into Healing Touch for emergencies, or into Wild Growth, which it makes instant since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 10; Tranquility, a 2 min cooldown with [[talent:druid:Improved Tranquility]], for party-wide damage.

[[talent:druid:Nature's Splendor]] stretches Rejuvenation to 15 sec and Regrowth to 27 for the same mana, so Swiftmend consumes 15 sec of Rejuvenation. It costs ten Balance points: Genesis (10% to every heal over time) and [[talent:druid:Nature's Majesty]] (4% crit) earn theirs, three in Moonglow are the price. [[talent:druid:Living Spirit]] adds 15% Spirit for Bloom and regeneration. The build skips [[talent:druid:Tranquil Spirit]], because Healing Touch, the main spell it discounts, is your least efficient heal.

## PvP

Bear and Cat Form each cost 55% of your base mana, so mana decides how often you can shift out of roots and snares.

### Feral

[[build:druid-feral-combat-pvp]]

Feral is a really strong PvP pick because it has a tool for every job: [[talent:druid:Feral Charge]] interrupts and roots, [[talent:druid:Brutal Impact]] makes Bash a 5 sec stun every 30 sec and Pounce a 3 sec one, Shifting Power gives it burst, and Bear Form with Frenzied Regeneration keeps it alive. It can duel, peel for a healer and carry a flag.

1. Open from Prowl with Pounce, or Ravage for damage. Shred while they are stunned, Claw when they face you.
2. Rake and Rip, then Ferocious Bite at high Energy for the kill.
3. Bear, Bash, cat: Furor gives you the 10 Rage Bash costs as you enter Bear Form, and your Energy back when you return.
4. Shift out of every root and snare; Natural Shapeshifter makes each shift 30% cheaper.
5. Focused by melee: Bear Form, Frenzied Regeneration, and Primal Bite to hit back.

To carry flags, move Rend and Tear's five points to Natural Reaction.

### Restoration

[[build:druid-restoration-pvp]]

Restoration heals on the move: Rejuvenation and Swiftmend are instant, Nature's Swiftness makes a Healing Touch instant, and Bloom still heals for half your Spirit when an enemy dispels your heals. Nature's Focus protects your casts, and Furor's 10 Rage gives you a Bash the moment you turn bear, a 5 sec stun every 30 sec with [[talent:druid:Brutal Impact]]. It struggles when silenced or drained of mana, and Wild Growth's 2.5 sec cast is easy to interrupt unless Nature's Swiftness makes it instant.

### Balance

[[build:druid-balance-pvp]]

Balance is strong at range: Moonkin Form multiplies your item armor and blocks Polymorph, [[talent:druid:Improved Entangling Roots]] lets a rooted target take 75% more damage before breaking free, [[talent:druid:Overgrowth]] keeps three roots up at once, and Improved Starfire has a 15% chance to stun. It struggles against interrupts and in long fights, and you must leave Moonkin Form to heal.

### Cooldowns and threats

[[ability:druid:Barkskin]] (1 min) cuts Physical damage by 20% and stops pushback. Nature's Swiftness and Frenzied Regeneration return every 3 min, [[ability:druid:Innervate]] every 6, and Nature's Grasp roots the next melee attacker.

- **Rogues and druids:** Faerie Fire keeps them out of stealth and [[ability:druid:Abolish Poison]] clears poisons.
- **Warriors:** shift out of Hamstring, then root and kite.
- **Mages:** forms block Polymorph and shifting breaks Frost Nova; Feral Charge and Bash interrupt.
- **Hunters:** close in with Feral Charge or Dash, and [[ability:druid:Hibernate]] the pet.
- **Warlocks and priests:** [[ability:druid:Remove Curse]] clears curses and Banes. You cannot break Fear, so stay out of range.
- **Shamans:** kill Earthbind Totem instead of shifting out of it again and again.

## Stat priorities

Balance spells stop missing a raid boss at 17% hit and players at 4%. [[talent:druid:Nature's Reach]] covers 4%. A cat's attacks stop missing a boss at 8% and players at 5%, with no talent to help, and Expertise, which lowers dodge and parry, removes a boss's dodges at 6.5% from behind. Bears stop taking crits from raid bosses at 440 Defense, and Thick Hide turns every point of Defense above 300 into armor. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Balance | Hit, spell damage, Critical Strike, Intellect, Spirit |
| Feral cat | Hit, Expertise, Agility, Strength, Critical Strike, Attack Power, Intellect |
| Feral bear | Armor, Stamina, Agility, Defense, Hit and Expertise for threat |
| Restoration | Healing power, Intellect, Spirit, mana per 5 sec, Critical Strike |

Agility counts as Attack Power in Cat Form, and Intellect matters to cats because it pays for Shifting Power. Bloom makes Spirit worth more to Restoration than in Classic. Weapon damage does not matter in your forms, so a feral weapon is just a stat stick.

## Leveling

Level as a cat: you spend no mana while you fight, and your caster heals cover the downtime.

[[build:druid-feral-combat-leveling]]

- Bear Form carries you from 10 to 20: Maul, Swipe, Demoralizing Roar and Enrage. Ferocity (10-14) makes Maul cost 10 Rage.
- Cat Form at 20, with Feral Charge to leap behind the mob. Claw from the front, Shred from behind, Rake and Rip, then Ferocious Bite.
- From 32, Prowl into Ravage, 10 Energy cheaper with Poised. Shifting Power at 40 turns the mana you do not use in Cat Form into Energy.
- Heal in caster form between pulls and shift back: Furor returns your Energy.
- Five ranks of the Talented Legacy perk move every point five levels earlier, so Shifting Power comes at 35.
- Restoration also levels as a cat, since Heart of the Wild and Naturalist work in forms, and gets Wild Growth at 40 for dungeons. Balance is slower: you rest more between pulls.

## Races

Night Elf and High Order Skyborne are Alliance, Tauren and Windshaper Skyborne Horde. Both Skyborne have [[racial:Wind Blessed]] (2% haste) and [[racial:Elemental Insight]] (5% more damage against Elementals).

**PvE**

- **Balance:** High Order Skyborne, Windshaper Skyborne, Night Elf, Tauren. Both Skyborne have 2% haste on every cast, and the High Order add mana from [[racial:Read Leyline]]; that beats [[racial:Elune's Light]] (10% crit for 15 sec every 3 min, under 1% on average). Tauren adds no damage.
- **Feral cat:** Skyborne, Night Elf, Tauren. Haste speeds up every white swing, Elune's Light is close behind, and [[racial:Endurance]] only adds health.
- **Feral bear:** Tauren, Night Elf, Skyborne. Endurance's 5% health is the best tank racial, [[racial:Quickness]] adds 1% dodge, haste a little threat.
- **Restoration:** High Order Skyborne, Windshaper Skyborne, Night Elf, Tauren. Haste on every cast, plus Read Leyline's regeneration for the High Order; [[racial:Shadowmeld]] in combat discourages enemies from attacking you; Tauren's 5% health helps you live through boss abilities.

**PvP**

- **Feral:** Night Elf, Tauren, Windshaper Skyborne, High Order Skyborne. Elune's Light makes the hardest opener and Quickness hides your Prowl as if you were a level higher; [[racial:War Stomp]] stuns up to five enemies; [[racial:Skysight]] speeds up flag runs.
- **Balance:** Tauren, Night Elf, Windshaper Skyborne, High Order Skyborne. War Stomp buys time to root and step away, Elune's Light crits your Starfires, and Skysight helps you kite.
- **Restoration:** Tauren, Night Elf, Windshaper Skyborne, High Order Skyborne. War Stomp and 5% health keep you alive under focus, Shadowmeld hides you to drink, and Skysight keeps you with your flag carrier.
