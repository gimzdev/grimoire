Each row of a path is the orange recipe with the fewest reagents, made until it turns yellow. Past 225 most of what you make comes from patterns, and each profession's pattern tables list every one with where it comes from. Leatherworkers: take Skinning and skin what you kill, because leather and hides are the main reagents of every stretch.

Recipes, skill levels, reagents, trainers, vendors and drop chances on this page are Classic’s (patch 1.12, from VMaNGOS): Forever may have changed them. Forever's launch client holds 2,205 profession recipes, 958 of them new and 834 changed from Classic Era, and most are taught by recipe items: 436 of Leatherworking's 512 and 358 of Blacksmithing's 441. The changes Blizzard and that client show are applied below; check a recipe's color in your profession window before you craft a stack. [Professions in Forever](/guides/professions) has the rest of what changed.

The paths use recipes a trainer teaches, that you start with, or whose pattern a vendor sells without a reputation; none has a cooldown or needs a specialization. Color is what the recipe shows for the whole stretch: orange, or yellow where nothing is orange. In the pattern tables a specialization in brackets means only crafters who took it can learn the recipe, and "and N more" counts the other creatures and containers that drop the pattern.

## Leatherworking

Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 6, the Tanning Rack, Leatherworking's camp craft, needs 125 skill, not 140, and the skill-ups it gives are 15 points lower to match. Dragonscale, Elemental and Tribal are taken from level 40 through a quest from each one's master, and the choice is permanent; [Professions in Forever](/guides/professions#leatherworking-s-three-specializations) has the masters and their quests.

## Skinning

In Classic, skinning a beast needs (its level − 10) × 10 skill while your Skinning is under 100, and its level × 5 from 100, so skin the band whose top level your skill reaches. Each band lists that skill, what its beasts give most often and the open-world zones with the most skinnable beasts of those levels, with how many there are. A creature can be skinned once, as Blizzard's development notes restored. Skinning's camp crafts are the Camp Chair at 20, the Field Guide at 140 and the Trapper's Workbench at 300.
