Warlocks gained seventeen talents in Forever and lost fifteen. Affliction channels Wrack, Demonology can sacrifice one demon and fight with another, and Destruction trades Shadow and Fire bonuses with Conflagrate, Shadowburn and Incinerate. Trolls can be warlocks for the first time. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips, with Blizzard's hotfixes up to November 13, which reworked Wrack and gave the demons more health.

## What changed from Classic

- **Banes.** Curse of Agony and Curse of Doom are now Bane of Agony and Bane of Doom; Bane of Doom cannot be cast on players. A target can carry one Bane and one Curse from you. Since 2 November Bane of Agony updates its damage dynamically, and [[talent:warlock:Amplify Curse]] works on it and on Curse of Exhaustion.
- **[[ability:warlock:Curse of the Elements]]** covers every school: at rank 4 the target takes 10% more Magic damage. Curse of Shadow is gone.
- **Affliction's DoTs crit.** [[talent:warlock:Pandemic]] doubles the critical damage bonus of Corruption, Bane of Agony, Bane of Doom, the drains, Siphon Life and Wrack, and [[talent:warlock:Malevolence]] adds 5% Shadow crit.
- **[[talent:warlock:Wrack]]** caps Affliction: a 6-second channel that deals 60 Shadow damage a second at 60, and while it lasts your Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony on the target deal their damage twice as fast. Blizzard's notes of 2 November more than doubled its damage (it was 36 a second) and stopped Improved Drains raising it; its hotfixes of 10 November cut the damage by 30% and swapped its 10% bonus to your DoTs for the faster ticks. [[talent:warlock:Soul Siphon]] now counts only those three DoTs.
- **[[talent:warlock:Demonic Sacrifice]] lasts 2 hours and flipped:** the Imp gives 15% Shadow damage, the Succubus or Incubus 15% Fire damage, the Voidwalker 2% of your mana and the Felhunter 3% of your Health every 4 sec. [[talent:warlock:Demonic Pact]] stops a different demon from cancelling it.
- **[[talent:warlock:Shadow and Flame]]** gives 20% Shadow damage for 20 seconds after Conflagrate and 20% Fire damage after Shadowburn (10% before 2 November), stops Conflagrate from using up Immolate and makes Shadowburn refund its Soul Shard.
- **[[talent:warlock:Suppression]]** adds 5% hit to every spell and cuts threat by 20%. [[talent:warlock:Improved Shadow Bolt]] raises only your own Shadow damage on the target.
- **Pets lost Health on 2 November.** They gain 2 Health per point of your Stamina, down from 7.5, so your demon dies sooner, whether it tanks or soaks Soul Link's 30%. Blizzard's hotfixes gave some back: the Voidwalker about 40 base health on 6 November, and the Imp, Voidwalker, Succubus and Incubus about 15% more base health on 10 November, with harder-hitting first ranks of the Imp's Firebolt.
- **Fewer Soul Shards.** Since 2 November Drain Soul rarely gives a shard from damage alone; a kill still always does.
- **Spellstones** add only Shadow damage since 2 November, and **wands** no longer gain from spell damage and need you to stand still for half a second.
- **Smaller changes:** Hellfire can crit, the Voidwalker's Sacrifice shield grows with 10% of your healing power, your demon is stunned while you summon another, and casting a spell stops Drain Soul.
- **Renamed:** Fel Intellect is now [[talent:warlock:Fel Vitality]], with Fel Stamina folded in, Improved Searing Pain is Agonizing Flames, Improved Curse of Agony is Improved Bane of Agony, Improved Drain Life is Soul Siphon and Improved Succubus is Improved Sayaad.
- **Removed:** Dark Pact, Devastation, Emberstorm, Improved Immolate, Improved Subjugate Demon, Improved Healthstone, Improved Firebolt, Improved Lash of Pain and the old curse, drain and stone talents.

## Affliction

[[build:warlock-affliction-raid]]

Affliction is a really strong PvE pick. It needs the fewest Life Taps of the three, has no demon to keep alive once the Imp is sacrificed, and Suppression cuts its threat. [[talent:warlock:Siphon Life]] arrives at 30 and Wrack at 40.

**Single target:**

1. Your raid's curse, usually Curse of the Elements.
2. Corruption, instant with [[talent:warlock:Improved Corruption]].
3. Bane of Agony, with Amplify Curse whenever it is ready.
4. Siphon Life.
5. Channel Wrack until a DoT needs refreshing.
6. Shadow Bolt only when [[talent:warlock:Nightfall]] makes it instant.

**Why Wrack:** on its own it deals 60 damage a second at 60 plus part of your spell power, less than a Shadow Bolt's 89 a second, but for its 6 seconds your Corruption, Bane of Agony and Siphon Life deal their damage twice as fast, and it gains 36% from [[talent:warlock:Soul Siphon]] with all three on the target, doubles its crit bonus with Pandemic and costs 200 mana. The DoTs run out sooner while you channel, so refresh them as they drop. [[talent:warlock:Shadow Mastery]] adds 10% to all of it.

Bane of Doom deals 1,742 damage after a minute; two and a half Agonies deal about 1,520 base damage with [[talent:warlock:Improved Bane of Agony]]. On a boss that lives well past a minute, Doom is about as good for fewer casts.

## Demonology

[[build:warlock-demonology-raid]]

Demonology keeps a sacrifice and a demon at once. [[talent:warlock:Demonic Brand]] arrives at 25, [[talent:warlock:Soul Link]] at 30 and Demonic Pact at 40.

**Before the pull:**

1. Sacrifice the Imp: 15% more Shadow damage for 2 hours.
2. Summon the Succubus or Incubus. Demonic Pact keeps the buff, and [[talent:warlock:Master Demonologist]] gives you both 10% more Shadow damage.
3. Soul Link: the demon takes 30% of your damage, and you both deal 3% more.

**Single target:**

1. Corruption and a Bane.
2. Searing Pain every 10 seconds. With Demonic Brand your demon's next six attacks each deal 65 to 68 extra Shadow damage plus 7.8% of your spell power, more than the Shadow Bolt the cast replaces. Those hits carry high threat.
3. Shadow Bolt as the filler. Its crits make the target take 20% more of your Shadow damage for 12 seconds.
4. Below 35% health, [[talent:warlock:Decimation]] makes Shadow Bolt deal 6% more and Soul Fire cast 40% faster at no Soul Shard cost.

Everything but the sacrifice stops while the demon is dead, and pets have less Health now, even with the 15% Blizzard gave back on November 10: [[talent:warlock:Demonic Energies]] heals it for 15% of your spell damage, and [[talent:warlock:Master Summoner]] takes 4 seconds off the summon. [[talent:warlock:Demonic Knowledge]] adds spell damage equal to 100% of your level while it is out.

## Destruction

[[build:warlock-destruction-raid]]

Destruction plays Fire against Shadow. [[talent:warlock:Conflagrate]] arrives at 25, Shadow and Flame at 35 to 39 and [[talent:warlock:Incinerate]] at 40.

**Single target:**

1. Immolate, and keep it up.
2. Conflagrate on cooldown: it keeps your Immolate and gives you 20% Shadow damage for 20 seconds.
3. Shadowburn at least every 20 seconds for 20% Fire damage. It refunds its Soul Shard.
4. Corruption and a Bane, which Conflagrate's bonus raises too.
5. Incinerate as the filler.
6. With a second target nearby, put [[talent:warlock:Bane of Havoc]] on it: it takes 15% of the damage you deal to your main target. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 10 casting it costs a global cooldown.

Shadow and Flame decides the build: both bonuses last 20 seconds while Conflagrate comes back every 10 and Shadowburn every 15, so both stay up. Incinerate casts in 2 seconds with [[talent:warlock:Bane]] and deals 25% more with your Immolate on the target, 25 to 30% more per second of casting than Shadow Bolt, so the build skips Improved Shadow Bolt and sacrifices the Succubus for 15% Fire damage. [[talent:warlock:Ruin]] doubles the crit bonus of Destruction spells, [[talent:warlock:Agonizing Flames]] adds 10% to their damage and [[talent:warlock:Fire and Brimstone]] 25% crit to Conflagrate. Expect to Life Tap often: it spends the most mana of the three.

## Stat priorities

Spells stop missing a raid boss at 17% hit and players at 4%. Suppression gives 5% to every spell: the Affliction and Destruction raid builds take all five ranks, Demonology two, and every PvP build four. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Affliction | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect, Stamina |
| Demonology | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Stamina, Intellect, Haste |
| Destruction | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Intellect, Haste |

Pandemic doubles what a crit adds to Affliction's DoTs and Wrack, and Ruin to Destruction spells. Stamina feeds Life Tap and Soul Link; Spirit raises what Life Tap returns. Compare gear in the [item database](/items?class=warlock).

## Leveling

Level as Affliction. It leans on your demon the least, which matters now that pets gain only 2 Health per point of your Stamina.

[[build:warlock-affliction-leveling]]

- Pull with Bane of Agony and Corruption, let the Voidwalker hold the mob and wand it down. Fear a second mob and drain it yourself.
- Drain Life heals you and Life Tap turns that Health into mana, so you rarely drink. With Soul Siphon (31-33), Drain Life heals up to 36% more.
- From 40, channel Wrack between DoT refreshes: unlike a wand, it gains from your spell power, and your DoTs tick twice as fast while it lasts.
- Finish a mob with Drain Soul when you need a Soul Shard. Howl of Terror (40) and Death Coil (42) get you out of a bad pull.
- Demonology also levels well once Demonic Brand arrives at 25, but nearly everything goes through the demon. Its build and Destruction's are in the [talent calculator](/talents/warlock).
- With all five ranks of the Talented Legacy perk, Wrack comes at 35.

## PvP

Destruction is a really strong PvP pick, with the most burst: Shadow and Flame adds 20% to both schools since 2 November, crits have dealt full damage in PvP since 8 October, and the sacrificed Succubus leaves no demon to kill now that pets have less Health.

[[build:warlock-destruction-pvp]]

Open with Immolate, then Conflagrate (it dazes and keeps your Immolate), Shadowburn, Incinerate and Death Coil. It wins short fights; it has no Spell Lock, and an interrupt or stun mid-burst costs it the most.

[[build:warlock-affliction-pvp]]

Affliction wears a target down with DoTs and Curse of Exhaustion, then Wrack, or Drain Life for health. It holds up in long fights, keeps melee at range and has a Felhunter for Spell Lock and Devour Magic. Classes that dispel DoTs or interrupt channels slow it down.

[[build:warlock-demonology-pvp]]

Demonology is the hardest to kill while its demon lives: Soul Link and Master Demonologist share and cut your damage, and Demonic Pact lets you sacrifice the Imp against casters or the Felhunter against melee. Its demon is what enemies go for.

**Curses and Banes:**

- Curse of Exhaustion slows by 30%, more after Amplify Curse. Curse of Tongues makes a caster's spells take 60% longer, and Curse of Weakness takes 37 off each melee hit.
- Never use Curse of Recklessness on a target you want to Fear or Death Coil: it makes them ignore both.
- Bane of Agony is your damage Bane against players, since Bane of Doom cannot be cast on them. Bane of Havoc on a healer makes them take 15% of the damage you deal to others.
- Orcs' [[racial:Shatter Curse]] removes your Curses and Banes, so reapply them.

**Key cooldowns:** Death Coil (2 min) horrifies for 3 seconds and heals you for its 460 damage. Howl of Terror (40 sec) sends up to 5 enemies within 10 yards running for 15 seconds. Shadow Ward (30 sec) absorbs 920 Shadow damage. Spell Lock silences for 3 seconds and locks the interrupted school for 8.

**Against the common threats:**

- **Rogues** open with a stun: Human Will to Survive breaks it, Orc Hardiness shortens it. Then Death Coil, Fear and DoTs; the Felhunter's Paranoia helps you spot them.
- **Warriors:** slow them with Curse of Exhaustion or Conflagrate's daze, and Fear when Berserker Rage is on cooldown.
- **Mages:** Curse of Tongues, Spell Lock their Polymorph, and let Devour Magic take one off you.
- **Priests:** Shadow Ward against Shadow priests, Will of the Forsaken against Psychic Scream, and Spell Lock on their heals.
- **Warlocks:** Spell Lock their Fear to lock their Shadow spells for 8 seconds, and keep Shadow Ward up.

## Races

In PvE racials are worth about 1% of your damage or less, except Orc for Demonology; in PvP the escapes matter more.

| Spec | PvE | PvP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affliction | Human, Orc | Undead, Orc |
| Demonology | Orc | Orc, Undead |
| Destruction | Human, Orc, Gnome | Undead, Orc, Gnome |

**PvE:**

1. **Orc:** [[racial:Blood Fury]] adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, and [[racial:Command]] 5% to your demon's damage, which only Demonology keeps out.
2. **Human:** [[racial:Ruthless]] adds 1% crit at all times, and Pandemic and Ruin double what a crit adds.
3. **Gnome:** [[racial:Eureka!]] adds 10% to direct spells and channels such as Wrack for 8 seconds, and [[racial:Expansive Mind]] 5% mana, which Destruction runs short of first.
4. **Troll:** [[racial:Berserking]] adds 10% casting speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes.
5. **Undead:** nothing that adds damage.

**PvP:**

1. **Undead:** [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks Fear, Seduction and sleep, and [[racial:Touch of the Grave]] drains Health back.
2. **Orc:** [[racial:Hardiness]] shortens stuns by 20%, Shatter Curse clears Curses and Banes, and Blood Fury adds to a burst.
3. **Human:** [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks stuns and [[racial:Perception]] reveals stealthed rogues and druids.
4. **Gnome:** [[racial:Escape Artist]] breaks roots and slows so you keep your range, and Eureka! adds 10% to a Destruction burst.
5. **Troll:** Berserking speeds up a burst, and [[racial:Rapid Regeneration]] restores half your Health between fights.
