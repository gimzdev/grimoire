The Forever rogue keeps Energy, combo points, poisons and stealth, but on November 2 Blizzard rebuilt all three trees around two new trainer spells, Shank and Poised. Combat is a really strong pick for leveling and PvE, and Subtlety for PvP. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips since the server hotfixes of November 3, which put the reworked trees in the game.

## What changed from Classic

- **Shank** strikes with your off-hand for 50% weapon damage, costs 5 Energy, generates 15 (25 at rank 2), cannot miss and has a 10 sec cooldown. [[talent:rogue:Venom]], [[talent:rogue:Flash of Steel]] and [[talent:rogue:Boot Knife]] build on it.
- **Poised:** out of combat at full Energy, your next Energy ability costs 10 less, and 10 less again with [[talent:rogue:Composure]].
- **One weapon talent.** [[talent:rogue:Hack and Slash]] replaces the four weapon specializations: daggers and fist weapons gain 5% crit, swords and axes a 5% chance of an extra attack, maces ignore 15% of armor. One-handed axes are new to rogues.
- **Assassination:** [[talent:rogue:Mutilate]] is the 31-point talent: 90% weapon damage with each weapon plus a flat bonus, 20% more against poisoned targets, two combo points. [[talent:rogue:Seal Fate]] (three ranks) and [[talent:rogue:Relentless Strikes]] (five ranks) feed it, Venom makes each Shank add 30% poison damage and 20% poison application for 6 sec, and [[talent:rogue:Blood Splatter]] adds 6 sec to Rupture and 9 to Garrote. [[talent:rogue:Improved Expose Armor]] now cuts its cost and refunds combo points.
- **Combat:** [[talent:rogue:Coup de Grace]], [[talent:rogue:Aggression]] and [[talent:rogue:Flawless Execution]] make Eviscerate stronger and cheaper, [[talent:rogue:Efficient Strikes]] also discounts Hemorrhage, [[talent:rogue:Dual Wield Specialization]] adds 50% off-hand damage in two ranks, [[talent:rogue:Puncturing Wounds]] gives Backstab crit and extra combo points, and [[talent:rogue:Weapon Expertise]] takes 2% off dodge and parry.
- **Subtlety:** [[talent:rogue:Hemorrhage]] adds 15% to your Rupture and Garrote, [[talent:rogue:Ghostly Strike]] lets your next Ambush or Garrote skip Stealth, [[talent:rogue:Dirty Deeds]] lets you Garrote from the front, and [[talent:rogue:Quick Getaway]] allows a second Sap. [[talent:rogue:Death Mark]], a 20-yard finisher on a 10 sec cooldown, stores 12% of your damage per combo point for 6 sec, twice as much from Garrote and Rupture, then deals it, up to a cap that rises with your Attack Power and crit.
- **Poisons** gain twice as much from Attack Power since November 2, but still little: 0.01 per point for Instant Poison, 0.009 for Deadly Poison. Since November 3 they hold more charges: 250 for Instant Poison VI, 240 for Deadly Poison V.
- **Removed:** Improved Backstab, Murder, Deadliness, Improved Sap, Sleight of Hand and Dagger, Fist Weapon and Mace Specialization; Sword Specialization became Hack and Slash.

## Assassination

[[build:rogue-assassination-raid]]

Assassination needs two daggers for Mutilate, which now comes at 40. It deals a few percent less than Combat and brings the strongest poisons.

**Single target:**

1. Open with Garrote, 27 sec with Blood Splatter.
2. Keep Slice and Dice up.
3. Shank on cooldown, for Venom and 25 Energy.
4. Keep Rupture up, 22 sec at five points.
5. Mutilate at three combo points or fewer.
6. Eviscerate at four or five, 20 Energy with Flawless Execution. [[talent:rogue:Cold Blood]] on Mutilate or Eviscerate.

**Why these talents:** Relentless Strikes returns 25 Energy on every five-point finisher, and Seal Fate adds a third combo point when Mutilate crits, more often with Puncturing Wounds' 15%. Four points of [[talent:rogue:Opportunity]] add 16% to Mutilate and Garrote, and [[talent:rogue:Precision]] adds 3% hit. [[talent:rogue:Improved Poisons]] is left out: row 4 needs filler points on the way to Venom and Relentless Strikes, and Vile Poisons' 20% more poison damage is worth more than a higher chance to apply them.

## Combat

[[build:rogue-combat-raid]]

Combat is a really strong PvE pick: its damage holds up through long fights and works with any weapon type.

**Single target:**

1. Keep Slice and Dice up; [[talent:rogue:Improved Slice and Dice]] makes it last 45% longer.
2. Shank on cooldown.
3. [[talent:rogue:Blade Flurry]] and [[talent:rogue:Adrenaline Rush]] together. Blade Flurry adds 20% attack speed and hits a second target.
4. Sinister Strike to five combo points, 40 Energy each.
5. Eviscerate, with Cold Blood when it is ready.

**Why it wins:** Shank at rank 2 nets 20 Energy every 10 sec, 2 per second. Flash of Steel makes it 35 every 8 sec, about 4.4 per second, so the talent adds over 20% to your base regeneration of 10 per second. Dual Wield Specialization adds 50% to your off-hand for two points.

**Weapons:** at equal weapon damage, Hack and Slash's three bonuses are within about 2% of each other, so use your strongest weapon. Sinister Strike favors slow weapons, and maces pull ahead against high armor.

[[build:rogue-combat-daggers]]

With a good dagger in the main hand, Backstab beats Sinister Strike by a few percent: Puncturing Wounds adds 30% crit and a 45% chance of a second combo point, Opportunity 20%. Use it when you can stay behind the target.

## Subtlety

[[build:rogue-subtlety-raid]]

Subtlety builds with Hemorrhage and Shank, opens a Garrote every 20 sec through Ghostly Strike, and repeats part of its damage with Death Mark. At five points Death Mark stores 60% of your damage (120% of Garrote's and Rupture's) and deals it again 6 sec later, up to 1,381 at rank 4 plus more from Attack Power and crit, so most of a good window comes back.

**Single target:**

1. Premeditation, then open with Garrote.
2. Keep Slice and Dice and Rupture up. Hemorrhage adds 15% to both bleeds, [[talent:rogue:Serrated Blades]] 30% to Rupture.
3. Shank on cooldown.
4. Ghostly Strike, then Garrote if it has run out, Ambush from behind if not.
5. Death Mark at five points, then your hardest hits inside its 6 sec.
6. Hemorrhage as the builder, 30 Energy with Efficient Strikes; Eviscerate with spare points.

**Why Boot Knife:** a Hemorrhage costs 30 Energy per combo point. A Shank with Boot Knife adds 2 points and nets 20 Energy, worth about 80 Energy every 10 sec, almost as much as your natural regeneration.

## PvP

Critical strikes deal full damage in PvP since October 8, and roots from procs such as Improved Hamstring and Entrapment have diminishing returns since November 2.

### Subtlety

[[build:rogue-subtlety-pvp]]

Subtlety is a really strong PvP pick: it is the only tree with [[talent:rogue:Preparation]], and Premeditation, [[talent:rogue:Initiative]] and Dirty Deeds make the strongest opener.

1. Stealth in with [[talent:rogue:Master of Deception]] and [[talent:rogue:Camouflage]]. Sap a healer first; [[talent:rogue:Dirty Tricks]] halves the cost of Sap and Blind.
2. Wait for full Energy (the Sap used up Poised, which comes back out of combat at full Energy), then Premeditation and Cheap Shot: five combo points for 20 Energy.
3. Death Mark, then Shank, Hemorrhage and Ghostly Strike while it stores, and Kidney Shot as Cheap Shot ends.
4. Eviscerate for the kill, and Kick heals: [[talent:rogue:Improved Kick]] silences for 2 sec.
5. Vanish and open again. Preparation brings back Vanish, Evasion, Sprint, Blind and Premeditation.

Death Mark is a Curse, so mages, druids and Orcs can remove it.

### Combat

[[build:rogue-combat-pvp]]

Combat is built for long fights: Evasion and Sprint every 2 min with [[talent:rogue:Endurance]], [[talent:rogue:Riposte]] disarms for 6 sec after a parry, [[talent:rogue:Improved Gouge]] makes Gouge 5.5 sec, and Adrenaline Rush with Cold Blood is the burst. Use maces: ignoring 15% of armor counts most against plate. Without Premeditation or Preparation, its opener is smaller and it has one Vanish.

### Assassination

[[build:rogue-assassination-pvp]]

Assassination brings the most burst after an opener: Premeditation, Cheap Shot (three points with Initiative) and Mutilate set up an early Kidney Shot, [[talent:rogue:Improved Kidney Shot]] adds 10% damage while it lasts, and Cold Blood and Eviscerate finish. Venom keeps Crippling Poison landing and [[talent:rogue:Vile Poisons]] protects it from dispels. It has no Improved Sprint or Endurance, so snares and kiting classes are its hardest fights.

### Cooldowns and threats

Your key cooldowns are Evasion (50% dodge for 15 sec), Vanish (it also breaks snares), Sprint, Blind and Kidney Shot. [[talent:rogue:Elusiveness]] puts Vanish and Blind on 3 min 30 sec; [[talent:rogue:Improved Sprint]] makes Sprint clear snares.

- **Warriors:** Evasion when they open, Crippling Poison so they cannot follow, Sprint out of Hamstring.
- **Paladins:** wait out Divine Shield and Kick their heals.
- **Hunters:** open from Stealth and stay in melee; Sprint out of Concussive Shot and Wing Clip.
- **Mages:** Kick the first Frostbolt or Polymorph, Vanish or Sprint out of Frost Nova, Blind after a Blink.
- **Warlocks and priests:** Kick Fear, or let Will of the Forsaken break it.
- **Druids:** shapeshifting breaks snares, so stun them instead.
- **Rogues:** [[talent:rogue:Heightened Senses]] or Perception spots them first; whoever opens usually wins.

## Stat priorities

Special attacks stop missing a raid boss at 8% hit and players at 5%, but dual-wield auto attacks need about 27% against a boss, so hit stays useful long after your special attacks stop missing. [[talent:rogue:Precision]], the only rogue hit talent, gives 3%, and every build here takes it except Assassination PvP. Expertise lowers dodge and parry: 6.5% removes a boss's dodges from behind. [[talent:rogue:Weapon Expertise]] gives 2%. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Combat | Hit, weapon damage (dagger damage for the daggers build), Expertise, Agility, crit, Attack Power |
| Assassination | Hit, Expertise, Agility, crit, Attack Power, weapon damage |
| Subtlety | Hit, Expertise, Agility, Attack Power, crit, dagger damage |
| PvP | Stamina, Agility, hit, crit, Attack Power |

Paladins no longer have Salvation, so use Feint when you near the tank's threat.

## Leveling

Level as Combat: it works with any weapon, its damage talents come first, and Deflection into Riposte is its one defensive pick before 25.

[[build:rogue-combat-leveling]]

- Sinister Strike to three or four combo points, then Eviscerate; Slice and Dice on anything with a lot of health.
- Poised (18) takes 10 Energy off your first ability each pull, 20 with Composure. New spells no longer go on your bars by themselves, so place Shank (24) yourself and use it on cooldown.
- Instant Poison from 20, Deadly Poison from 30. Throw now has a 0.5 sec windup, so pulling with a thrown weapon is quick.
- Vanish, Blind and Sprint are your escapes; Blade Flurry (30) handles two-mob pulls. Dual Wield Specialization at 31 and 32 adds 50% to your off-hand's damage.
- With all five ranks of the Talented Legacy perk, every talent comes five levels sooner.

Subtlety also levels well if you open every pull from Stealth with a dagger. Assassination is the slowest until Mutilate at 40.

## Races

Ranked from the racials alone.

**PvE.** The spread is a few percent from first to last; Combat gains a little more from haste, Assassination from Eureka!.

1. **Undead:** [[racial:Touch of the Grave]] drains up to 5% of your maximum Health every 15 sec at most, the only racial with damage of its own.
2. **Gnome:** [[racial:Eureka!]] makes your direct-damage abilities 10% cheaper and 10% stronger for 8 sec every 2 min; [[racial:Expansive Mind]] adds 5% maximum Energy.
3. **Skyborne (both):** [[racial:Wind Blessed]], 2% haste for more auto attacks and poison procs.
4. **Orc:** [[racial:Blood Fury]], 10% Attack Power for 15 sec every 2 min, best with Adrenaline Rush.
5. **Human:** [[racial:Ruthless]], 1% crit with all spells and attacks.
6. **Dwarf:** [[racial:Artisan's Precision]], 1% hit, worth less once Precision is in your build.
7. **Night Elf:** [[racial:Elune's Light]], 10% crit for 15 sec every 3 min.
8. **Troll:** [[racial:Berserking]], 10% attack speed for 15 sec every 3 min.

**PvP.** The order suits all three specs; for Assassination's burst, Night Elf and Orc move up a place.

1. **Undead:** [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks fear, charm and sleep, and Touch of the Grave heals you in every fight.
2. **Human:** [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks stuns, the rogue's own weapon, and [[racial:Perception]] spots rogues first.
3. **Orc:** [[racial:Hardiness]] shortens stuns by 20%, and [[racial:Shatter Curse]] removes Curse of Exhaustion, Banes and an enemy's Death Mark.
4. **Night Elf:** [[racial:Quickness]] hides you as if you were a level higher, and Elune's Light adds 10% crit to your opener.
5. **Gnome:** [[racial:Escape Artist]] breaks roots and snares, and Eureka! adds 10% damage after your opener (not in Stealth).
6. **Troll:** [[racial:Rapid Regeneration]] heals 50% of your Health over 6 sec behind a Blind.
7. **Dwarf:** [[racial:Stoneform]] removes poisons and bleeds, Crippling Poison included, and cuts Physical damage by 10%.
8. **Windshaper Skyborne:** [[racial:Skysight]] adds 15% speed for flag runs, 30% after an elemental convergence.
9. **High Order Skyborne:** 2% haste and [[racial:Walk on Air]]; [[racial:Read Leyline]] only helps between fights.
