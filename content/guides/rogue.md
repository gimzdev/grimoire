The Forever rogue keeps Energy, combo points, poisons and stealth, and gets three trees that finally play differently from each other. Assassination dual-wields Mutilate and buffs its poisons, Combat replaces four weapon talents with one and can now pick up axes, and Subtlety builds its whole kit around Rupture.

| Topic | Details |
| --- | --- |

## What changed from Classic

- **Hack and Slash replaces the weapon specializations.** Dagger, Fist, Mace and Sword Specialization are gone. [[talent:rogue:Hack and Slash]] in Combat gives axes and swords a 5% chance for an extra attack, daggers and fists 5% crit, and maces 15% armor penetration.
- **Mutilate** is the new Assassination builder: both weapons for 75% weapon damage plus a bonus, 20% more against poisoned targets, and two combo points.
- **Venom** caps Assassination. It is a finishing move that raises your poison damage by 30% and your poison application chance by 10%, lasting from 9 seconds at one combo point to 21 at five.
- **Subtlety revolves around Rupture.** [[talent:rogue:Hemorrhage]] makes the target take 15% more Rupture damage, [[talent:rogue:Serrated Blades]] adds 30% Rupture damage and armor penetration, and [[talent:rogue:Thousand Cuts]] makes each Rupture tick lower the cost of your next Hemorrhage or Backstab.
- **[[talent:rogue:Cutthroat]]** gives Backstab a 15% chance to let your next Ambush ignore the Stealth requirement.
- **[[talent:rogue:Weapon Expertise]]** now reduces the chance to be dodged or parried by 2% instead of adding weapon skill.
- **Removed:** Improved Backstab, Deadliness, Improved Sap, Sleight of Hand and Dagger, Fist Weapon and Mace Specialization. Sword Specialization became Hack and Slash.

## Assassination

[[build:rogue-assassination-raid]]

Assassination wants two weapons with poisons on both. [[talent:rogue:Mutilate]] at 32 builds two combo points per press, and [[talent:rogue:Seal Fate]] adds another on every critical strike, so you often reach five points from two Mutilates.

**Single target:**

1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
2. Keep [[talent:rogue:Venom]] up. It boosts every poison tick and proc, and its duration grows with combo points.
3. Build with Mutilate.
4. Spend the rest on Eviscerate.
5. [[talent:rogue:Cold Blood]] guarantees a crit on the next Mutilate or Eviscerate.

[[talent:rogue:Improved Poisons]] adds 10% to poison application chance and gives each application a 50% chance not to use a charge, though the raid build does without it. [[talent:rogue:Vile Poisons]] adds 20% poison damage and makes your poisons harder to dispel. From Combat, [[talent:rogue:Puncturing Wounds]] adds 15% crit to Mutilate and [[talent:rogue:Precision]] 3% hit.

## Combat

[[build:rogue-combat-raid]]

Combat is the steady, forgiving spec, and it now works with any one-handed weapon type. Pick the weapon first and Hack and Slash adapts:

- **Swords or axes** for extra attacks. The best choice in most raids.
- **Daggers or fist weapons** for 5% crit.
- **Maces** against high-armor bosses.

**Single target:**

1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
2. Build with Sinister Strike, 5 Energy cheaper with [[talent:rogue:Improved Sinister Strike]].
3. Spend on Eviscerate, 10 Energy cheaper with [[talent:rogue:Flawless Execution]] and 20% stronger with [[talent:rogue:Improved Eviscerate]].
4. Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush together for burst. Blade Flurry now adds 20% attack speed and hits a second target for 15 seconds.

[[talent:rogue:Aggression]] adds 6% to Sinister Strike, Backstab and Eviscerate, and [[talent:rogue:Dual Wield Specialization]] 25% to the off-hand. The Assassination points cover [[talent:rogue:Malice]], [[talent:rogue:Ruthlessness]], [[talent:rogue:Murder]] and [[talent:rogue:Lethality]].

## Subtlety

[[build:rogue-subtlety-raid]]

Subtlety is the Rupture spec. You keep a Rupture ticking at all times and feed it with Hemorrhage or Backstab.

**Single target:**

1. Open from Stealth with Ambush. [[talent:rogue:Initiative]] adds a combo point and [[talent:rogue:Premeditation]] two more.
2. Keep Rupture and Hemorrhage on the target.
3. Build with Hemorrhage or, with a dagger, Backstab from behind. Every Rupture tick stacks Thousand Cuts, up to 15 Energy off your next builder.
4. When Cutthroat procs, Ambush without Stealth.
5. Spend extra combo points on Slice and Dice and Eviscerate.

[[talent:rogue:Ghostly Strike]] deals 180% weapon damage with a dagger and adds 15% dodge for 7 seconds. [[talent:rogue:Preparation]] resets your other cooldowns, a second Vanish, Sprint and Evasion that keeps Subtlety a staple of PvP.

## Stat priorities

Blizzard has not published raid caps for Forever yet. Dual-wield auto attacks miss far more often than special attacks, so hit stays valuable long after the special-attack cap.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Assassination | Hit, Agility, Critical Strike, Expertise, Attack Power, Haste |
| Combat | Hit, Expertise, Agility, Critical Strike, Haste, Attack Power |
| Subtlety | Hit, Agility, Critical Strike, Attack Power, Expertise |

Weapon choice matters more than any single stat for Combat because of Hack and Slash.

## Leveling

Level as Combat with a sword or an axe in the main hand. Riposte arrives at 23, Blade Flurry at 32, Hack and Slash from 33 and Adrenaline Rush at 43.

- Sinister Strike to three or four points, then Eviscerate. Use Slice and Dice on anything with a lot of health.
- Put poisons on your weapons as soon as you can: Instant Poison at 20, Deadly Poison at 30.
- Riposte and [[talent:rogue:Deflection]] make Combat surprisingly durable when you pull two mobs.
- Vanish, Blind and Sprint are your escape plan. [[talent:rogue:Endurance]] takes 60% off the cooldowns of Sprint and Evasion.
- With the Talented Legacy perk, every talent comes five levels sooner.

## Races

- **Human:** 1% crit with any weapon from [[racial:Ruthless]], plus Perception against other stealthers and Will to Survive against stuns.
- **Orc:** Blood Fury for burst and 20% shorter stuns from Hardiness.
- **Night Elf:** [[racial:Quickness]] makes you harder to detect in Stealth and adds 1% dodge, and Shadowmeld is a second vanish out of combat.
- **Gnome:** [[racial:Escape Artist]] breaks roots and snares.
- **Undead:** Will of the Forsaken breaks fear, charm and sleep, the best PvP racial for a rogue.
- **Dwarf:** Stoneform removes bleeds and poisons, and [[racial:Artisan's Precision]] adds 1% hit.
- **Troll:** Berserking for 10% attack speed.
- **Skyborne:** 2% haste, and [[racial:Walk on Air]] to glide down from cliffs and rooftops.
