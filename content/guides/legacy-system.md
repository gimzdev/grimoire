The Legacy system is Forever’s account-wide progression. Instead of making your character stronger forever, it rewards you for seeing more of the game: leveling different classes, mastering professions, exploring, running dungeons and raids, and fighting in PvP. You can earn everything it offers on one character, so alts are a choice, not a chore.

The [Legacy planner](/tools/legacy) has both halves: tick the challenges your account has done to count its points, then plan where a character spends them on the perk trees.

## Unlocking it

You gain access with your first Legacy Point. For most players that happens when their first character reaches **level 25**, but reaching 150 skill in a crafting profession or exploring the whole world map works too. Open the Legacy window from the shield icon on the menu bar or with the **Y** key.

## Legacy Challenges

There are 65 challenges. Each one is worth one Legacy Point, and points are shared by all of your non-Hardcore characters on the Battle.net account.

| Group | Points | Challenges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classes | 27 | For each of the nine classes, the first time a character of it reaches level 25 (Novice), 45 (Experienced) and 60 (Master) |
| Crafting | 18 | For Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring, the first time you reach 150 (Journeyman), 225 (Expert) and 300 (Artisan) |
| PvP | 12 | Honor ranks 3, 7, 10, 13 and 14; Exalted with each battleground’s faction (Master of Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, Alterac Valley and Darkspear Islands); weeks 4, 7 and 10 of the Field of Honor journey |
| Dungeons and raids | 6 | Novice, Experienced and Master Spelunker (below); Conqueror of the Lair (Onyxia), of the Wilds (all 13 Hyjal Summit bosses) and of the Deeps (all 8 Barrow Deeps bosses) |
| Other | 2 | Explorer (a character with the Explore Azeroth achievement); Lord Valthalak Laid to Rest (the questline from An Earnest Proposition to Saving the Best for Last) |

Levels, skills and honor ranks are climbed in order, so a character who reaches level 60 earns the level 25 and 45 challenges of its class on the way. The planner ticks the steps below a step you tick.

### Which dungeons each Spelunker challenge asks for

Each one wants the final boss of every dungeon on its list:

- **Novice Spelunker** (levels 15 to 25): [Ragefire Chasm](/dungeons/ragefire-chasm) or [the Hall of Thanes](/dungeons/hall-of-thanes), [Ruins of Lordaeron](/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron), [Wailing Caverns](/dungeons/wailing-caverns), [the Deadmines](/dungeons/the-deadmines), [Shadowfang Keep](/dungeons/shadowfang-keep) and [Whelgar’s Excavation](/dungeons/whelgars-excavation).
- **Experienced Spelunker** (levels 26 to 45): [City of Dalaran](/dungeons/city-of-dalaran), [Blackfathom Deeps](/dungeons/blackfathom-deeps), [Gnomeregan](/dungeons/gnomeregan), [the Drowned City](/dungeons/drowned-city), all four wings of [Scarlet Monastery](/dungeons/scarlet-monastery), [Razorfen Kraul](/dungeons/razorfen-kraul) and [Krol’dok Stronghold](/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold).
- **Master Spelunker** (levels 46 to 60): [Razorfen Downs](/dungeons/razorfen-downs), [Uldaman](/dungeons/uldaman), [Zul’Farrak](/dungeons/zulfarrak), [Maraudon](/dungeons/maraudon), [Alcaz Prison](/dungeons/alcaz-prison), [the Temple of Atal’Hakkar](/dungeons/the-temple-of-atalhakkar), [Blackrock Depths](/dungeons/blackrock-depths), [Blackmaw Hold](/dungeons/blackmaw-hold), Lower and Upper [Blackrock Spire](/dungeons/blackrock-spire), [Scholomance](/dungeons/scholomance), both sides of [Stratholme](/dungeons/stratholme), all three wings of [Dire Maul](/dungeons/dire-maul) and [the Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace).

## Legacy Perks

Each character spends the account’s points on perks for itself, so different characters can specialize in different things. At launch a character can spend **up to 16 points**, one per rank. That cap keeps new players close to veterans, and 16 points are reachable on a single character: one character can earn 3 points from leveling, up to 6 from tradeskills, 12 from PvP, 2 from Explorer and Lord Valthalak, and 6 from dungeons and raids: 29 in all.

The three trees work like talent trees:

- Perks in a tree’s first column are open from the start. The next column opens once the tree has **5 points** in the perks before it, and the one after at **10**.
- A few perks need the perk before them at **full rank**, shown by an arrow.
- Each tree has two slots the game marks *To be added in a future update*.

The numbers below are from the beta client, so Blizzard can still change them before launch.

## Legacy Rewards

Points also unlock cosmetic rewards for the whole account. Four are available at launch, with more planned for every content update:

- **Replica Ironforge Air Rifle**, a toy for shootouts that stun other players and keep score.
- **Spectral Bear Cub**, a pet inspired by the dwarf hunter and his bear in Forever’s cinematic.
- **Spectral Bear Tabard**.
- **Reins of the Spectral Bear**, a spectral mount.

## Hardcore and Legacy

Hardcore is not in at launch. When it arrives, challenges earned in other rulesets will not show up in Hardcore, but challenges earned in Hardcore will count in every other ruleset. Because PvP challenges cannot be done in Hardcore, it will get 12 challenges of its own.

## What to prioritize

1. Hit **level 25** on your main to unlock the system.
2. The quickest early points are your main reaching 45 and 60 and one crafting profession reaching 150 and 225.
3. Spend by what the character does. Grimoire’s four builds, each 16 points (the planner has them under Grimoire builds):

- [Leveling an alt](/tools/legacy?tab=perks&p=kkkkklllllooooot): Well Rested and Talented, then Thrill of Adventure and a point of Field Medicine.
- [Raiding](/tools/legacy?tab=perks&p=EEAAABBIIIGJJJJJ): The Quick and the Dead, Gourmand and Reinforce open Permanence and Reagent Economy, and Diplomat takes the rest.
- [Battlegrounds](/tools/legacy?tab=perks&p=EEIIIxxxxxIIGAAA): The Quick and the Dead and Reinforce open For Great Honor, then Reagent Economy and Gourmand.
- [Crafting and gathering](/tools/legacy?tab=perks&p=4444488888556999): Working Overtime and Bountiful Harvest, Bartering, Dedicated Study and Performance Bonus.
