Professions look like Classic on the surface: two main ones, three secondary ones, a trainer in every city. Underneath, Blizzard says there are over 600 new recipes, reworked crafted food that gives stats and a little experience, and a camping system that turns crafting into buffs for everyone at the fire. This page keeps four kinds of fact apart: what Blizzard has said, what Blizzard's beta notes changed, what the launch client's data shows without Blizzard having mentioned it, and what Blizzard's hotfixes have changed since launch. Skill caps per rank are not published yet.

For leveling, [Professions 1 to 300](/guides/professions-1-to-300) has a Leatherworking path and what Skinning gives at each level, and [Fishing by skill](/guides/fishing) has where to fish.

## At a glance

| Topic | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Main professions | You learn two. Gathering: Herbalism, Mining, Skinning. Crafting: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring |
| Secondary professions | You learn all three: Cooking, Fishing, First Aid |
| Ranks | Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, Artisan |
| Trainers | In the starting areas and the big cities; they teach higher ranks as your level and skill grow |
| Skill cap | Not published. The client shows 300 for every profession, and the [Legacy challenges](/guides/legacy-system) ask for 150, 225 and 300 |
| Not in the game | Jewelcrafting is in none of Blizzard's lists |

## Camping

Camping is Forever's biggest profession change. What Blizzard has said:

- **The campfire.** Cooking's Cooking Fire becomes the **Basic Campfire**, learned at Cooking skill 1. It is a shared campsite that holds up to 3 camp features, each placed by a different nearby player. Cooks later learn campfires that hold 5 or 10; Blizzard has not said which holds which.
- **Who gets the buffs.** Anyone near the camp who sits or crafts there for at least a minute and is Rested, not only your group. Camp effects are variants of class buffs and do not stack with the matching class buff. They last an hour.
- **The cooldown.** Every camp feature shares one hour-long cooldown. The Field Guide Legacy perk shortens it.
- **Where the items come from.** Items for skill 20 come from starter-zone quests or the trainer. Anything that needs skill 140 or more drops in dungeons and raids as Blueprint recipes from specific bosses.
- **Safety.** On a PvP ruleset, enemy players can find your camp.

Blizzard's sample of the skill-20 camp effects (the ranges scale with level, and Blizzard calls the list a sample):

| Camp feature | Profession | Effect | Class buff it replaces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First Aid Kit | First Aid | 3 to 56 Stamina | Power Word: Fortitude |
| Mana Well | Alchemy | 10 to 29 mana per 5 seconds | Blessing of Wisdom |
| Sharpening Wheel | Blacksmithing | 6 to 34 Strength | Strength of Earth Totem |
| Enchanted Lute | Enchanting | 28 to 260 armor, up to 13 to all stats, up to 22 to all resistances | Mark of the Wild |
| Incense Candle | Herbalism | 2 to 25 Intellect | Arcane Intellect |
| Camp Chair | Skinning | 2% critical strike chance with spells and attacks | Moonkin Aura |
| Faction Banner | Tailoring | 14 to 32 Spirit, own faction only | Divine Spirit |
| Fishing Bowl | Fishing | 8% to all stats | Blessing of Kings |
| Camp Tent | Leatherworking | 5% of a level of Rested experience | none |
| Reagent Bot | Engineering | Nearby players can buy reagents | none |

## What the launch client shows

Blizzard has not announced this part. It comes from the client's own data as read by a fan site, wowforevertalents.com, for build 1.60.1.70707, the client Forever launched with; what Blizzard's hotfixes changed since is under Since launch below.

**Camp crafts.** Every profession has three camp crafts, at skill 20, 140 and 300. The 20s match Blizzard's table above.

| Profession | Skill 20 | Skill 140 | Skill 300 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alchemy | Mana Well | Fermenter | Alchemy Laboratory |
| Blacksmithing | Sharpening Wheel | Anvil | Master Forge |
| Enchanting | Enchanted Lute | Arcane Salvager | Arcane Forge |
| Engineering | Reagent Bot | Repair Bot | Anarchist's Workbench |
| Leatherworking | Camp Tent | Tanning Rack (125 since November 6) | Sewing Machine |
| Tailoring | Faction Banner | Spinning Wheel | Loom |
| Herbalism | Incense Candle | Greenhouse | Seed Hybridizer |
| Skinning | Camp Chair | Field Guide | Trapper's Workbench |
| Mining | Lodestone | Rock Garden | Molten Foundry |
| Fishing | Fishing Bowl | Fishing Rack | Fishing Hut |
| First Aid | First Aid Kit | Toxin Study | Plague Doctor's Laboratory |

Cooking has five: the Basic Campfire at 1, then campfires at 90 and 200, Cookie's Feast at 140 and the Iron Oven at 300. The 20-skill crafts are taught by trainers and the later ones by Blueprint items. The Tanning Rack belongs to Leatherworking, not Skinning, and the Smelt Heavy Thorium recipe needs a Molten Foundry.

**Recipes.** The client holds 2,205 profession recipes: 958 new in Forever, 834 changed from Classic Era, 12 moved to another profession and 6 removed. The six healing potions, Minor to Major, are First Aid recipes now, not Alchemy. Gone are Elixir of Tongues, Elixir of Poison Resistance, Cowardly Flight Potion, Purification Potion, Charged Scale of Onyxia and Crystal Throat Lozenge. Most recipes come from recipe items rather than trainers: 358 of Blacksmithing's 441, 436 of Leatherworking's 512 and 112 of Cooking's 125.

**Merchant's Favor.** A new currency you earn by turning in crates to the Azeroth Commerce Authority (Alliance) or Durotar Supply and Logistics (Horde); the Performance Bonus Legacy perk gives a chance of double. Profession vendors at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains sell 304 recipes for it, at 30 to 360 each: Leatherworking 81, Tailoring 67, Blacksmithing 61, Enchanting 39, Engineering 28 and Alchemy 28. That stock was seen on the beta, not read from the client.

**The 31 October additions.** The client's hotfix cache of 31 October gives records to 36 recipe items that crafts already named as their source. Blacksmithing plans (15, rare, bind on pickup) cover the Veteran's, Protector's, Acolyte's and Crusader's chain sets (shirts at 80, leggings at 95, helms at 125; Guard's has no shirt) and the Shining Mithril Helm at 215. Leatherworking patterns (18, rare, tradeable) cover the Brawler's, Defender's, Stormrider's, Totemic, Trapper's and Wisdom's sets (armor at 75, pants at 100, helm at 125). Enchanting formulas (3, rare, bind on pickup, skill 265) teach the Frozen, Molten and Dormant Heart of the Mountain. The same cache adds a Formula: Enchant Boots - Speed (skill 280, 8% movement speed) and cuts Enchant Boots - Minor Speed from 8% to 4%. None of this appears in a Blizzard post yet.

## From Blizzard's beta notes

- **Cultivation.** The Tauren racial now needs a player level equal to the herb's pick requirement divided by 5, so at level 60 it works on herbs that need more than 300 Herbalism.
- **Bartering.** The Legacy perk no longer discounts training or repairs.
- **Gathering.** Poor Copper Vein nodes are rarer in starter areas, and creatures can again be skinned only once.
- **Crafted goods.** Wizard Oils are back to Classic values (8, 16 and 24 spell power), wands no longer gain the user's spell damage, many profession-made items sell for less, and Skinning can be learned in Elwynn Forest even while you hold a quest from the trainer.
- **Camping.** You can craft, cast and buff others while you wait for the Campfire buff, and you can receive it sitting, lying down or kneeling.

## Since launch

Blizzard's hotfixes of November 5 to 13 changed these:

- **Blacksmithing.** "The Old Ways" gives the Orcish War Leggings plans as an item; if you never got them or lost them, Sumi in the blacksmithing hut nearby replaces them once the quest is done. Bronze Mace binds when equipped, where it did not bind at all (November 17).
- **Mining.** Miners can track Spectral Iron Ore in Shen’dralas, and it spawns more often (November 17).
- **Herbalism and Skinning.** Find Herbs shows the wildflowers of "Her World" only while you are on the quest, and a few creatures that were never meant to be skinned no longer can be (November 17).
- **Crates and writs.** Some Waylaid Crate conversions gave an obsolete turn-in item: those crates can now be handed in for the right reward. Craftsman's Writ: Golden Scale Gauntlets points to who takes it (November 17).
- **Enchanting.** Formula: Enchant Boots - Speed, the one bought with reputation, binds when picked up (November 16).
- **Bags (Tailoring).** Since November 11: Red Mageweave Bag takes 5 bolts of mageweave (was 4) and holds 14; Green Silk Pack takes 5 bolts of silk (was 4) and Black Silk Pack 6 (was 5), both holding 12; Green Woolen Bag and Red Woolen Bag take 5 bolts of wool (was 4) and hold 10. Grimoire's item pages show these bags without their slots, so the numbers are here.
- **Items.** Bloody Claws of Attack add +1 Fist and Unarmed skill, and Earthen Silk Slippers no longer give too much spell damage (November 11). Glimmering Mithril Insignia, the Blacksmithing trinket, no longer needs Mail (November 13).

- **Tanning Rack (Leatherworking).** The skill it needs is 125, not 140, so it no longer stands in the way of the many patterns at 125, and the skill-ups it gives are 15 points lower to match (November 6).
- **Campfires (Cooking).** The Journeyman Campfire Kit can be placed at Cooking 90, the skill its recipe asks for; it needed 140 (November 10).
- **Arcane Salvager (Enchanting).** It needs a Runed Golden Rod to craft, not a Runed Truesilver Rod, and its disenchanting bonus now works for enchanters of lower skill too (November 10).
- **Enchants.** Enchant Boots and Enchant Bracer - Lesser Spirit give Spirit, as their names say; they gave resistances (November 10).
- **Blacksmithing.** Coarse Grinding Stones give skill-ups up to 90, not 75 (November 10).
- **Alchemy.** Recipe: Elixir of Greater Firepower is called Recipe: Elixir of Holy Power, and the elixir's buff Holy Power, to match what it does (November 10).
- **Engineering.** Swiftstrike Shot and Swiftfeather Arrow are no longer bound when picked up (November 10).
- **Fishing.** Fishing Racks give their lures to everyone, and the Bloated Fish caught with them drop more often (November 16).
- **Selling crafts.** Vendors pay less for some crafted Leatherworking, Tailoring and Blacksmithing armor, for Iron Grenades (1 silver 25 copper, was 5 silver), Minor Arcane Elixir (5 copper, was 25) and Mild Spices (1 copper) (November 5 and 10).
- **Camps and crates.** A Pouch of Violet Dust gives the extra hour of Camp Benefits it should, and Waylaid Crates and Craftsman's Writs drop in dungeons again (November 9).
- **Cultivation.** The Tauren racial goes on cooldown only once its cast completes (November 9), and it works on Mageroyal (November 17).
- **Zephras Isle.** Its trade supply vendors sell the apprentice tradeskill bags (November 5).

## Leatherworking's three specializations

From level 40 a leatherworker can take one of three specializations, each through a quest from its master. The choice is permanent: each quest says so, and each specialization has patterns of its own.

| Specialization | Alliance master | Horde master | The quest asks for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dragonscale (mail) | Peter Galen, Azshara | Thorkaf Dragoneye, Badlands | 2 Tough Scorpid Breastplates, 2 Tough Scorpid Gloves, 10 Worn Dragonscale |
| Elemental | Sarah Tanner, Searing Gorge | Brumn Winterhoof, Arathi Highlands | 2 each of Heart of Fire, Globe of Water, Core of Earth and Breath of Wind |
| Tribal | Caryssia Moonhunter, Feralas | Se'Jib, Stranglethorn Vale | a Wild Leather Vest and a Wild Leather Helmet |

These are Classic's quests as Grimoire's quest data holds them; Blizzard has announced no change to them.

## What Blizzard has not published

Skill caps per rank and the level you need to train each one, trainer locations and costs, a full recipe list, the full list of camp features (Mining's is missing from Blizzard's table), the effect of the Alchemy Laboratory and the Tanning Rack, what the crates hold, and what Merchant's Favor is worth.
