The Forever shaman fixes the class's oldest chores and gives every tree a rotation of its own. A totem bar drops four totems in one cast, Elemental's bolts can fire twice, Enhancement turns Intellect into Attack Power, and Restoration pairs Riptide with Chain Heal. Dwarves and the Windshaper Skyborne can now be shamans, so the class is no longer Horde-only. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips, with Blizzard's hotfixes up to November 13, which turned Enhancement back toward Windfury.

## What changed from Classic

- **Totems.** Three Call spells each drop up to four totems from your totem bar in one 3-second cast; Totemic Recall refunds 25% of their mana, and Totemic Call (30-second cooldown) teleports them to you. Strength of Earth, Grace of Air, Windfury and Flametongue Totems reach the whole raid, though Windfury and Flametongue still exclude each other. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 10 a weapon totem and your own weapon imbue work at the same time.
- **Fire Nova is a spell** that burns enemies within 10 yards of your Fire totem.
- **Lightning hits harder.** Lightning Bolt casts in 2.5 seconds from rank 3 up (Classic's top ranks took 3), and on November 2 every rank's base damage rose, the top one by 80%, from 189 to 211 to 342 to 380. Chain Lightning casts in 2.5 seconds for about 145% of the matching bolt's base damage and 225% of its mana: 495 to 551 for 495 mana at rank 4. Shocks are back to Classic Era base damage.
- **Elemental:** [[talent:shaman:Lightning Overload]] gives your bolts a 30% chance to cast again for half damage, and [[talent:shaman:Call of Thunder]] (5% crit) took the place of the beta's Lava Burst. [[talent:shaman:Lightning Mastery]], the renamed Elemental Mastery, makes Lightning Bolt 0.5 seconds faster and removes Chain Lightning's cooldown for 15 seconds. [[talent:shaman:Concussion]] no longer covers Flame or Frost Shock.
- **Enhancement:** [[talent:shaman:Mental Dexterity]] turns 100% of your Intellect into Attack Power (it was 150% before November 10) and [[talent:shaman:Mental Quickness]] 30% into spell damage and healing. [[talent:shaman:Stormstrike]] hits for 100% weapon damage every 8 seconds since November 10 (it was 150% every 6) and adds 20% to your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock. Each [[talent:shaman:Maelstrom Weapon]] stack takes 20% off your next Lightning Bolt's cast and mana at every rank, it triggers 6 to 10 times a minute by rank, and five stacks make the bolt instant and free. [[talent:shaman:Flurry]] reaches 30% attack speed.
- **Restoration:** [[talent:shaman:Water Shield]] returns 2% of your maximum mana when you are hit or a spell crits. [[talent:shaman:Riptide]] now only heals over time, on a 12-second cooldown at about 40% less mana, and makes Chain Heal cast on its target 25% stronger. [[talent:shaman:Tidal Focus]] also gives 5% hit; Tidal Mastery covers heals only.
- **Renamed:** Classic's Lightning Mastery is now [[talent:shaman:Elemental Alacrity]]. Improved Fire Totems is Trial by Fire, Storm Reach is Elemental Reach, Parry is Spirit Weapons and Healing Grace is Natural Grace.
- **Removed:** Shield Specialization, Enhancing Totems, Two-Handed Axes and Maces, Improved Weapon Totems, Weapon Mastery, Nature's Guidance and Totemic Mastery.

## Elemental

[[build:shaman-elemental-raid]]

**Single target:**

1. Keep Water Shield up and a Searing Totem down, unless your group needs a different Fire totem.
2. Lightning Bolt is your main spell, 2 seconds with Elemental Alacrity.
3. Chain Lightning on cooldown when it will hit two or more targets. On one target it costs more than twice a bolt's mana for at most 45% more damage, so cast it there only while your mana is high.
4. Lightning Mastery for burst: Lightning Bolts at 1.5 seconds on one target, Chain Lightning back to back on two or more.
5. Flame Shock while you move. Earth Shock hits hard but costs a lot of mana.

Lightning Overload adds 15% to your bolts for three points. Call of Thunder is worth about 4% for its single point once [[talent:shaman:Elemental Fury]] doubles your crit damage bonus. Elemental Fury adds about 7 to 11% at 15 to 25% crit. Long fights are a mana problem, which is why the build takes Convection, Elemental Focus and three Restoration tools: Tidal Focus (5% hit, worth about 6% damage against a raid boss), Mindfulness and Water Shield. [[talent:shaman:Thundering Strikes]] adds 5% crit, about 4% damage.

## Enhancement

[[build:shaman-enhancement-raid]]

**Single target:**

1. Windfury Weapon, Lightning Shield and your totems before the pull. Windfury Totem reaches the whole raid, and since November 10 it works for you alongside Windfury Weapon.
2. Stormstrike on cooldown.
3. Spend Stormstrike's 20% bonus on an instant Lightning Bolt at five Maelstrom stacks, otherwise on Earth Shock. [[talent:shaman:Shamanistic Focus]] makes shocks 45% cheaper, but in a long fight you cannot afford one on every cooldown.
4. [[talent:shaman:Rage of the Farseer]] (30% attack speed for 25 seconds) with your racial and trinkets.

Blizzard's hotfixes of November 10 moved the spec's damage from Intellect and Stormstrike back to your swings and Windfury: Mental Dexterity now adds 250 Attack Power at 250 Intellect (275 with Ancestral Knowledge), and Stormstrike comes three quarters as often for two thirds of the damage, half what it dealt. Thundering Strikes is worth about 8% melee damage and Flurry about 6%. Tidal Focus's 5% hit is worth up to about 7% until you reach the cap.

**Imbue.** Windfury Weapon, 40% stronger with Elemental Weapons. Until November 10 it switched a Windfury Totem off for you, and Rockbiter Weapon plus the totem dealt more; since Blizzard's hotfixes of that day the imbue and the totem work together, so your swings get a chance at both, and [[talent:shaman:Spirit Weapons]] cuts your threat by 30% while Rockbiter is not on.

## Restoration

[[build:shaman-restoration-heal]]

Restoration is a really strong PvE pick: groups always need healers, the toolkit is complete at 40, and nothing in it depends on a hit cap.

**How it plays:**

1. Riptide on cooldown on the tank or whoever takes the next hit. It no longer heals up front, so cast it before the damage.
2. Chain Heal cast on the Riptide target, which heals that target for 25% more.
3. Healing Wave for big single-target heals: [[talent:shaman:Healing Way]] adds 25% and Improved Healing Wave cuts the cast to 2.5 seconds.
4. Lesser Healing Wave when speed matters, Nature's Swiftness for an instant Healing Wave.
5. Mana Tide Totem when your group runs low, and Water Shield again whenever its three globes are spent.

[[talent:shaman:Purification]] adds 10% to all your healing. Tidal Focus, Mindfulness, Water Shield and [[talent:shaman:Totemic Focus]] carry the mana, and Thundering Strikes adds 5% crit, which pays twice: each crit can return 2% of your mana through Water Shield. [[talent:shaman:Ancestral Healing]] raises the target's armor by 25% for 15 seconds after a crit.

## PvP

Enhancement is a really strong PvP pick: most of its damage is instant melee, so interrupts, pushback and school lockouts barely touch it, and its own tree gives it instant Ghost Wolf, Toughness, dodge and parry. Restoration keeps a team alive and controls fights with totems and Purge, and Elemental hits hardest from range when it gets room to cast.

[[build:shaman-enhancement-pvp]]

- **Burst:** Stormstrike, Earth Shock with its 20% bonus, then a five-stack Lightning Bolt, with Rage of the Farseer and your racial. Use Windfury Weapon: its procs are the burst, and it needs no totem.
- **Defense:** Improved Stormstrike resets Stormstrike when you dodge or parry, so Anticipation and Spirit Weapons count as damage too. Kiting is your problem: instant Ghost Wolf closes the gap and Frost Shock slows them, but neither breaks a root.

[[build:shaman-restoration-pvp]]

- **Healing:** Riptide on the move, Nature's Swiftness for an instant Healing Wave, Healing Focus against pushback.
- **Control:** Grounding Totem every 13 seconds, Purge, and instant Ghost Wolf to reach a teammate or escape. You kill slowly, so you win by outlasting.

[[build:shaman-elemental-pvp]]

- **Damage:** shocks every 5 seconds with Reverberation, and Lightning Mastery's 1.5-second bolts once the target is rooted or busy.
- **Control:** Earthbound roots everything near your Earthbind Totem for 5 seconds and Frost Shock slows by 50%. Melee and interrupts are your problem: root, step away, then cast. If Nature is locked out, Frost Shock and Flame Shock still work.

**Totems, Purge and cooldowns.** Grounding Totem takes one harmful spell and dies, so drop it before a Polymorph, Fear or Death Coil. Tremor Totem removes Fear, Charm and Sleep. [[ability:shaman:Purge]] removes two beneficial magic effects: Power Word: Shield, Ice Barrier, Blessing of Freedom, Rejuvenation and Innervate first. Nature's Swiftness, Lightning Mastery and Rage of the Farseer have 3-minute cooldowns, Reincarnation an hour (40 minutes with Improved Reincarnation).

| Against | What works |
| --- | --- |
| Rogues | Poison Cleansing Totem; Frost Shock and Earthbind to break away |
| Warriors | Frost Shock and Earthbind to kite; Tremor Totem for Intimidating Shout |
| Mages | Grounding before Polymorph; Purge Ice Barrier; Earth Shock their casts |
| Warlocks | Tremor for Fear, Howl of Terror and Seduction; Grounding for Death Coil; Orcs clear Curse of Tongues with Shatter Curse |

## Stat priorities

Against a raid boss your spells stop missing at 17% hit and your attacks at 8%, auto attacks included, since shamans cannot dual-wield. Against players it is 4% for spells and 5% for attacks. Tidal Focus's 5% counts for both, and Expertise, which lowers dodge and parry, removes a boss's dodges at 6.5% from behind. No Forever item adds flat haste. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Elemental | Hit, spell damage, Critical Strike, Intellect, mana per 5 seconds |
| Enhancement | Hit, Expertise, Strength, Critical Strike, Agility, Intellect |
| Restoration | Healing, Intellect, mana per 5 seconds, Critical Strike |

Enhancement still gets something from Intellect, 1 Attack Power a point through Mental Dexterity since the changes of November 10 (live everywhere November 12) plus spell damage and mana for its shocks, but Strength gives 2 and Windfury rewards Strength and crit, as Blizzard meant it to. Elemental and Restoration move Intellect and mana per 5 seconds up for long fights, and crits refill mana through Water Shield. Compare items in the [item database](/items?class=shaman).

## Leveling

Level as Enhancement: most of your damage costs no mana, you wear mail from 40, and Stormstrike arrives at 25.

[[build:shaman-enhancement-leveling]]

- **Rockbiter Weapon until 30, then Windfury Weapon.** Since the Shaman changes listed on November 10, live in every region since November 12, your own Windfury Totem (from 32) works alongside Windfury Weapon, so your swings get a chance at both.
- Drop your totem bar with Call of the Elements from 20, bring it along with Totemic Call from 22, and pick it up with Totemic Recall for a quarter of the mana back.
- Improved Ghost Wolf moved to row 4, so instant Ghost Wolf comes at 26-27. Improved Stormstrike (30-31) resets Stormstrike when you dodge or parry, which is why Spirit Weapons comes early (32) and Anticipation's last points come later.
- Rage of the Farseer comes at 40, after Mental Quickness, which it needs.
- Five ranks of the Talented Legacy perk move every talent five levels earlier, Stormstrike to 20.
- The [talent calculator](/talents/shaman) also has Elemental and Restoration leveling builds.
