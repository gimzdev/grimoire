Level with a Cat or a Boar, take a Wolf to dungeons and raids, and in PvP bring a Wind Serpent, or a Crocolisk, Bat or Bird of Prey for what its ability takes from the other side. You get [[ability:hunter:Tame Beast]] at 10. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 10, a pet has 115% of its normal health; it had 150% by mistake.

## Which family

- **Solo leveling: a Cat or a Boar.** A Cat learns both [[ability:hunter:Claw]] and [[ability:hunter:Bite]], and [[ability:hunter:Prowl]] gives the first attack from stealth a 50% bonus to damage. A Boar opens with [[ability:hunter:Charge]], which immobilizes the target for 1 sec and adds 490 melee attack power to the boar's next attack.
- **Dungeons and raids: a Wolf.** [[ability:hunter:Furious Howl]] gives every party or raid member within 15 yards 83 melee attack power for 1 min, on a 30 sec cooldown. A Cat is the pick when nobody in melee needs the howl.
- **Raids without a pet.** The [Marksmanship raid build](/builds/hunter/marksmanship-raid) leaves the pet out: [[talent:hunter:Lone Wolf]] gives you 20% increased damage with all attacks while you do not have an active pet.
- **PvP: a Wind Serpent.** [[ability:hunter:Lightning Breath]] deals Nature damage from 20 yards, so the pet keeps hitting a target that runs from it.
- **PvP in a premade:** a Crocolisk for [[ability:hunter:Dismember]] (25% less healing for 6 sec), a Bat for [[ability:hunter:Sonic Blast]] (casting time 30% longer for 20 sec) or a Bird of Prey for [[ability:hunter:Mine!]] (a 3 sec disarm).

These are the families Warcraft Tavern's Forever pet guide recommends for each job (read November 14); the reasons above are the abilities' own tooltips. Pets inherit your crit chance, so the hunter's crit talents raise the pet's damage too.

Feed a new pet at once: the [[ability:hunter:Tame Beast]] tooltip says a beast is very unhappy and disloyal when you tame it, and [[ability:hunter:Feed Pet]] raises its happiness. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 9, Vulgara the Insatiable and the Shrieklings of Zephras belong to the right pet families.
