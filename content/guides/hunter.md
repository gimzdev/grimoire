Forever splits the hunter into three clearly different specs. Beast Mastery sends a pet and two hawks after the target, Marksmanship is the long-range sniper, and Survival fights in melee. Humans can be hunters, and the November 2 changes made your shots much stronger while leaving pet damage alone. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips, with Blizzard's hotfixes up to November 13: since then a pet has 115% of its normal health (it had 150% by mistake), Arcane Shot and Summon Hawk cost 11% of your base mana at every rank, and the hawks hit about 80% harder.

## What changed from Classic

- **Aimed Shot is baseline:** a 2-second cast sharing a 6-second cooldown with Multi-Shot. Auto Shot keeps firing while you cast it or Sniper Shot.
- **Auto Shot needs you to stand still** for half a second before it fires, so moving costs you shots.
- **Launch numbers.** Aimed Shot's bonus rose about 110% (350 at rank 6), Arcane Shot deals 280 plus 15% of your Attack Power, Serpent Sting 675 plus 5% of your Attack Power per tick, and Sniper Shot's bonus doubled to 590. Aspect of the Hawk gives 120 Ranged Attack Power at 60, and Trueshot Aura 100 to the whole raid.
- **Hunter's Mark** ranks no longer stack on one target; rank 4 gives 143 Ranged Attack Power. [[ability:hunter:Disengage]] sheds twice the threat it did.
- **Traps** share one 30-second cooldown, one Fire and one Frost trap can be out at once, and Fire traps scale with Attack Power. [[talent:hunter:Entrapment]] roots on every trigger, but roots from procs now have diminishing returns.
- **Intellect is a damage stat.** [[talent:hunter:Careful Aim]] adds Attack Power equal to 100% of your Intellect, and every build here takes it except Beast Mastery PvP.
- **New tools:** [[talent:hunter:Summon Hawk]] keeps up to two hawks on a target, [[talent:hunter:Lone Wolf]] adds 20% damage with no pet out, and [[talent:hunter:Rapid Recuperation]], [[talent:hunter:Bestial Discipline]] and [[talent:hunter:Resourcefulness]] keep regeneration running while you fight.
- **Pets** inherit your crit chance and keep their innate attack speed. New family abilities include [[ability:hunter:Dismember]] (Crocolisk, 25% less healing), [[ability:hunter:Sonic Blast]] (Bat, 30% slower casts) and [[ability:hunter:Mine!]] (Bird of Prey, 3-second disarm). [Hunter pets](/guides/hunter-pets) has the family for each job, every family's ability and where to tame one at every level.
- **Survival fights in melee** around Mongoose Bite, [[talent:hunter:Expose Prey]] and [[talent:hunter:Lacerating Strikes]].
- **Removed:** Wyvern Sting, Trap Mastery, Killer Instinct, Improved Feign Death, Thick Hide, Improved Eyes of the Beast, Improved Hunter's Mark and Improved Scorpid Sting. Improved Serpent Sting became [[talent:hunter:Improved Stings]], which also shortens Viper Sting's cooldown and lengthens Scorpid Sting. The slaying talents became [[talent:hunter:Improved Tracking]], and Lethal Shots [[talent:hunter:Lethal Attacks]].

## Beast Mastery

[[build:hunter-beast-mastery-raid]]

Beast Mastery puts a large share of your damage on the pet and two hawks, and it is the easiest spec on mana: Bestial Discipline keeps half your regeneration running while you shoot. [[talent:hunter:Bestial Wrath]] adds 50% pet damage for 18 seconds every 2 minutes.

**Single target:**

1. Hunter's Mark, then Serpent Sting, your best damage per point of mana.
2. Summon Hawk whenever fewer than two are out. Hawks stay on the target you cast them on.
3. Aimed Shot while mana allows. Arcane Shot puts Summon Hawk on cooldown and Aimed Shot does not.
4. Arcane Shot only while you move.
5. Bestial Wrath and Rapid Fire together.

Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 10 the hawks deal about 80% more melee damage. [[talent:hunter:Unleashed Fury]] and [[talent:hunter:Ferocity]] buff them too, and your pet inherits your crit, so Lethal Attacks also feeds [[talent:hunter:Frenzy]]. [[talent:hunter:Deadly Aspects]] comes first because Auto Shot costs no mana: its 10% chance means about 13% more Auto Shots. Only one Trueshot Aura counts per raid; if a Marksmanship hunter brings it, move that point and Rapid Killing into [[talent:hunter:Endurance Training]]. A Wolf's [[ability:hunter:Furious Howl]] gives 83 melee Attack Power to everyone within 15 yards.

## Marksmanship

[[build:hunter-marksmanship-raid]]

Marksmanship is a really strong PvE pick. Its damage does not depend on a pet surviving, [[talent:hunter:Trueshot Aura]] gives every hunter within 45 yards 100 Ranged Attack Power, and the November 2 changes went straight into its shots.

**Mana decides your damage.** Aimed Shot on every cooldown plus Serpent Sting costs 4,100 mana a minute, about a full mana bar. Per point of mana, Serpent Sting deals about 5 damage, Aimed Shot 3.8, Sniper Shot 3.6 and Arcane Shot 3.2 (at 1,500 Ranged Attack Power with this build). So the build takes [[talent:hunter:Efficiency]], Rapid Recuperation and [[talent:hunter:Improved Stings]], and skips [[talent:hunter:Improved Arcane Shot]].

**Auto Shot is free** and about half your damage, so Deadly Aspects, [[talent:hunter:Ranged Weapon Specialization]] and Improved Tracking (5% against the creature type you track) pay off. **Lone Wolf** adds 20% to everything you deal; a pet would have to deal a fifth of your damage to beat it, and an untalented pet no longer does. [[talent:hunter:Mortal Shots]] adds 30% to the crit damage of your ranged abilities, worth more than [[talent:hunter:Surefooted]]'s 3% hit, which would cost eight points with the Survivalist points it needs; three ranks of Survivalist add 6% Health.

**Single target:**

1. Hunter's Mark, then Serpent Sting, which also starts Rapid Recuperation.
2. Aimed Shot on cooldown, or Multi-Shot on two or more targets.
3. [[talent:hunter:Sniper Shot]] when you have mana to spare. Its 4-second cast fits between Aimed Shots.
4. Arcane Shot only while you move.
5. Rapid Fire on cooldown, every 3 minutes with [[talent:hunter:Rapid Killing]].

## Survival

[[build:hunter-survival-raid]]

Survival fights in melee from [[ability:hunter:Aspect of the Beast]] (110 melee Attack Power at 60). Hunter's Mark and Trueshot Aura only raise Ranged Attack Power, but Expose Prey needs Hunter's Mark on the target. Turn on the Auto Attack/Auto Shot setting to switch between shooting and swinging.

**Single target:**

1. Hunter's Mark and Serpent Sting before you close in.
2. Keep Lacerate ticking: 406 damage over 21 seconds at rank 4.
3. Mongoose Bite whenever it lights up. Lacerating Strikes always overwrites, so each Bite replaces the bleed; with a Bite every 8 seconds it adds about 15% to Bite's damage.
4. Raptor Strike and Strider Kick on cooldown.
5. Immolation Trap at your feet whenever traps are ready: every 18 seconds with [[talent:hunter:Survivalist's Discipline]], 30% stronger with [[talent:hunter:Clever Traps]].

[[talent:hunter:Predator's Edge]] adds 30% crit damage to melee abilities, [[talent:hunter:Lightning Reflexes]] 15% Agility, and Resourcefulness cuts melee and trap costs by 60%. A slow two-hander hits hardest, since Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite and [[talent:hunter:Strider Kick]] use weapon damage; dual-wield, with Predator's Edge's 50% off-hand bonus, once you have hit to spare.

## PvP

Auto Shot stops while you move, so your instants and pet do the work while you kite.

Marksmanship is a really strong PvP pick: [[talent:hunter:Scatter Shot]] needs 21 Marksmanship points, so it sits beside Marksmanship's capstone, and the November 2 changes went into the shots it bursts with.

[[build:hunter-marksmanship-pvp]]

Open with Sniper Shot or Aimed Shot, keep Viper Sting on healers and Serpent Sting on everyone else, and fire Arcane Shot as you kite. When a melee player reaches you: Concussive Shot or Wing Clip, Scatter Shot, then a Freezing Trap where they will run at you.

[[build:hunter-beast-mastery-pvp]]

Beast Mastery keeps up pressure on the move, and [[talent:hunter:Intimidation]] stuns for 3 seconds. It has no Scatter Shot, and its damage drops while the pet is controlled or dead.

[[build:hunter-survival-pvp]]

Survival has no dead zone: it shoots enemies as they come and fights them when they arrive, but it has to reach its target, which kiting casters make hard.

### Trap play

- One trap every 30 seconds (18 with Survivalist's Discipline), but a Fire and a Frost trap can be out together: lay a Freezing Trap on a flag or node before the fight and you can still drop a Fire trap later.
- [[ability:hunter:Freezing Trap]] holds a target for up to 20 seconds and breaks on damage, so keep stings and your pet off it. [[ability:hunter:Frost Trap]] leaves a 60% slow on the ground for 30 seconds. Clever Traps makes both last 30% longer.

### Key cooldowns

- [[talent:hunter:Deterrence]]: 25% dodge and parry for 10 seconds, the answer to a rogue or warrior opener.
- Scatter Shot (Marksmanship, 30 seconds) or Intimidation (Beast Mastery, 1 minute) is your stop: use it to land a trap or to finish a cast.
- [[ability:hunter:Feign Death]] makes enemies drop you as a target.

### Against the common threats

- **Rogues and Feral druids:** Hunter's Mark keeps its target visible in stealth, and [[ability:hunter:Flare]] and Track Hidden find the rest. Deterrence through the opener, then Scatter Shot and trap.
- **Warriors:** kite with Concussive Shot, Wing Clip and Frost Trap, and let a Bird of Prey's Mine! disarm them.
- **Casters and healers:** [[ability:hunter:Viper Sting]] drains their mana every 9 seconds with Improved Stings, Sonic Blast slows casts, Dismember cuts healing and Scatter Shot interrupts. Bestial Wrath makes your pet immune to Fear.
- **Hunters:** Hawk Eye and Sniper Shot's 45 yards win the range fight.

## Stat priorities

Your shots stop missing a raid boss at 8% hit and players at 5%. [[talent:hunter:Surefooted]] covers 3%. Survival's melee attacks share those caps unless you dual-wield, when auto attacks need about 27% against a boss. Expertise, which lowers dodge and parry, only helps Survival's melee: 6.5% removes a boss's dodges from behind. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Beast Mastery | Hit, Agility, Intellect, Critical Strike, Attack Power |
| Marksmanship | Hit, Agility and Intellect, Attack Power, Critical Strike |
| Survival | Hit, Expertise, Agility, Critical Strike, Attack Power, Intellect |

Careful Aim turns each point of Intellect into 1 Attack Power, and its 15 mana pays for more shots, so at 60 Intellect is worth about as much as Agility. Crit counts for more in Beast Mastery because your pet inherits it. Survival needs little mana, so Intellect is only Attack Power there.

## Leveling

Level as Beast Mastery. The pet tanks and deals a large share of the damage, so you spend the least time drinking, mending and reviving.

[[build:hunter-beast-mastery-leveling]]

- Pick a pet with good armor or health, and pull with Hunter's Mark and Serpent Sting so the pet takes aggro. From 25 keep two hawks out and use Aimed Shot as your other shot.
- [[talent:hunter:Deadly Aspects]] comes first, 10 to 14: Auto Shot costs no mana and fires 30% faster for 12 sec on a proc. Then [[talent:hunter:Improved Revive Pet]], so a dead pet comes back 6 sec faster, and [[talent:hunter:Pathfinding]] at 20 and 21, which adds 6% to Aspect of the Cheetah.
- Volley no longer triggers procs and stops when you move, since November 11.
- With all five ranks of the Talented Legacy perk, Summon Hawk arrives at 20 and Bestial Wrath at 35. Tame Beast no longer works on beasts above your level.
- Marksmanship levels well too: from 34, Rapid Recuperation keeps all your regeneration running while you shoot after each kill.

## Races

**PvE, best first.** Beast Mastery wants an Orc or a Human, Marksmanship and Survival a Skyborne.

1. **Skyborne:** [[racial:Wind Blessed]] adds 2% haste, so 2% more Auto Shots, and [[racial:Elemental Insight]] 5% damage against Elementals.
2. **Orc:** [[racial:Command]] adds 5% pet damage, and [[racial:Blood Fury]] 10% Attack Power every 2 minutes, with Bestial Wrath.
3. **Human:** [[racial:Ruthless]] adds 1% crit for you and your pet, [[racial:The Human Spirit]] 10% Spirit.
4. **Dwarf:** [[racial:Artisan's Precision]] adds 1% hit, about 1% damage until the hit cap, and [[racial:Big Game Hunter]] 5% against Beasts.
5. **Troll:** [[racial:Berserking]] adds 10% attack speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes, and [[racial:Beast Slaying]] 5% against Beasts.
6. **Night Elf:** [[racial:Elune's Light]] adds 10% crit for 15 seconds every 3 minutes.
7. **Tauren:** nothing for damage; [[racial:Endurance]] adds 5% Health.

**PvP, best first,** for every spec:

1. **Human:** [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks every stun, the best answer to rogues and warriors, and [[racial:Perception]] spots them first.
2. **Orc:** [[racial:Hardiness]] shortens stuns by 20%, Blood Fury lines up with your burst, and [[racial:Shatter Curse]] clears Curse of Exhaustion.
3. **Night Elf:** Elune's Light adds burst, [[racial:Shadowmeld]] sets up ambushes, and [[racial:Quickness]] adds 2% speed.
4. **Tauren:** [[racial:War Stomp]] stuns up to five enemies within 8 yards for 2 seconds, time to leave the dead zone.
5. **Dwarf:** [[racial:Stoneform]] clears poisons and bleeds, Crippling Poison included.
6. **Troll:** Berserking adds burst, and [[racial:Rapid Regeneration]] restores half your health if a trap buys you 6 seconds.
7. **Skyborne:** Windshapers get [[racial:Skysight]] for speed; the High Order bring Wind Blessed, regeneration from [[racial:Read Leyline]] and [[racial:Walk on Air]], a glide that doubles as an escape.
