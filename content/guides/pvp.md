Forever keeps the war in Warcraft. Factions stay separate on every ruleset, the honor ranks are back, and a new fifteen-player battleground joins Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley. PvP is also one of the biggest sources of Legacy points: twelve of the sixty-five challenges come from it.

## Choosing where to fight

- **PvP ruleset:** every trip into contested territory is dangerous. You can only create characters for one faction on it, and Blizzard can temporarily limit character creation for a faction to keep populations closer together, as it did in Season of Discovery.
- **Roleplay-PvP ruleset:** the same open danger, with Roleplaying's naming and behavior rules. Blizzard added it for launch on October 22.
- **Normal and Roleplaying rulesets:** you choose when to fight. Battlegrounds are open to you either way.
- **Battlegrounds mix rulesets.** Players from every launch ruleset meet in the same battlegrounds. Hardcore will stay separate when it arrives.

See the [rulesets guide](/guides/rulesets) for the full rules.

## Darkspear Islands

[Darkspear Islands](/dungeons/darkspear-islands) is a new fifteen-against-fifteen battleground for levels 30 to 60, off the coast of Dustwallow Marsh. The Darkspear Raiders fight the Theramore Expeditionary Force, and the map mixes Eye of the Storm's central flag with Arathi Basin's capture points: holding more of the four points scores faster, and carrying the flag back to a base you hold scores extra. The first team to 2,000 points wins.

Queue from a battlemaster in any capital or the Barrens, or at the faction bases: the Darkspear Raiders at the Merchant Coast and the Theramore Expeditionary Force at Northwatch.

**Reputation and rewards.** Each side has its own reputation: the Theramore Expeditionary Force for the Alliance and the Darkspear Raiders for the Horde. Vendors at their bases sell trinkets, rings, cloaks, weapons and armor as your standing rises, the reputation quests reward Marks of Honor, and a short questline at Exalted earns the faction's tabard.

**What wins it:** hold more nodes than the enemy, then send a small, fast group for the flag. Dropping the flag no longer gives you the Recently Dropped Flag debuff, so you can pick it up again at once. Classes with speed and escapes carry flags best: druids in Travel Form, shamans in Ghost Wolf, rogues with Sprint, and anyone with the Windshaper Skyborne's Skysight.

## The classic three

- **[Warsong Gulch](/dungeons/warsong-gulch):** ten against ten, capture the flag.
- **[Arathi Basin](/dungeons/arathi-basin):** fifteen against fifteen, five resource nodes.
- **[Alterac Valley](/dungeons/alterac-valley):** forty against forty in the snow.

## Ranks, reputation and the season

The Legacy challenges confirm that honor ranks run up to 14 again. They also mention four battleground reputations to take to Exalted, and a **Field of Honor PvP Season's Journey** with weekly quests: the quests for weeks 4, 7 and 10 each award a Legacy point.

**Honor, marks and crits.** You can hold up to 25,000 honor, because PvP gear and its upgrade tokens cost about half as much again as they did early in the beta. Battleground Marks of Honor stack to 30. Critical strikes against players hit as hard as they do against creatures: Forever has no PvP reduction on them and no resilience. Spells stop missing players at 4% hit and attacks at 5%; the [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| PvP Legacy challenges | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Reach honor ranks 3, 7, 10, 13 and 14 | 5 |
| Reach Exalted with each battleground reputation | 4 |
| Complete the week 4, 7 and 10 quests of the Field of Honor season | 3 |

Because PvP challenges cannot be completed in Hardcore, Hardcore will get its own set of challenges when it launches. The Legacy perk **For Great Honor** increases the honor you earn, and **High Alert** improves stealth detection outside battlegrounds.

## Class tools that shine in PvP

- **Warriors:** Mortal Strike's healing reduction, [[talent:warrior:Concussion Blow]] and [[talent:warrior:Improved Hamstring]]. Since November 13 Berserker Rage also breaks Sap.
- **Paladins:** [[talent:paladin:Repentance]] and Templar's Bulwark for burst survival (the Bulwark can be dispelled since November 2), and [[talent:paladin:Divine Precision]] shortens silences by up to 20%. Judgement is a ranged attack since November 11: it uses your ranged hit chance and triggers no melee effects. Judgement of Command is the exception since November 13: a melee attack again, with your melee hit and your on-hit effects.
- **Rogues:** [[talent:rogue:Preparation]], [[talent:rogue:Improved Kick]], [[talent:rogue:Dirty Tricks]] for cheaper Sap and Blind.
- **Priests:** [[talent:priest:Silence]], Psychic Scream and Shadowform's 15% Physical damage reduction.
- **Mages:** [[talent:mage:Improved Counterspell]] silences for 4 seconds; Frost's Ice Lance shatters for huge damage.
- **Shamans:** [[talent:shaman:Earthbound]] makes Earthbind Totem root on cast; Grounding and Tremor Totems.
- **Hunters:** [[talent:hunter:Entrapment]] on every trap and Scatter Shot.
- **Warlocks:** Fear, Howl of Terror, Death Coil and [[talent:warlock:Curse of Exhaustion]].
- **Druids:** Feral Charge in bear and cat form, and every shapeshift frees you from roots, snares and Polymorph.

**Stuns diminish.** A stun on a target that was stunned shortly before lasts less, and the target is immune after a few in a row. Since November 13 this works again between players and creatures, both ways: a pet's or a guard's stun on you diminishes, and so do your stuns on them.

## Racials that matter

- **Undead:** [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks fear, charm and sleep.
- **Human:** [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks stuns, and [[racial:Perception]] spots stealthers.
- **Orc:** [[racial:Hardiness]] shortens stuns by 20%, and [[racial:Shatter Curse]] removes curses and Banes and cuts magic damage by 5% for 8 seconds.
- **Gnome:** [[racial:Escape Artist]] breaks roots and snares and grants immunity to them for 3 seconds.
- **Dwarf:** [[racial:Stoneform]] clears bleeds, poisons and diseases and cuts Physical damage by 10%.
- **Tauren:** [[racial:War Stomp]] stuns up to five enemies.
- **Night Elf:** [[racial:Shadowmeld]] and harder-to-spot stealth for rogues and druids.

## Just for fun

The **Replica Ironforge Air Rifle**, a Legacy reward, is a toy that lets you join shootouts with other players. Its shots stun, and it keeps score.
