Forever keeps the road to 60 long and dangerous on purpose: there is no level scaling, no flying and no dungeon finder. What it adds is more than 1,000 new quests by Blizzard's count, four new zones and nine new dungeons spread across the whole climb, so the old bottlenecks in the 30s and 40s are gone.

Level ranges below come from each zone's quests and creatures, and for the new dungeons from Blizzard's own list. Zones overlap, so treat the bands as a guide and move on when the quests turn green.

## Step by step

Grimoire's [step-by-step routes](/tools/leveling) take every race and class to 60 quest by quest: who gives each quest and where they stand, where to do it, who takes it back, the flight paths to learn, the boats to take and when to see your trainer, each with its map coordinates. The class's own quests are on the way too, from druid forms to warlock demons. Start from your race: [Human](/tools/leveling#human), [Dwarf](/tools/leveling#dwarf), [Night Elf](/tools/leveling#night-elf), [Gnome](/tools/leveling#gnome), [Orc](/tools/leveling#orc), [Undead](/tools/leveling#undead), [Tauren](/tools/leveling#tauren) or [Troll](/tools/leveling#troll). The Skyborne have routes too, from their first quest on Zephras Isle.

The [Grimoire add-on](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/grimoire-field-guide) follows the same route in game, your own, from wherever your character stands: an arrow toward the next place (drag it anywhere on your screen) with the distance and the walking time, the objectives counted from your quest log and each step ticked as you do it. Talk to a quest giver and it takes the route's quests and hands in the finished ones (hold Shift to do it yourself), and where the route has you kill your way to the next level, it counts down the experience left and about how many kills that is.

## What makes the climb easier

What helps most, biggest first:

- **Rested experience.** Kills pay double while it lasts, and kills are a big part of what you earn. It builds while you are logged out in an inn or a capital city, so log out there.
- **Dungeons, once each.** Forever pays a dungeon's experience mostly through its quests, and most of that on the first run. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 9 every kill inside a dungeon pays 50% more, and since November 13 a kill pays no one in the group when the enemy is trivial to any one of you. Take all of a dungeon's quests before you go in, run it once with a group at its level, then carry on. The routes suggest a few, all optional.
- **Grouping.** Each player's experience for a lower-level kill depends on their own level, as in Classic Era, so a friend a few levels above you no longer cuts what you earn. If someone in the group is far above the enemy, nobody gets experience from it, and since November 13 that holds in every dungeon too: a high-level friend can no longer pull you through one for experience.
- **Busy zones at launch.** Blizzard raised respawn rates across the world for launch, fastest in the starting zones and slower as you level, and plans to bring the low-level zones back down once the first rush has passed. Since its hotfixes of November 9 fewer players share a crowded zone, fewer still on the PvP ruleset.
- **Food and a bag.** Cooked food gives +5% experience from kills for about 15 minutes, and a Cozy Sleeping Bag up to 3% that survives death. Since November 17 you set the bag down where you choose, like a hunter's trap, a Resting aura shows while you stand in its area, and you can no longer sleep on it mounted.
- **A class that kills without stopping.** A hunter's pet tanks while you shoot, with 115% of its normal health since November 10 (it had 150% by mistake). A Frost mage's slows and freezes keep mobs away. A warlock's demon gets far less Health from the warlock's Stamina since November 2, so a Voidwalker holds a mob for less time; Blizzard gave the demons about 15% more base health back on November 10. On November 2 Blizzard also raised damage across every class and gave higher-level creatures more health to match.

The routes take Forever's own quests, from QuestieDB (traces of November 17): 535 of them are on at least one route, between 166 and 270 on each. Every route has steps in Riverglades (at 41 to 45) and Mount Hyjal (at 55 to 58), and the Skyborne's routes on Zephras Isle from level 1 to 12. No open data gives the size of those zones' maps, so each is routed on its own map: the places are QuestieDB's, and the distances and minutes there are estimates. Only the quests QuestieDB places completely (a giver, a turn-in and a place for every objective) are steps, with what follows them; each zone's note on the route says how many of its quests the route takes and why the others are left out. A quest whose item no open data gives a drop chance for is timed the same way. Shen’dralas has steps on 28 of the 56 routes (at 38 to 44): its quests open through the Gelkis's quests in Desolace, which ask you to be Friendly with that centaur clan, about 50 Magram centaurs killed by Classic's numbers; a route goes that way where it grinds less for it without taking longer, and its note says which it does. Each route's notes give the zones' quest counts and levels.

## 1 to 12: your starting zone

Every race starts where it did in Classic, except the Skyborne, who begin on [Zephras Isle](/world/16593), a floating island in the elemental plane of Skywall. It runs to level 12 for both factions and teaches the race's story before you set foot on Azeroth. The [Skyborne guide](/guides/skyborne) covers it.

In Dun Morogh, the way into The Hall of Thanes lies beneath old Ironforge.

## 10 to 20: the first dungeons

This band is where Forever first feels different. Two new dungeons open in the mid-teens:

- **[The Hall of Thanes](/dungeons/hall-of-thanes)** (13 to 18), the burial vaults of Ironforge's ancient thanes, broken open by Dark Iron looters. Its quests send you in for the head of Durgen Dirgehammer.
- **[Ruins of Lordaeron](/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron)** (15 to 20), the shattered capital above the Undercity. It has quests for both factions.

If you are Horde, The Barrens is still the backbone of this band, and Ragefire Chasm and Wailing Caverns are close by.

## 20 to 30: Wetlands and Dalaran

[Whelgar’s Excavation](/dungeons/whelgars-excavation) (24 to 29) sits at a titan dig site in the Wetlands. Alterac's [City of Dalaran](/dungeons/city-of-dalaran) (28 to 33) is a dungeon in the violet city, its great barrier now down.

Your first Legacy point usually lands at 25, when your first character reaches that level. It opens the [Legacy system](/guides/legacy-system) for the whole account.

## 30 to 45: the new middle

The 30s were Classic's slowest stretch. Forever fills them with two new zones:

- **[Riverglades](/world/16591)** on the eastern coast of the Eastern Kingdoms, with Krol’dok Stronghold (40 to 45), a five-player dungeon entered through a cave on a hill north of Sunnyglade. QuestieDB has 172 quests in Riverglades, most of them for levels 38 to 44.
- **[Shen’dralas](/world/16651)** between Mulgore and Desolace, with a Highborne and centaur storyline. QuestieDB has 35 quests in Shen’dralas, most of them for levels 40 to 43.

Blizzard calls Riverglades a zone for the mid 30s to mid 40s and gives no level for Shen’dralas; the levels above are those of the quests themselves.

Stranglethorn Vale still carries both factions through the 30s, and The Drowned City adds a dungeon off its coast.

## 45 to 60: the long road to Hyjal

From the mid-40s, Tanaris, Feralas and The Hinterlands lead into Un’Goro Crater, Felwood and the Plaguelands, as in Classic. Since Blizzard’s hotfixes of November 10 most quests of level 48 and up pay gold as well as experience. The new endgame zone is **[Mount Hyjal](/world/616)**, on the slopes of the World Tree. It is home to the Hyjal Summit raid and an entrance to the Barrow Deeps. QuestieDB has 145 quests in Mount Hyjal, most of them for levels 56 to 60.

The last three new dungeons fill the gaps: [Alcaz Prison](/dungeons/alcaz-prison) off Dustwallow Marsh, [Blackmaw Hold](/dungeons/blackmaw-hold) in Azshara, and [The Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace) above Un’Goro.

## Things that make leveling faster

- **Use your talent order as a plan.** Every build in the [talent calculator](/talents) has a point order. It shows the level each point unlocks and which talent to take.
- **Leveling builds.** Every class has a leveling build for each spec on the [builds page](/builds), and each says who it is for, the day and game build it was checked on, and which guides to compare it with. The first one listed for each class: Beast Mastery hunters, Protection warriors (Arms for groups), Retribution paladins, Combat rogues, Shadow priests, Enhancement shamans, Frost mages, Affliction warlocks and Feral druids. All 84 builds were checked on November 14 against game build 1.60.1.70938; none takes a pure utility talent before 40 without saying why.
- **Secondary Talents at 40.** Blizzard opens Dual Specialization at level 40, which the game calls Secondary Talents: a second set of talents, so you can keep a leveling build and a dungeon or healing build. You change talents at your class trainer in a major city; Icy Veins and players report 50 gold for the second set, a price Blizzard has not posted. Since November 13 your action bars no longer reset at logout once you have it.
- **Legacy perks.** They add convenience rather than raw power: Well Rested raises your rested experience cap, Thrill of Adventure restores health and mana after kills, Field Medicine shortens Recently Bandaged, Frequent Flier makes flights cheaper and faster, and Talented unlocks talent points five levels early. See the [Legacy planner](/tools/legacy).
- **Camping.** Pitch a camp to share profession stations and give nearby players buffs after a minute at the fire. Placing camping features has a one-hour cooldown, which the Field Guide perk shortens. The [professions guide](/guides/professions) has the details.
- **Class tools.** Warriors have Victory Rush at 20, druids can Revive out of combat from 12, and shamans drop all their totems in one cast from 20.
- **Dungeons pay.** Clearing the final boss in every dungeon of a level band earns a Legacy point. At 60, Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay gives weekly dungeon quests; since November 13 they leave your quest log at the weekly reset.
- **Plan your gear.** The [leveling tool](/tools/leveling) shows the best items you can wear at each level, ranked on their Classic stats.
