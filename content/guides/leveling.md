Forever keeps the road to 60 long and dangerous on purpose: there is no level scaling, no flying and no dungeon finder. What it adds is hundreds of new quests (775 recorded in the beta so far), four new zones and nine new dungeons spread across the whole climb, so the old bottlenecks in the 30s and 40s are gone.

Level ranges below come from each zone's quests and creatures, and for the new dungeons from Blizzard's own list. Zones overlap, so treat the bands as a guide and move on when the quests turn green.

## Step by step

Grimoire's [step-by-step routes](/tools/leveling) take every race and class to 60 quest by quest: who gives each quest and where they stand, where to do it, who takes it back, the flight paths to learn, the boats to take and when to see your trainer, each with its map coordinates. The class's own quests are on the way too, from druid forms to warlock demons. Start from your race: [Human](/tools/leveling#human), [Dwarf](/tools/leveling#dwarf), [Night Elf](/tools/leveling#night-elf), [Gnome](/tools/leveling#gnome), [Orc](/tools/leveling#orc), [Undead](/tools/leveling#undead), [Tauren](/tools/leveling#tauren) or [Troll](/tools/leveling#troll). The Skyborne pick up a route of their class at 12, after Zephras Isle.

The [Grimoire add-on](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/grimoire-field-guide) follows the same route in game, your own, from wherever your character stands: an arrow toward the next place (drag it anywhere on your screen) with the distance and the walking time, the objectives counted from your quest log and each step ticked as you do it. Talk to a quest giver and it takes the route's quests and hands in the finished ones (hold Shift to do it yourself), and where the route has you kill your way to the next level, it counts down the experience left and about how many kills that is.

## 1 to 12: your starting zone

Every race starts where it did in Classic, except the Skyborne, who begin on [Zephras Isle](/world/16593), a floating island in the elemental plane of Skywall. It runs to level 12 for both factions and teaches the race's story before you set foot on Azeroth. The [Skyborne guide](/guides/skyborne) covers it.

In Dun Morogh, the way into The Hall of Thanes lies beneath old Ironforge.

## 10 to 20: the first dungeons

This band is where Forever first feels different. Two new dungeons open in the mid-teens:

- **[The Hall of Thanes](/dungeons/hall-of-thanes)** (13 to 18), the burial vaults of Ironforge's ancient thanes, broken open by Dark Iron looters. Its quests send you in for the head of Durgen Dirgehammer.
- **[Ruins of Lordaeron](/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron)** (15 to 20), the shattered capital above the Undercity. It has quests for both factions.

If you are Horde, The Barrens is still the backbone of this band, and Ragefire Chasm and Wailing Caverns are close by.

## 20 to 30: Wetlands and Dalaran

[Whelgar’s Excavation](/dungeons/whelgars-excavation) (24 to 29) sits at a titan dig site in the Wetlands. Alterac's [City of Dalaran](/dungeons/city-of-dalaran) (28 to 33) is a dungeon in the violet city, its great barrier now down.

Your first Legacy point usually lands at 25, when your first character reaches that level. It opens the [Legacy system](/guides/legacy-system) for the whole account.

## 30 to 45: the new middle

The 30s were Classic's slowest stretch. Forever fills them with two new zones:

- **[Riverglades](/world/16591)** on the eastern coast of the Eastern Kingdoms, with more than 150 quests and the outdoor dungeon Krol’dok Stronghold (40 to 45). There is no instance portal: the ogre fortress is open to everyone in the zone.
- **[Shen’dralas](/world/16651)** between Mulgore and Desolace, with a Highborne and centaur storyline.

Blizzard has not announced the level ranges of either zone yet.

Stranglethorn Vale still carries both factions through the 30s, and The Drowned City adds a dungeon off its coast.

## 45 to 60: the long road to Hyjal

From the mid-40s, Tanaris, Feralas and The Hinterlands lead into Un’Goro Crater, Felwood and the Plaguelands, as in Classic. The new endgame zone is **[Mount Hyjal](/world/616)** (about 55 to 60), on the slopes of the World Tree. It is home to the Hyjal Summit raid and an entrance to the Barrow Deeps.

The last three new dungeons fill the gaps: [Alcaz Prison](/dungeons/alcaz-prison) off Dustwallow Marsh, [Blackmaw Hold](/dungeons/blackmaw-hold) in Azshara, and [The Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace) above Un’Goro.

## Things that make leveling faster

- **Use your talent order as a plan.** Every build in the [talent calculator](/talents) has a point order. It shows the level each point unlocks and which talent to take.
- **Legacy perks.** They add convenience rather than raw power: Well Rested raises your rested experience cap, Thrill of Adventure restores health and mana after kills, Field Medicine shortens Recently Bandaged, Frequent Flier makes flights cheaper and faster, and Talented unlocks talent points five levels early. See the [Legacy planner](/tools/legacy).
- **Camping.** Pitch a camp to share profession stations and give your group buffs. Placing camping features has a one-hour cooldown, which the Field Guide perk shortens.
- **Class tools.** Warriors have Victory Rush at 20, druids can Revive out of combat from 12, and shamans drop all their totems in one cast from 20.
- **Dungeons pay.** Clearing the final boss in every dungeon of a level band earns a Legacy point.
- **Plan your gear.** The [leveling tool](/tools/leveling) shows the best items you can wear at each level, ranked on their Classic stats.
