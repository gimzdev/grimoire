Forever gives each priest tree its own job. Discipline heals by absorbing damage with Power Word: Shield, Penance and Divine Aegis, Holy brings the biggest heals and ends in Prayer of Mending, and Shadowform now halves the cost of every Shadow spell and doubles its critical damage bonus. Gnomes can be priests for the first time. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips; Blizzard's hotfixes since launch have only fixed priest bugs.

## What changed from Classic

- **[[talent:priest:Penance]]** heals an ally or hits an enemy instantly and every second for 2 seconds (12 second cooldown).
- **Discipline is built around Power Word: Shield.** [[talent:priest:Soul Warding]] removes its 4 second cooldown, [[talent:priest:Renewed Hope]] adds crit to heals on targets with Weakened Soul and shortens it, and [[talent:priest:Divine Aegis]] turns non-periodic crit heals into shields. A shield can now replace one of equal or lower rank.
- **Holy ends in [[talent:priest:Prayer of Mending]]**, a heal that jumps to the next player who takes damage. [[talent:priest:Litany of Light]] refunds mana when you alternate heals, and Spirit of Redemption lasts 15 seconds.
- **Binding Heal is gone.** Its talent slot holds [[talent:priest:Holy Binding]]: Flash Heal on someone else heals you for 50% as much.
- **Renew and Devouring Plague can crit.**
- **Shadowform** adds 10% Shadow damage, halves Shadow spell costs, doubles their crit damage bonus and cuts Physical damage taken by 15%, but blocks healing spells. Curing a disease no longer cancels it, and since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 9 neither does casting Fear Ward.
- **Shadow Weaving is personal** (2% more Shadow damage from you per stack, up to five). **Vampiric Embrace** lasts 30 seconds on a 30 second cooldown, cannot be resisted, and no longer crits.
- **Shadow Word: Death** (18 second cooldown) now costs you 30% of your maximum health when the target survives, and nothing when it misses.
- **Devouring Plague and Fear Ward are no longer race spells.** Devouring Plague has a 24 second cooldown; [[talent:priest:Devouring Contagion]] halves its cost and spreads it when the target dies (not from critters).
- **Wands** no longer use spell damage and need half a second standing still. Talent procs no longer lose chance on low spell ranks.
- **Removed:** Unbreakable Will, Improved Power Word: Fortitude, Force of Will, Improved Prayer of Healing, Lightwell, Shadow Affinity, Improved Fade and Improved Vampiric Embrace. Healing Focus is now [[talent:priest:Twilight Focus]] and covers every spell.

## Discipline

[[build:priest-discipline-heal]]

Discipline heals by preventing damage.

**Priority:**

1. [[ability:priest:Power Word: Shield]] whoever is about to take damage, starting with the tank. With Soul Warding only Weakened Soul limits it.
2. Penance on cooldown, on the tank or the most injured player.
3. Greater Heal on shielded targets: Renewed Hope adds 10% crit and takes 5 seconds off Weakened Soul per heal. Each crit leaves a Divine Aegis shield and [[talent:priest:Inspiration]]'s 25% armor.
4. Renew for steady damage, Flash Heal for emergencies, Prayer of Healing for group damage.
5. [[talent:priest:Inner Focus]] before a Greater Heal: free, with 25% more crit.
6. [[talent:priest:Power Infusion]] (20% more spell damage and healing for 15 seconds) on another healer or your best caster during a burn.

**Why these talents:** Power Word: Shield never overheals, is instant and stops pushback, though Penance heals more per point of mana: [[talent:priest:Improved Power Word: Shield]] adds 20% (928 becomes about 1,114 at rank 10), and Soul Warding and [[talent:priest:Mental Agility]] take 15% and 10% off. Penance heals 425 three times for 385 mana at rank 4, and [[talent:priest:Improved Healing]] cuts that by 15%. [[talent:priest:Holy Specialization]] feeds Divine Aegis, [[talent:priest:Divine Fury]] cuts Greater Heal to 2.5 seconds, and [[talent:priest:Meditation]] keeps 50% of your regeneration while you cast.

## Holy

[[build:priest-holy-heal]]

Holy is a really strong PvE pick: big heals and strong group healing.

**Priority:**

1. Keep [[talent:priest:Prayer of Mending]] bouncing: recast it on the tank whenever it is ready.
2. Renew on the tank and anyone taking steady damage.
3. Greater Heal on the tank. Lower ranks save mana on smaller hits, but ranks far below your level get less from your healing power.
4. Flash Heal for emergencies; Holy Binding heals you for half of each one you cast on someone else.
5. Prayer of Healing for group damage, [[talent:priest:Holy Nova]] when your group is stacked (it causes no threat).
6. Inner Focus with Greater Heal or Prayer of Healing.

**Why these talents:** [[talent:priest:Spiritual Healing]] adds 10% to every heal and [[talent:priest:Spiritual Guidance]] turns 25% of your Spirit into healing power. Litany of Light refunds 10% of a heal's base cost when it differs from the last one, so alternate Greater Heal, Renew and Prayer of Mending. Improved Healing and Divine Fury make Greater Heal cheaper and faster, and Inspiration gives your tank 25% more armor after a crit heal.

## Shadow

[[build:priest-shadow-raid]]

Shadow is the priest's DPS spec: damage over time, a big nuke and a party heal. [[talent:priest:Shadowform]] halves the cost of every Shadow spell and makes crits deal double damage.

**Single target:**

1. Shadowform, then [[talent:priest:Vampiric Embrace]] on the target: your party heals for 10% of your Shadow damage and you for twice that, by Blizzard's hotfix notes of November 16 for the weekly maintenance of November 17 (it was 20% for everyone, and the client's files still said so on November 18). Recast it every 30 seconds.
2. Shadow Word: Pain, 24 seconds long with [[talent:priest:Improved Shadow Word: Pain]].
3. Devouring Plague on cooldown (24 seconds).
4. Mind Blast on cooldown (6 seconds with four points of [[talent:priest:Improved Mind Blast]], room for two Mind Flays) while your mana allows.
5. Mind Flay as the filler. Let each channel finish: with [[talent:priest:Improved Mind Flay]] every tick hits 20% harder than the last, and a full channel triggers [[talent:priest:Dark Inspiration]].
6. Shadow Word: Death only when you can spare 30% of your health: on a boss it rarely kills, so you take the backlash. It is instant, so use it while moving.

**Mana is the limit.** Even at half cost, this rotation spends more mana than you regenerate, so when mana runs low drop Mind Blast before Mind Flay, which does nearly twice the damage per mana. [[talent:priest:Shadow Weaving]] and [[talent:priest:Darkness]] add 10% each, and [[talent:priest:Twin Disciplines]] adds 5% to everything here but Mind Blast. Blessing of Salvation is gone in Forever, so Fade if you pull threat. [[talent:priest:Silence]] is your interrupt.

## Stat priorities

Spells stop missing a raid boss at 17% hit and players at 4%. [[talent:priest:Shadow Focus]] gives 5% to Shadow spells, so the Shadow raid build takes all five ranks and the PvP build four. Heals never miss, and healing power also adds a third as much spell damage. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Discipline | Healing power, Intellect, Critical Strike, Spirit, mana per 5 seconds |
| Holy | Healing power, Spirit, Intellect, mana per 5 seconds, Critical Strike |
| Shadow | Hit up to the cap, spell damage, Critical Strike, Intellect, Spirit |
| PvP | Stamina, then your spec's first two |

Critical Strike is worth more to Shadow than in Classic because crits deal double damage in Shadowform, and Discipline values it for Divine Aegis and Inspiration (Renewed Hope adds crit of its own). Compare gear in the [item database](/items?class=priest).

## PvP

Each spec has a PvP build for a different role, all with a Discipline core: [[talent:priest:Silent Resolve]] (15% shorter stuns, fears and silences), [[talent:priest:Martyrdom]] (no pushback and 20% interrupt resistance after a melee or ranged crit) and [[talent:priest:Improved Inner Fire]] (45% more armor, 12 more charges). Crits deal full damage in PvP.

[[build:priest-discipline-pvp]]

**Discipline** keeps a team alive and is the hardest priest to kill, but deals little damage.

1. Shield whoever is being hit; each Flash Heal or Penance on a shielded player takes 5 seconds off Weakened Soul.
2. Penance for burst, Flash Heal for the rest: Holy Binding heals you for half of each one on a teammate.
3. When your team is safe, Mana Burn a healer: 2 seconds with [[talent:priest:Improved Mana Burn]], up to 788 mana.
4. Power Infusion on yourself when focused, or on a teammate for a kill.

[[build:priest-shadow-pvp]]

**Shadow** brings the most damage and control: Silence, Psychic Scream every 26 seconds and [[talent:priest:Blackout]] stuns. Shadowform blocks heals, so you rely on Power Word: Shield, Vampiric Embrace and Devouring Plague.

1. Shadow Word: Pain, Devouring Plague and Vampiric Embrace, then Mind Blast; Mind Flay's 50% slow keeps melee off you.
2. Silence a healer as a big cast starts, and Psychic Scream when melee reach you.
3. Finish with Shadow Word: Death below 20% ([[talent:priest:Early Demise]] adds 30% crit), only when it will kill.

[[build:priest-holy-pvp]]

**Holy** heals the most and keeps healing after death: [[talent:priest:Spirit of Redemption]] gives you 15 seconds of free spells. It has fewer answers to focused melee than Discipline.

1. Prayer of Mending on cooldown, Renew and Holy Nova on the move.
2. Holy Nova heals instantly, and its damage knocks stealthed enemies within 10 yards out of stealth.
3. [[talent:priest:Blessed Recovery]], [[talent:priest:Spell Warding]] and Inspiration soften burst.

**Cooldowns to track:** Psychic Scream (30 seconds, 26 with the talent), Silence (45 seconds), Fear Ward, Inner Focus and Power Infusion (3 minutes) and your race spells.

**Against the common threats:**

- **Rogues:** keep Inner Fire up, shield when opened on, and Psychic Scream when the stun ends. Holy Nova and the Human [[racial:Perception]] find them.
- **Warriors:** kite with Mind Flay and Psychic Scream; shield through Mortal Strike, which halves healing.
- **Mages:** dispel Frost Nova from yourself and Polymorph from teammates, then Silence or Mana Burn them.
- **Warlocks:** Fear Ward before you engage, dispel Fear from teammates, and Silence the caster.
- **Paladins:** Mana Burn them and dispel Blessing of Freedom or Templar's Bulwark (dispellable since November 2). Divine Precision shortens your Silence on a Holy paladin by up to 20%.

## Leveling

Level as Shadow: Wand Specialization comes first for the wand that finishes your mobs, Spirit Tap and Vampiric Embrace keep downtime short, Mind Flay's slow keeps mobs off you, and Shadowform arrives at 42 with no respec. Another road is Penance at 30 and a respec to Shadow at 40: the Discipline leveling build is its first part.

[[build:priest-shadow-leveling]]

- Shield yourself, open with Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Pain, and finish with the wand, or with Mind Flay.
- Pull the next mob while [[talent:priest:Spirit Tap]] is up: for 15 seconds your Spirit is doubled and half your regeneration runs while you cast. A mob that dies with Vampiric Embrace on also triggers it.
- From 32, Shadow Word: Death finishes a mob outright. Only cast it when it will kill.
- With the Talented Legacy perk at five ranks, Shadowform comes at 37. Since November 9 casting Fear Ward no longer ends it.

Discipline levels slower but heals dungeons from 30 with Penance. Holy is a dungeon healer that reaches 60 on the Holy PvE build.

## Races

Every priest race brings two race spells: Humans [[ability:priest:Divine Grace]] and [[ability:priest:Feedback]], Dwarves [[ability:priest:Chastise]] and [[ability:priest:Desperate Prayer]], Night Elves [[ability:priest:Elune's Grace]] and [[ability:priest:Starshards]], Gnomes [[ability:priest:Confounding Flash]] and [[ability:priest:Contingency Plan]], Undead [[ability:priest:Dark Sacrifice]] and [[ability:priest:Touch of Weakness]], and Trolls [[ability:priest:Hex of Weakness]] and [[ability:priest:Shadowguard]]. Humans no longer get Desperate Prayer.

| Role | Strongest first |
| --- | --- |
| Healer, PvE | Human, Undead, Gnome, Night Elf, Troll, Dwarf |
| Shadow, PvE | Undead, Human, Gnome, Dwarf, Night Elf, Troll |
| Healer, PvP | Human, Undead, Dwarf, Gnome, Night Elf, Troll |
| Shadow, PvP | Undead, Human, Gnome, Troll, Dwarf, Night Elf |

- **Human:** [[racial:The Human Spirit]] (+10% Spirit) feeds Spiritual Guidance and Meditation, [[racial:Ruthless]] adds 1% crit, Divine Grace is an emergency heal that clears Weakened Soul, and [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks stuns.
- **Undead:** Dark Sacrifice turns 1,600 health into 1,600 mana plus your Spirit every 10 minutes, best for mana-hungry Shadow, and [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks fear, charm and sleep.
- **Gnome:** [[racial:Expansive Mind]] (+5% mana) and [[racial:Eureka!]] (8 seconds of 10% stronger, 10% cheaper non-periodic spells, shields included) help every spec; [[racial:Escape Artist]] and Confounding Flash help in PvP.
- **Dwarf:** [[racial:Artisan's Precision]] adds 1% hit for Shadow; [[racial:Stoneform]], Desperate Prayer and Chastise's 2 second root make a sturdy PvP healer, but nothing helps PvE healing.
- **Night Elf:** [[racial:Elune's Light]] adds 10% crit for 15 seconds. Starshards costs full mana in Shadowform, and Elune's Grace makes melee and ranged attacks miss more.
- **Troll:** [[racial:Berserking]] adds 10% casting speed for 15 seconds, and Hex of Weakness cuts an enemy's healing by 20%, a PvP tool for Shadow.
