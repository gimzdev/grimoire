World of Warcraft: Forever is a new way to play the original Azeroth. It keeps the level cap at **60 for good**, then grows the world with regular content updates instead of expansions. It launched worldwide on **November 4, 2026** at 3:00 p.m. PST and comes with any World of Warcraft subscription or game time.

If you played Classic, most of the map will feel like home. Almost everything you do on it has changed: more than a thousand new quests, four new zones, nine new dungeons, a new race, rebuilt talent trees for every class and a set of account-wide systems that reward you for exploring the whole game.

## Your first login: Classic or Enhanced

The first time you log in, before you pick a ruleset, the game asks you to choose an **experience preset**. The screen only appears once, and every setting it changes can be adjusted later in Game Menu > Options.

| | Classic | Enhanced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Character models | Original (standard definition) | Updated (high definition) |
| Quest markers and objective areas on the map | Off | On |
| Bags | Separate bags | One bag |
| Transmog | Off | On |
| A and D keys | Turn | Strafe |
| Interface layout | Classic | Centered |

Blizzard suggests Enhanced if you are unsure. Character models are also a choice for each character: any character can use the original or the updated models, and you can switch in the options or at a barbershop. It only changes how characters look.

## Rulesets instead of realms

There is no realm list. You pick a **ruleset**, and each ruleset is one large player ecosystem:

- **Normal** for questing and cooperation, with PvP when you choose it.
- **PvP** for open-world danger. You can only create characters for one faction here.
- **Roleplaying** for players who want to lean into the fantasy, with strict naming and behavior rules.
- **Roleplay-PvP**, added on October 22: the same rules in a world where you are almost always at risk of attack.
- **Hardcore**, where death is permanent, arrives some time after launch.

You can group, run dungeons and raid only with players on the same ruleset, and factions stay separate. Battlegrounds can mix rulesets, except Hardcore. To play under a different ruleset you make a new character there. Every character has two names, and the full name must be unique across your region. The [rulesets guide](/guides/rulesets) covers the details, including how layering works at launch.

## Choosing a race and class

The new race is the **Skyborne**, Highborne exiles who made a pact with the wind spirits. They start on Zephras Isle and choose a faction at character creation: Horde Windshapers can be Shamans, Alliance High Order can be Mages. Six older races also gained a class: Orc Mages, Troll Warlocks, Undead Paladins, Human Hunters, Gnome Priests and Dwarf Shamans. See every combination on the [races page](/races) and the [Skyborne guide](/guides/skyborne).

Classes play differently from Classic. Every talent tree was rebuilt, and many familiar talents moved to the trainer. Open the [talent calculator](/talents) for your class before you commit: the new and reworked talents are marked.

## How leveling works

You still earn one talent point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in total, and each row of a tree opens after five points in that tree. The [leveling guide](/guides/leveling) lays out a zone-by-zone route for both factions, including the new zones:

- **Zephras Isle**, levels 1 to 12, the Skyborne starting zone.
- **Riverglades**, on the eastern coast of the Eastern Kingdoms, home to the level 40 to 45 Krol’dok Stronghold.
- **Shen’dralas**, between Mulgore and Desolace.
- **Mount Hyjal**, for the last levels and the endgame.

New dungeons sit along the way, from [The Hall of Thanes](/dungeons/hall-of-thanes) at 13 to [The Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace) at 58. There are no flying mounts and no level scaling, so zones keep their danger.

## Systems you will meet early

- **Legacy**: account-wide challenges that earn points you spend on perks for each character, plus cosmetic rewards. Most players unlock it when their first character reaches 25. Read the [Legacy guide](/guides/legacy-system) or plan it with the [Legacy planner](/tools/legacy).
- **Camping**: pitch a camp in the wild to share profession stations and give nearby players buffs after a minute at the fire. Placing camping features has a one-hour cooldown. See the [professions guide](/guides/professions).
- **Layering**: busy zones are split into copies of the world. If you join a group or a guild on another copy, a notice gives you time to move when you are ready instead of moving you at once. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 9 fewer players share a crowded zone, fewer still on the PvP ruleset.
- **Transmog**: on with the Enhanced preset and off with Classic; you can turn it on or off either way.
- **Graphics**: real-time shadows, volumetric fog and new water, with a toggle between the original and updated character models.
- **Controller support** is complete since Blizzard's notes of November 2: movement, spells, targeting and every core window work on a gamepad, set up in Edit Mode and the options.
- **Bags** no longer equip themselves. Drag each new bag into a free bag slot yourself.
- **New spells** no longer land on your action bars when you learn them: open the spellbook and place them where you want them.
- **Training** every class ability from 1 to 60 costs between 206 and 207 gold, the same for every class (Blizzard's development notes of November 2). Warlocks pay less at the trainer because they also buy Grimoires for their pets, and most abilities learned at the same level cost the same.
- **Day and night** follow a 24-hour clock. Oceanic players currently share a clock that puts their evenings in Azeroth's night; Blizzard has floated Australian time for their worlds, at the cost of a sudden switch when grouping with players from the Americas, and asked players there for feedback.

## Before your first character

1. Pick a ruleset: you group, trade and raid only with players on the same one, and your full name must be unique across your region.
2. Decide between the Classic and Enhanced presets: the screen appears once, at your first login.
3. Plan your talents in the [calculator](/talents) and save the link. The point order doubles as a leveling plan.
4. Read your [class guide](/guides).
5. Open the [leveling tool](/tools/leveling) to see where to quest and which trainer spells and gear upgrades come at each level.
