Forever's warrior still lives on Rage, stances and plate, but every tree was rebuilt: eleven talents are new, thirty-seven work differently and ten are gone. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips, with Blizzard's hotfixes up to November 17.

## What changed from Classic

- **More Rage.** Critical auto attacks generate 100% more Rage, and since October 8 the Rage you get from damage taken is tuned for the armor of a leveling warrior, so questing warriors get more than in earlier beta builds.
- **Stances.** [[ability:warrior:Defensive Stance]] adds 80% threat, and [[ability:warrior:Thunder Clap]] works in it. Battle Shout now reaches raid members. Paladins lost Salvation, so Arms and Fury carry their full threat.
- **Trainer.** Tactical Mastery keeps 10 Rage through a stance change, and [[talent:warrior:Improved Tactical Mastery]] keeps 15 more. Victory Rush heals you for 10% of your health after a kill.
- **One weapon talent.** [[talent:warrior:Weaponmaster]] replaces the four weapon specializations: axes and polearms gain 5% crit, maces and staves ignore 15% of armor, swords get a 5% chance for an extra attack.
- **Arms:** [[talent:warrior:Bloodthrill]] makes Overpower proc on a target with your Rend, [[talent:warrior:Spearing Strike]] deals 75% weapon damage, and 75% more to Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets, [[talent:warrior:Improved Slam]] makes Slam a 10-second cooldown that never delays your swing, and [[talent:warrior:Deep Wounds]] adds up instead of restarting but ignores attack power.
- **Fury:** Whirlwind strikes with both weapons, [[talent:warrior:Enrage]] can start on any hit you take, [[talent:warrior:Flurry]] gives 30% attack speed in three ranks, and Death Wish raises damage taken by 5% instead of lowering armor. New: [[talent:warrior:Reckless Swings]], [[talent:warrior:Improved Whirlwind]], [[talent:warrior:Gore Drinker]], Furious Precision and Lingering Rage. Raging Blows, new in the beta, left the tree with Blizzard's hotfixes of November 3.
- **Protection:** Rage from blocks ([[talent:warrior:Shield Specialization]]) and from dodges and parries ([[talent:warrior:Master of Defense]]), cheaper attacks ([[talent:warrior:Focused Rage]]), 10% damage with a shield ([[talent:warrior:Bastion]]) and Charge in Defensive Stance ([[talent:warrior:Vanguard]]).
- **Removed:** Axe, Mace, Polearm and Sword Specialization, Toughness, Improved Battle Shout, Improved Cleave, Improved Demoralizing Shout, Improved Shield Block and Improved Taunt.

## Arms

[[build:warrior-arms-raid]]

Arms fights from Battle Stance with one two-hander: 32 points in Arms, plus the Fury talents a two-hander can use.

**Why it works.** Bloodthrill gives every main-hand attack on a target with your Rend a 20% chance to allow Overpower. A 3.6-speed weapon plus Mortal Strike and Slam makes about 0.6 of those attacks a second, so Overpower lights up roughly every 8 seconds: 5 Rage, impossible to dodge, and 50% more crit chance from [[talent:warrior:Improved Overpower]] for [[talent:warrior:Impale]] and Deep Wounds to feed on. Improved Slam takes Slam from four casts a minute to six. Reckless Swings gives up to 20 Rage every 15 seconds (5 on each of your next four swings within 8 seconds), up to four times what [[talent:warrior:Anger Management]] gives. Blizzard's notes of November 2 gave it a cost of 25% of your base health; the game's tooltip names none since the hotfixes of November 3.

**Single target, in order:**

1. Rend whenever it is missing.
2. Overpower whenever it lights up.
3. Mortal Strike on cooldown.
4. Slam on cooldown.
5. Spearing Strike on cooldown against Dragonkin and Giants.
6. Reckless Swings when you are short of Rage.
7. Heroic Strike with spare Rage (12 Rage).

Below 20%, Execute (10 Rage) replaces Slam and Heroic Strike. On two targets, open with [[talent:warrior:Sweeping Strikes]]; since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 10 a swing that finds no second target keeps its charge.

**Weapon.** Axes and polearms are the safe pick, since crit feeds Impale, Deep Wounds and Rage; maces and staves pay off when armor is not already sundered; swords add extra attacks.

## Fury

[[build:warrior-fury-raid]]

Fury dual-wields from Berserker Stance (3% crit, 10% more damage taken). [[talent:warrior:Dual Wield Specialization]] adds 25% off-hand damage and 50% more off-hand Rage, and Furious Precision 10% off-hand hit, since queueing Heroic Strike no longer helps it.

**Why it works.** Flurry makes three swings 30% faster after each crit: at 25% crit that covers about 58% of your swings, at 30% about 66%, more as Bloodthirst and Whirlwind crits refresh it. Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 16, Cleave and Heroic Strike no longer use up its charges, and they activate Unbridled Wrath and Reckless Swings as your other attacks do. Improved Whirlwind means a Whirlwind every 8 seconds with 30% more damage on the closest target, about 60% more Whirlwind damage on one boss. The Arms points add [[talent:warrior:Improved Heroic Strike]], Improved Overpower for the swaps to Battle Stance, Anger Management, Deep Wounds and Impale.

**Single target, in order:**

1. Bloodthirst on cooldown.
2. Whirlwind on cooldown.
3. Reckless Swings when you are short of Rage.
4. Heroic Strike with spare Rage (12 Rage).
5. Below 20%: Bloodthirst and Whirlwind first, Execute (10 Rage) instead of Heroic Strike.

[[talent:warrior:Improved Berserker Rage]] makes Berserker Rage worth 10 Rage. Save Death Wish and [[ability:warrior:Recklessness]] (from 50) for burn phases, once the tank has a lead. Whirlwind hits up to four enemies, so Fury is the stronger pick on packs.

## Protection

[[build:warrior-protection-tank]]

Defensive Stance adds 80% threat and [[talent:warrior:Defiance]] 20% more with a shield. Bastion adds 10% damage and Enrage 10% Physical damage, and Enrage stays up while you are being hit.

**Rage.** Shield Specialization (5 per block) and Master of Defense (5 per dodge or parry) make [[ability:warrior:Shield Block]] free: it costs 10 Rage and blocks up to two attacks, and each block opens Revenge. Focused Rage takes 3 Rage off attacks, and a raid boss's hits bring the rest, so the build skips [[talent:warrior:Improved Sunder Armor]] for 3% parry from [[talent:warrior:Deflection]]: each parry opens Revenge. Reckless Swings covers slow phases.

**Threat on one target:**

1. [[talent:warrior:Shield Slam]] on cooldown: 640 to 670 plus your Block Value at rank 4.
2. Revenge whenever it lights up.
3. Shield Block on cooldown.
4. Thunder Clap to keep the boss attacking 20% slower.
5. Sunder Armor as filler, Heroic Strike with spare Rage.

**Packs:** Thunder Clap (four targets), Demoralizing Shout and Cleave, then Revenge and Sunder Armor across the targets.

**Cooldowns:** [[talent:warrior:Improved Shield Wall]] makes Shield Wall a 4-minute cooldown, [[talent:warrior:Last Stand]] adds 30% of your health for 20 seconds, and [[talent:warrior:Concussion Blow]] stuns adds for 5 seconds. In five-player dungeons the Protection leveling build at 60 fits better: it trades Deflection and the second point of Improved Shield Wall for [[talent:warrior:Improved Bloodrage]], the full [[talent:warrior:Improved Thunder Clap]] and Vanguard.

## PvP

Arms is a really strong PvP pick. [[talent:warrior:Mortal Strike]] cuts healing on its target by 50% for 10 seconds, the only healing reduction warriors have, and every healer class gained new heals in Forever. Frequent Overpower crits add the burst.

[[build:warrior-arms-pvp]]

Charge (21 Rage), Rend, Hamstring, then Mortal Strike on whoever is being healed and Overpower on every proc. [[talent:warrior:Improved Hamstring]] roots for 5 seconds 15% of the time, though proc roots now have diminishing returns, and [[talent:warrior:Piercing Howl]] slows everyone within 10 yards by 50%.

[[build:warrior-fury-pvp]]

Fury moves and heals: Improved Berserker Rage clears snares and roots every 30 seconds, Bloodthirst keeps 10% movement speed up, [[talent:warrior:Improved Intercept]] stuns every 20 seconds, and Gore Drinker heals 3% of your health after each Bloodthirst. It has no healing reduction, so a well-healed target can outlast it.

[[build:warrior-protection-pvp]]

Protection carries flags and holds nodes: Shield Wall every 4 minutes, Last Stand, 15% shorter stuns and fears from [[talent:warrior:Iron Will]], a 3-second silence from [[talent:warrior:Improved Shield Bash]] and Disarm every 40 seconds with [[talent:warrior:Improved Disarm]]. It deals the least damage of the three.

**Stance dancing.** Improved Tactical Mastery keeps 25 Rage through a swap: Battle for Charge, Overpower and Retaliation; Berserker for Intercept, Pummel, Berserker Rage and Recklessness; Defensive for Disarm and Shield Wall. A macro can swap a two-hander for a one-hander and shield in one global cooldown.

**Key cooldowns.** Berserker Rage (immune to fear and incapacitates for 10 seconds, every 30), [[ability:warrior:Intimidating Shout]], [[ability:warrior:Retaliation]], Recklessness, and Victory Rush after every kill.

**Common threats.** Kiting mages and hunters: Intercept, Hamstring and Piercing Howl. Rogues: Overpower their dodges, Disarm their burst. Warlocks and priests: Berserker Rage before the fear lands, Pummel the casts. Druid forms are immune to Disarm.

## Stat priorities

Against a raid boss your special attacks stop missing at 8% hit, and so do auto attacks with a two-hander or a shield. Dual-wield auto attacks need about 27%, so hit keeps paying for Fury. [[talent:warrior:Furious Precision]] adds 10% to the off-hand's. Expertise, which lowers dodge and parry, removes a boss's dodges at 6.5% when you attack from behind. Tanks stop taking crits from raid bosses at 440 Defense; the tank build's Anticipation gives 20 of it. Crit is worth more than in Classic because critical auto attacks generate 100% more Rage. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Arms | Hit, Strength, Critical Strike, Expertise, Agility, Haste |
| Fury | Hit, Expertise, Critical Strike, Strength, Haste, Agility |
| Protection (survival) | Defense, Stamina, Armor, Block Value, Dodge and Parry |
| Protection (threat) | Hit and Expertise, Block Value, Strength, Critical Strike |
| PvP | Stamina, Strength, Critical Strike, Hit |

Shield Slam adds your Block Value to every hit, and Flurry, Deep Wounds and Impale lift crit above Strength for Fury.

## Leveling

Level as Protection if you play alone: a one-hander and a shield, Thunder Clap on two or three mobs at a time, and blocks, dodges and parries that pay your Rage. Method's guide picks it for solo leveling, and Icy Veins' leveling guide of November 4 calls it the fastest warrior spec. Level as Arms with the biggest two-hander you can find if you group: it kills a single mob fastest with a healer behind you. Fury kills fast once you have two good one-handers and heals itself with Gore Drinker after 40.

[[build:warrior-protection-leveling]]

[[build:warrior-arms-leveling]]

- Protection: Charge (from Defensive Stance once Vanguard is in at 25), Thunder Clap, Revenge whenever it lights up (60% stronger from 17) and Shield Block for the Rage. Shield Slam comes at 40.

- Arms: Charge, Rend, then Heroic Strike with spare Rage. From 31, keep Rend up so Bloodthrill can allow Overpower.
- Victory Rush heals you for 10% of your health after each kill, and Anger Management (22) slows Rage loss between pulls by 30%.
- Spearing Strike (25) on Giants and Dragonkin, Sweeping Strikes (30) on two-mob pulls, Improved Slam at 35, Mortal Strike at 40, Enrage from 51.
- With all five ranks of the **Talented** Legacy perk, talent points start at level 5, so everything comes five levels sooner.

## Races

Every race can be a warrior. Ranked by role from the racials alone:

**DPS (Arms and Fury)**

1. **Human:** [[racial:Ruthless]], 1% crit that is always on, and crit is Rage too.
2. **Skyborne (both):** [[racial:Wind Blessed]], 2% haste for more swings and Rage; worth a little more to Fury.
3. **Dwarf:** [[racial:Artisan's Precision]], 1% hit, best while you are below the hit cap.
4. **Orc:** [[racial:Blood Fury]], 10% attack power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes, to stack with Death Wish or Recklessness.
5. **Night Elf:** [[racial:Elune's Light]], 10% crit for 15 seconds every 3 minutes.
6. **Undead:** [[racial:Touch of the Grave]], a drain of up to 5% of your health, at most every 15 seconds.
7. **Troll:** [[racial:Berserking]], 10% attack speed for 15 seconds every 3 minutes.
8. **Gnome:** [[racial:Eureka!]], abilities 10% cheaper and stronger for 8 seconds every 2 minutes.
9. **Tauren:** no damage racial.

**Tank**

1. **Dwarf:** [[racial:Stoneform]] takes 10% off Physical damage for 8 seconds, and 1% hit helps threat.
2. **Tauren:** [[racial:Endurance]] adds 5% health, and [[racial:War Stomp]] stuns a pack.
3. **Night Elf:** [[racial:Quickness]] adds 1% dodge, which Master of Defense turns into Rage.
4. **Undead:** [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks fear, and Touch of the Grave heals you.
5. **Human:** Ruthless adds threat and Rage, and [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks stuns.
6. **Orc:** Blood Fury for a fast threat lead; then Skyborne, Troll and Gnome.

**PvP**

1. **Human:** Will to Survive breaks every stun, [[racial:Perception]] finds stealthed rogues and druids, and Ruthless adds crit.
2. **Orc:** [[racial:Hardiness]] makes every stun 20% shorter, Blood Fury adds burst, and [[racial:Shatter Curse]] removes curses and Banes.
3. **Undead:** Will of the Forsaken breaks fear, charm and sleep every 2 minutes.
4. **Gnome:** [[racial:Escape Artist]] breaks roots and snares and blocks them for 3 seconds, the answer to being kited.
5. **Dwarf:** Stoneform clears poisons, bleeds and diseases and cuts Physical damage by 10%.
6. **Tauren:** War Stomp stuns up to five enemies for 2 seconds.
7. **Night Elf, Troll and Skyborne:** burst from Elune's Light or Berserking, speed from the Windshapers' [[racial:Skysight]], or the High Order's Walk on Air glide.
