The paladin got more new talents in Forever than any other class, plus a melee strike and a real tanking seal. Blizzard's notes of November 2, which also cover the October 30 hotfixes, reshuffled all three trees, moved Righteous Fury to level 10 and removed Salvation. Undead can be paladins for the first time. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips, with Blizzard's hotfixes up to November 17.

## What changed from Classic

- **Holy Strike** is an instant strike: at 60, 50% weapon damage plus 40 to 53 Holy damage every 10 seconds.
- **Righteous Fury** comes at 10 and raises all threat by 33%, with a further 90% on Holy damage. The Legacy perk The Quick and the Dead makes it free after you are resurrected.
- **Seal of Fury** is the tanking seal: Holy damage on every swing, an absorb worth half of it while you wear a shield, and a Judgement that taunts for 3 seconds.
- **Judgement keeps your seal**, so you judge on cooldown and recast the seal only when its 30 seconds run out.
- **Salvation is gone**, with Mass Salvation, Revoke and Blessing of Light. Tanks got more threat instead, so DPS manage their own threat.
- **Holy:** [[talent:paladin:Light's Vigil]] only heals now. [[talent:paladin:Oath of Light]] and [[talent:paladin:Boundless Light]] are new, [[talent:paladin:Holy Power]] raises crit healing instead of crit chance, and [[talent:paladin:Divine Precision]] also shortens silences.
- **Protection:** [[talent:paladin:Templar's Bulwark]] can now be dispelled, [[talent:paladin:Sacred Duty]] also shortens Forbearance, [[talent:paladin:Stoicism]] is new and [[talent:paladin:Shieldsworn]] caps the tree. Divine Intellect moved here.
- **Retribution:** [[talent:paladin:Unsealed Wrath]] caps the tree behind Repentance, [[talent:paladin:Vengeance]] stacks five times behind Seal of Command, Two-Handed Weapon Specialization now raises Holy damage too and cuts threat, Vindication lost its attack power, and [[talent:paladin:Turn Evil]] lets Turn Undead hit Demons.
- **Removed talents:** Blessing of Sanctuary, Sanctity Aura, Lasting Judgement, the aura and blessing improvements, Improved Lay on Hands, Improved Seal of the Crusader, One-Handed Weapon Specialization, Improved Seal of Fury, Instrument of Law, Twist of Light and Voice of Truth.
- Blizzard said before launch that it planned no big tuning before the raids open in December. Its hotfixes since launch have not touched the paladin beyond bug fixes.

## Holy

[[build:paladin-holy-heal]]

Holy is a really strong PvE pick: every group needs a healer, and Holy brings a complete kit.

**Priorities:**

1. Oath of Light on the main tank: 5 minutes, no cooldown, up to 400 more on Holy Light, 115 on Flash of Light and 230 on Holy Shock.
2. [[talent:paladin:Holy Shock]] on cooldown, on someone without your Oath when you can, for Boundless Light's 30% crit. A crit refunds half its base cost through [[talent:paladin:Illumination]] and arms [[talent:paladin:Infusion of Light]].
3. Holy Light on the tank while Infusion of Light is up: the cast drops to 1.5 seconds.
4. Flash of Light as the filler: your cheapest heal, and the most efficient once your gear has healing power.
5. Light's Vigil before damage hits a party, then Holy Shock that player: 684 to 724 healing on each party member for 1,665 mana, so save it for damage that hits most of the party. You can keep one Vigil in each party.
6. [[talent:paladin:Divine Favor]] on a Holy Light for a tank in trouble, [[ability:paladin:Lay on Hands]] as the last resort.

**Why these talents:** almost everything in Holy pays off on critical heals, so [[talent:paladin:Conviction]] from Retribution is the best point outside the tree. Through Illumination its 6% crit refunds about 3% of the mana you spend, about what [[talent:paladin:Divine Intellect]] adds to your pool over a five-minute fight, and the crits heal more on top. Both are in. [[talent:paladin:Reverence]] keeps 30% of your regeneration while casting, [[talent:paladin:Spiritual Focus]] saves 70% of casts from pushback, and [[talent:paladin:Pursuit of Justice]] and [[talent:paladin:Guardian's Favor]] add speed and a 3-minute Blessing of Protection. Never cast Righteous Fury as a healer in PvE: it raises the threat of your heals too.

## Protection

[[build:paladin-protection-tank]]

You build threat with Holy damage, which Righteous Fury multiplies, and you pay for it with blocks.

**Threat priority:**

1. Righteous Fury and Seal of Fury before the pull.
2. [[talent:paladin:Holy Shield]] whenever it is down: 30% more block chance for 10 seconds and 221 Holy damage on each of four blocks at rank 4 (from 60), with 20% extra threat.
3. Holy Strike on cooldown. With Shieldsworn that is every 6 seconds, and each one also deals Holy Shield's damage without using a charge, restores 4% of your maximum mana and keeps [[talent:paladin:Iron Creed]]'s 6% damage reduction up.
4. Judgement every 8 seconds: Judgement of Fury hits for 153 to 167 and taunts for 3 seconds. [[talent:paladin:Swift Judgement]] is a second one on its own 1-minute cooldown, for the opener or a second taunt.
5. Consecration on every pack, and on bosses while your mana holds.

**Why these talents:** Shieldsworn makes Holy Strike your best button, and its spell block lasts as long as the cooldown. [[talent:paladin:Improved Righteous Fury]] and Iron Creed together cut the damage you take by about 14%. [[talent:paladin:Shield Specialization]] restores 4% of your maximum mana per block, once every 3 seconds, and [[talent:paladin:Sanctified Judgement]] returns 120 mana per Judgement with Seal of Fury. That covers your mana, so the build skips Divine Intellect and spends 13 points in Retribution: [[talent:paladin:Deflection]] for 5% parry, Conviction for 6% crit (threat) and the two Judgement talents.

**Cooldowns:** Templar's Bulwark absorbs your maximum health for 8 seconds, every 4 minutes with Sacred Duty. Divine Protection stops you attacking and Divine Shield halves your damage and threat, and all three share Forbearance, so Bulwark is the tank's cooldown.

## Retribution

[[build:paladin-retribution-raid]]

Retribution swings a two-hander under Seal of Command and layers Holy damage on top.

**Single target:**

1. Before the pull, judge the seal your raid assigns you, usually Crusader, Wisdom or Light; your melee hits keep that Judgement up. Then Seal of Command.
2. Judgement every 8 seconds. Judgement of Command doubles against stunned or incapacitated targets, and Sanctified Judgement returns 126 mana each time.
3. Holy Strike on cooldown. [[talent:paladin:Sacred Arbiter]] adds 20% and refreshes your Judgements on the target.
4. Seal swaps when mana allows: three swings under Seal of Command store three copies with Unsealed Wrath. Switch to Seal of Righteousness and your next hit gives each copy its normal chance to fire, then switch back.
5. [[ability:paladin:Hammer of Wrath]] below 20%, instant with [[talent:paladin:Champion of the Light]].
6. [[ability:paladin:Exorcism]] on Undead and Demons, Consecration on packs.

**Why these talents:** Vengeance adds up to 15% after crits, [[talent:paladin:Two-Handed Weapon Specialization]] 6% to Holy and Physical damage with 20% less threat, which matters now that Salvation is gone, and [[talent:paladin:Improved Seals]] 15% to seals and Judgements. [[talent:paladin:Precision]] from Protection gives 3% hit, worth more than further Holy points below the hit cap; its entry fee, Divine Intellect, feeds Champion of the Light (60% of your Intellect as spell damage). [[talent:paladin:Holy Conduit]] makes Hammer of Wrath, Consecration and Exorcism 40% cheaper. [[talent:paladin:Vindication]] lowers the target's attack power by 204; one per raid is enough.

## PvP

Holy is a really strong PvP pick: battlegrounds are won by keeping people alive, and Holy pairs instant and fast heals (Holy Shock, Infusion of Light, Light's Vigil) with plate, Divine Shield, Blessing of Protection and Templar's Bulwark.

[[build:paladin-holy-pvp]]

- Keep Righteous Fury on: threat means nothing against players, and Improved Righteous Fury cuts the damage you take by 9%. The Quick and the Dead makes it free after a resurrection.
- Oath of Light on your flag carrier or whoever takes the most hits; Holy Shock everyone else for Boundless Light's crit.
- Peel with [[ability:paladin:Hammer of Justice]], every 45 seconds with [[talent:paladin:Improved Hammer of Justice]]. Guardian's Favor makes [[ability:paladin:Blessing of Freedom]] last 16 seconds on its 20-second cooldown and puts [[ability:paladin:Blessing of Protection]] on 3 minutes.

**Forbearance:** Divine Shield, Divine Protection, Blessing of Protection and Templar's Bulwark all apply it for 1 minute (50 seconds with Sacred Duty), and none can be cast while it is on you. Plan one per minute.

[[build:paladin-retribution-pvp]]

Retribution brings the burst: stun with Hammer of Justice, then Judgement, Swift Judgement and Holy Strike. Each Judgement of Command on a stunned target deals double damage, and [[talent:paladin:Repentance]] sets up the same on humanoids. With Unsealed Wrath, swing under [[ability:paladin:Seal of Justice]], switch to Seal of Command, and your next hit rolls every stored copy for a 2-second stun. It struggles against kiting; Pursuit of Justice and Blessing of Freedom answer snares.

[[build:paladin-protection-pvp]]

Protection is the flag carrier and node guard: Stoicism, Improved Righteous Fury, Iron Creed and Templar's Bulwark make it the toughest of the three, and [[talent:paladin:Reckoning]] answers every critical hit you take with an extra attack. It deals little damage and wins by surviving.

**Common threats:**

- **Rogues and warriors:** stuns and Mortal Strike. Save Hammer of Justice for when they commit, and use Bulwark or Divine Shield before you drop low. [[ability:paladin:Cleanse]] removes Wound Poison, not Mortal Strike.
- **Warlocks and priests:** fears and silences. Divine Precision cuts silences by 20%; the Retribution and Protection builds also take [[talent:paladin:Unyielding Faith]] for 30% shorter fears, and [[talent:paladin:Turn Evil]], if you swap it in, sends a warlock's demon running.
- **Mages and hunters:** snares and kiting. Blessing of Freedom first, then close the gap.
- **Dispellers:** priests, shamans and Felhunters can strip Templar's Bulwark and your blessings.

## Stat priorities

Against a raid boss your attacks stop missing at 8% hit and your spells at 17%; against players it is 5% and 4%. One Hit stat covers both, so [[talent:paladin:Precision]]'s 3% helps your attacks and your spells alike, and Divine Precision adds 8% for Holy spells on its own. Protection stops taking crits from raid bosses at 440 Defense; the tank build's Anticipation gives 20 of it. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Holy | Intellect, healing power, Critical Strike, mana per 5 seconds |
| Protection | Defense until bosses cannot crit you, Stamina, Armor, Block Value, Hit and Expertise, spell damage |
| Retribution | Hit, Expertise, Strength, Critical Strike, Agility, Intellect, spell damage |
| PvP, any spec | Stamina first, then the spec's own order |

Since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 17, Soulforge Armor, the upgraded dungeon set for tanks, has Spell Power where it had Strength: Blizzard says it is leaning into Spell Power as the stat a Paladin tank deals damage with.

Crit sits high for Holy because Illumination, Infusion of Light and Holy Power all key off critical heals, and Boundless Light makes more of them. Retribution values Intellect more than paladins of the past did: Champion of the Light turns it into spell damage, and seal swapping costs mana.

## Leveling

Level as Retribution. It kills fastest, Holy Strike arrives at 6, Sanctified Judgement starts refunding your seal's mana at 15 (fully at 17) and Pursuit of Justice makes you 15% faster from 20.

[[build:paladin-retribution-leveling]]

- [[ability:paladin:Seal of Righteousness]], then Seal of Command from 22; Judgement on cooldown, Holy Strike, auto-attack. Judgement is a ranged attack since November 11 and uses your ranged hit; Judgement of Command is a melee attack again since November 13.
- Hammer of Justice, or Repentance from 33, then Judgement: Judgement of Command doubles against stunned or incapacitated targets.
- Consecration (20) handles two or three mobs, Exorcism hits Undead and Demons (all satyrs count since November 2), and Hammer of Wrath (44) is instant with Champion of the Light.
- [[ability:paladin:Blessing of Might]] on yourself. Righteous Fury and Seal of Fury with a shield let any spec tank early dungeons.
- Each Unsealed Wrath swap from 40 costs a seal cast: swap when your mana is high.
- Five ranks of the Talented Legacy perk start your talent points at level 5, so every talent above comes five levels sooner.

The Protection leveling build takes Pursuit of Justice first, as Retribution does, then tanks dungeons and rarely runs out of mana once Shield Specialization is in at 33; the Holy one heals them.

## Races

Horde paladins are always Undead; on the Alliance you choose between Human and Dwarf. Best first:

| Protection, PvE | Dwarf, Undead, Human | [[racial:Stoneform]] cuts Physical damage by 10% for 8 seconds and [[racial:Artisan's Precision]] adds 1% hit; [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks boss fears; Ruthless only adds threat. |
| Retribution, PvE | Dwarf, Human, Undead | 1% hit beats 1% crit until you reach the hit cap, then Human edges ahead; Touch of the Grave's drain adds little damage. |
| Holy, PvP | Undead, Human, Dwarf | Will of the Forsaken breaks the fears, charms and sleeps aimed at healers every 2 minutes; [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks stuns and [[racial:Perception]] spots rogues. |
| Retribution, PvP | Human, Undead, Dwarf | Will to Survive keeps you in melee through a stun and Ruthless adds burst; Will of the Forsaken answers fears. |
| Protection, PvP | Human, Undead, Dwarf | A flag carrier takes damage all the time, which can break fears but never stuns; Stoneform helps against warriors and rogues. |
