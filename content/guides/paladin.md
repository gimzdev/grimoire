The paladin got more new toys in Forever than any other class: nineteen new talents, two new trainer abilities you will press all game, and a real tanking seal. Holy Strike gives Retribution and Protection a melee button from level 6, Seal of Fury makes shield tanking work without reckoning tricks, and seal twisting is now an official talent. Undead can be paladins for the first time.

## What changed from Classic

- **Holy Strike** is an instant melee attack that adds Holy damage to a weapon swing. At level 60 it hits for 50% weapon damage plus 97 to 121 Holy damage. Retribution and Protection both build around it.
- **Seal of Fury** adds Holy damage to every swing for 30 seconds. With a shield equipped, each hit also gives you an absorb shield worth half the Holy damage dealt. It is the tanking seal, and Judgement of Fury unleashes it.
- **Blessing of Kings and Consecration are trainer spells.** Every paladin gets both at 20, so no tree pays for them any more.
- **Light's Vigil** caps the Holy tree. Put it on a target and your next Holy Shock on them has no cooldown and heals their whole party, or deals extra damage and refunds mana on an enemy.
- **Templar's Bulwark** gives Protection an absorb equal to 100% of your maximum health for 8 seconds on a four-minute cooldown. It applies Forbearance, so it shares the lockout with Divine Shield.
- **Twist of Light** makes seal twisting a talent: replace one seal with another and your next swing still applies the old seal's effect.
- **Reverence** lets Holy keep 30% of its mana regeneration while casting, something paladins never had.
- **Removed:** Blessing of Sanctuary, Sanctity Aura and Lasting Judgement, the three aura improvements, Improved Blessing of Wisdom and Might, Improved Lay on Hands, Improved Seal of Righteousness and Improved Seal of the Crusader.

## Holy

[[build:paladin-holy-heal]]

Holy is a single-target healer with a burst of party healing on demand. [[talent:paladin:Holy Shock]] at 36 and [[talent:paladin:Light's Vigil]] at 42 are the heart of it.

**How a healing cycle looks:**

1. Keep Light's Vigil on the main tank's group. Only one can be active per party.
2. When the party takes damage, Holy Shock the Vigil target. It costs no cooldown and heals everyone in that party.
3. Holy Shock and Flash of Light crits trigger [[talent:paladin:Infusion of Light]], which takes a full second off your next Holy Light.
4. Fill with Flash of Light for efficient top-ups and Holy Light for the big hits.
5. [[talent:paladin:Divine Favor]] guarantees a crit on your next Flash of Light, Holy Light or Holy Shock. Save it for a tank in trouble.

Mana comes from three places: [[talent:paladin:Illumination]] refunds half the base cost of every critical heal, Reverence keeps some regeneration while casting, and [[talent:paladin:Sanctified Judgement]] in Retribution returns 60% of a seal's cost each time you judge it. [[talent:paladin:Voice of Truth]] makes you immune to silence and interrupts for 6 seconds when a boss starts casting at the healers.

The Retribution points also add [[talent:paladin:Benediction]] for cheaper instants, [[talent:paladin:Vindication]] to lower the attack power of whatever you hit, and [[talent:paladin:Pursuit of Justice]] for 15% movement speed.

## Protection

[[build:paladin-protection-tank]]

Forever's Protection paladin is a shield tank first. You build threat with Holy damage and sustain mana by blocking.

**Threat:** keep Righteous Fury on, then Seal of Fury, Judgement, Holy Strike, and [[talent:paladin:Holy Shield]] whenever it is down. On packs, add Consecration. [[talent:paladin:Iron Creed]] raises Holy Strike's threat by 25% and, while Righteous Fury is active, makes each Holy Strike reduce your damage taken by 10% for 6 seconds. [[talent:paladin:Swift Judgement]] resets the Judgement cooldown and makes the next one free, which is your opener on a fresh pull.

**Mana:** [[talent:paladin:Shield Specialization]] restores 6% of your maximum mana on a block, at most once every 3 seconds, and [[talent:paladin:Improved Seal of Fury]] returns mana whenever a Seal of Fury shield is fully absorbed, more when the attacker is higher level than you. Block chance from Holy Shield and [[talent:paladin:Redoubt]] keeps both running.

**Cooldowns:** [[talent:paladin:Templar's Bulwark]] for planned damage spikes, Divine Protection and Divine Shield when the raid can afford it. [[talent:paladin:Sacred Duty]] takes 60 seconds off all three and adds 4% Stamina.

The Holy points are [[talent:paladin:Divine Strength]], [[talent:paladin:Divine Intellect]] and three points of Reverence for mana during long pulls.

## Retribution

[[build:paladin-retribution-raid]]

Retribution is a two-hander spec that layers Holy damage over weapon swings.

**Single target:**

1. Open by judging the seal your raid wants on the boss, usually Seal of the Crusader, Wisdom or Light. In Forever, Judgement does not consume your seal.
2. Switch to Seal of Command and keep it up.
3. Holy Strike on cooldown. With [[talent:paladin:Sacred Arbiter]] it deals 20% more and refreshes every Judgement on the target, so the debuff from step 1 never falls off.
4. Judgement on cooldown for Seal of Command's burst of Holy damage, doubled against stunned or incapacitated targets.
5. Twist seals right before a slow weapon swing: switch from Seal of Command to Seal of Righteousness and [[talent:paladin:Twist of Light]] lets that swing apply Command's effect as well.
6. Hammer of Wrath once the target is under 20% health. [[talent:paladin:Instrument of Law]] cuts its cast time by a second.
7. Exorcism and Holy Wrath against Undead and Demons, Consecration on packs.

[[talent:paladin:Champion of the Light]] turns 60% of your Intellect into spell damage, and [[talent:paladin:Holy Conduit]] makes Consecration, Holy Wrath, Exorcism and Hammer of Wrath 40% cheaper. Together they make the spell side of the kit worth casting. [[talent:paladin:Vengeance]] stacks up to three times after crits, 3% Physical and Holy damage per stack.

In Holy the build takes [[talent:paladin:Improved Seals]] for 15% more seal and judgement damage, plus Voice of Truth and [[talent:paladin:Purifying Power]].

## Stat priorities

Forever converts ratings at 10 Hit Rating per 1% hit and 14 Critical Strike Rating per 1% crit. Blizzard has not published raid hit caps for Forever yet, so these orders assume Classic-like combat against raid bosses and will move once the first raids are logged.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Holy | Intellect, healing power, Critical Strike, Haste, mana per 5 seconds |
| Protection | Stamina, Defense, Block Value, Armor, spell power for threat, Hit |
| Retribution | Hit, Strength, Critical Strike, Intellect, Agility, spell power |

Crit sits high for Holy because Illumination, Infusion of Light and Holy Power all key off critical heals. Retribution values Intellect above most paladin builds of the past because Champion of the Light turns it into spell damage. Build and compare sets in the [gear planner](/tools/gear?class=paladin).

## Leveling

Level as Retribution. Holy Strike arrives at 6, long before any talent matters, and Seal of Command comes at 30.

- Seal of Righteousness or Seal of Command, Judgement, Holy Strike, then auto-attack while Holy Strike recovers.
- Consecration comes to every paladin at 20, so you can take on two or three mobs whichever tree you pick.
- Blessing of Might on yourself, Blessing of Kings on groups.
- Exorcism hits hard against the Undead of Tirisfal, Duskwood and the Plaguelands; Hammer of Wrath finishes runners from 44.
- The Retribution build's order puts Seal of Command at 30, Sacred Arbiter at 33, Repentance at 40 and Twist of Light at 46. The Talented Legacy perk moves each of those five levels earlier.
- Seal of Fury and a shield make low-level dungeons tankable in any spec, and its absorb shields smooth out bad pulls.

## Races

- **Human:** [[racial:Sword Specialization]] gives 2% crit with swords for Retribution, [[racial:The Human Spirit]] adds 5% Spirit for healers, and [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks stuns.
- **Dwarf:** [[racial:Stoneform]] removes bleeds, poisons and diseases and reduces Physical damage by 10% for 8 seconds, a strong tank cooldown. [[racial:Mace Specialization]] adds 1% crit to spells and attacks while you hold a mace, which works for healers too.
- **Undead:** [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks fear, charm and sleep, and [[racial:Touch of the Grave]] drains a little health on hits. The Forsaken paladins tie into the story of Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom.
