Forever adds nine five-player dungeons between level 13 and 60, placed to fill the gaps where Classic left you questing alone. Four are already in the beta; the other five have been announced with their level ranges and locations. They sit alongside the Classic dungeons. The level ranges are the ones Blizzard published.

## The Hall of Thanes

The burial vaults of Ironforge's ancient thanes, broken open by Dark Iron looters and engineers. The restless dead have woken with them.

**Quests:** [[quest:96393]] sends you beneath Old Ironforge for the head of Durgen Dirgehammer, [[quest:96395]] asks you to put the spirit of Faldrim Anvilmar to rest, and [[quest:96403]] has you gather Dwarven Heirlooms in the vaults.

## Ruins of Lordaeron

The shattered throne city above the Undercity, overrun by a necromancer's invasion of banshees, flesh golems and fallen champions.

**Quests:** both factions. Horde players are sent in from the Undercity and Brill with [[quest:92421]], [[quest:92422]], [[quest:95204]] and [[quest:95216]]. Alliance players get theirs in Stormwind: [[quest:92415]] and [[quest:95189]]. [[quest:95250]] and [[quest:92401]] also lead into the ruins.

## Whelgar’s Excavation

A titan dig site in the Wetlands where the Explorers’ League dug too deep. Blizzard's level list calls it Excavation Site: Wetlands.

## City of Dalaran

The violet city with its great barrier down, now a five-player dungeon in Alterac.

## The announced five

- **[The Drowned City](/dungeons/drowned-city)** (35 to 40): sunken troll ruins off the Stranglethorn coast, contested by jungle trolls and naga.
- **[Krol’dok Stronghold](/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold)** (40 to 45): an ogre fortress in the new Riverglades zone, built as an outdoor dungeon with no instance portal. Everyone in the zone can walk in.
- **[Alcaz Prison](/dungeons/alcaz-prison)** (48 to 53): the island prison where King Varian Wrynn was held.
- **[Blackmaw Hold](/dungeons/blackmaw-hold)** (55 to 60): a furbolg stronghold in Azshara whose tribe has fallen to corruption.
- **[The Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace)** (58 to 60): titan structures above Un’Goro Crater, the last new dungeon on the road to 60.

## Legacy points from dungeons

The Legacy system awards a point for defeating the final boss of every dungeon in each of three bands: levels 15 to 25, 26 to 45 and 46 to 60. Completing the Lord Valthalak dungeon set questline is worth one more. See the [Legacy guide](/guides/legacy-system) for the full list.
