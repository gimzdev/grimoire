Forever adds nine five-player dungeons between level 13 and 60, placed to fill the gaps where Classic left you questing alone. All nine opened with the game on November 4, alongside the Classic dungeons. The level ranges are the ones Blizzard published.

Each section below names the bosses that Blizzard’s hotfixes name or two guide sites agree on (Wowhead, Warcraft Tavern, Warcraft Wiki, read on November 14), in the order of a run where two agree on it; a name only one site gives is left out, and each [dungeon page](/dungeons) says which. Their abilities, loot and drop chances are in no open source, and QuestieDB’s traces of November 17 rank no creature inside them as a boss. QuestieDB has the quests of five of them and the entrances of four, below.

## The Hall of Thanes

The burial vaults of Ironforge's ancient thanes, broken open by Dark Iron looters and engineers. The restless dead have woken with them.

**Entrance:** Ironforge, 27.6, 47.8.

**Bosses:** Faldrim Anvilmar, Magmatus, Plunder and Durgen Dirgehammer.

**Quests:** [[quest:96393]] sends you beneath Old Ironforge for the head of Durgen Dirgehammer, [[quest:96395]] asks you to put the spirit of Faldrim Anvilmar to rest, [[quest:96403]] has you gather Dwarven Heirlooms in the vaults, and [[quest:96394]] has you put down its Enraged Apparitions and Tormented Souls.

## Ruins of Lordaeron

The shattered throne city above the Undercity, overrun by a necromancer's invasion of banshees, flesh golems and fallen champions.

**Entrance:** Undercity, 72.4, 11.4.

**Bosses:** Bjork, Rath'mael, The Abandoned, The Baron, Viktor the Vile and Witherfang. The sites agree on these six, not on their order.

**Quests:** both factions. Horde players are sent in from the Undercity with [[quest:92421]] and [[quest:95216]], and from Brill with [[quest:92422]]; [[quest:95204]] starts from the crest itself and is handed in in the Undercity. The Alliance's two start from an item and are handed in in Stormwind: [[quest:92415]] and [[quest:95189]]. [[quest:95250]] and [[quest:92401]] also lead into the ruins.

## Whelgar’s Excavation

A titan dig site in the Wetlands where the Explorers’ League dug too deep. Blizzard's level list calls it Excavation Site: Wetlands.

**Entrance:** Wetlands, 47.8, 56.2.

**Bosses:** Saltspine, Shadetooth and Relic Guardian.

**Quests:** Alliance players get [[quest:95646]] from Rethiel the Greenwarden and [[quest:95647]] from Caitlin Grassman in the Wetlands, and [[quest:95772]] from Dorin Songblade in Redridge Mountains. The Horde gets [[quest:95663]] from Zaruk in Arathi Highlands, which leads to [[quest:95682]], and [[quest:95697]] from Borstan in Orgrimmar. The Titan Relic found inside goes to Ironforge ([[quest:98824]]) or, for the Horde, to Muln Earthfury in Mulgore ([[quest:98823]], given in Thunder Bluff).

## City of Dalaran

The violet city with its great barrier down, now a five-player dungeon in Alterac.

**Entrance:** Alterac Mountains, 8.5, 59.2. The way in is through the city’s sewers, behind a gate the Dalaran Sewer Key opens; the Image of Archmage Modera outside the city gives it, to the Horde after a quest chain from Magus Wordeen Voidglare in Tarren Mill.

**Bosses:** Arcane Anomaly, Arcanic Enigma, Atrexis the Grave Knight, Fel Ancient, Lyn the Ignored, Mana Devourer and Shade of the Archmage, in no order two sites agree on. Both name an eighth, but spell it two ways.

**Quests:** Alliance players get [[quest:92456]] from Shylamiir and [[quest:92489]] from High Sorcerer Andromath in Stormwind, and [[quest:92457]] and [[quest:92458]] from the Image of Archmage Modera near the city. The Horde gets [[quest:96984]] from the Image of Archmage Modera, [[quest:96986]] from Melisara in Tarren Mill and [[quest:96988]] from Doctor Martin Felben in the Undercity.

## The Drowned City

Sunken troll ruins off the Stranglethorn coast, contested by jungle trolls and naga. Levels 35 to 40.

**Entrance:** underwater off the west coast of Stranglethorn Vale, at 21, 28.

**Bosses:** Captain Dreadrise, Deathless Marrow, Min'loth the Serpent, Primeval Elemental, Var'Taka, Zul'Alai, Gill (rare) and Umbra (rare), in no order two sites agree on.

## Krol’dok Stronghold

An ogre fortress on a hill north of Sunnyglade in the new Riverglades zone, held by Krol’dok ogres and Twilight cultists. Levels 40 to 45; a five-player instance.

**Entrance:** on a hill north of Sunnyglade, in the Riverglades.

**Bosses:** Slavecrusher Grox, Twilight Commander Zarmok, War Handler Ashka, Nar'gurk, Bossman Toof and Overlord Mugg.

## Alcaz Prison

The island prison where King Varian Wrynn was held. Levels 48 to 53.

**Entrance:** on Alcaz Island, off the north-east of Dustwallow Marsh.

**Bosses:** Blazeroar, Katerina the Cleaner, Overseer Wrex, The Questioner and Tidemistress Ca'zass, in no order two sites agree on.

## Blackmaw Hold

A furbolg stronghold in Azshara whose tribe has fallen to corruption. Levels 55 to 60.

**Entrance:** through a furbolg tunnel behind the gates in northern Azshara.

**Bosses:** Varrox Bleakhoof. One site lists more; no second source confirms them.

## The Shaper’s Terrace

Titan structures above Un’Goro Crater, the last new dungeon on the road to 60. Levels 58 to 60.

**Entrance:** near Marshal’s Refuge, in Un’Goro Crater.

**Bosses:** Cinder and Snowtalon, named by Blizzard’s hotfixes and by Warcraft Tavern.

**Quests:** Alliance players get [[quest:95924]] and [[quest:95925]] from Curator Thorius in Ironforge; the Horde gets [[quest:95926]] from Master Apothecary Faranell, [[quest:95928]] from Mayena Earthseeker and [[quest:97049]] from Chemist Fuely.

## Since launch

Blizzard's hotfixes of November 5 to 17 changed these, for every dungeon:

- **Experience.** Every kill in a dungeon pays 50% more than at launch (November 9). Most of a dungeon's experience still comes from its quests.
- **No more boosting.** Since November 13 a kill gives no experience to anyone in the group when the enemy is trivial to any one member. Run each dungeon with players at its level.
- **Stuns.** Since November 13 stuns on creatures have diminishing returns again: the same mob stunned twice in a row is stunned for less the second time.
- **[The Drowned City](/dungeons/drowned-city).** Its creatures dealt about 30% more damage than intended; they no longer do. Its instance portal works, and its creatures no longer drop combat on their own, nor do Blackmaw Hold's. Since November 13 its enemies other than bosses come back 2 hours after they die, not 1.
- **The Drowned City, November 16.** Captain Dreadrise can be stunned, the Abyssal Bubble he summons can no longer block, dodge or parry, and the rare "Pyromancer" Braxton fights back when attacked.
- **Upper Blackrock Spire, November 17.** Its enemies had far less health and damage than intended. They are back to what Blizzard meant, and the run takes a full group again.
- **Sunken Temple, November 16.** The serpent statues of the Pit of Refuse no longer lock up when used in the wrong order.
- **[Alcaz Prison](/dungeons/alcaz-prison).** Zarla Ober drops loot.
- **[The Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace).** Vitrix, Snowtalon and Cinder drop loot and higher-level skinning materials, and Cinder appears once you use the Archival Panel.
- **The Deathless Marrow encounter, in The Drowned City.** Its creatures have 25% less health, the Deathless Sorcerer's Fireball and the Deathless Watcher's Charge hit 25% and 50% softer, and the Deathless Guardian casts Shield Slam about half as often.
- **[Ruins of Lordaeron](/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron).** The Abominable Head from The Baron offers [[quest:97288]] to the Horde from level 15 (it was 16).

## Weekly dungeon quests at 60

Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay gives weekly quests that send you into level-60 dungeons; since November 13 they leave your quest log at the weekly reset. Until the raids open on December 9 the same vendor also trades a Blessed Band of Light for a Channeler's Ring (spell power), for good.

## Legacy points from dungeons

The Legacy system awards a point for defeating the final boss of every dungeon in each of three bands: levels 15 to 25, 26 to 45 and 46 to 60. Completing the Lord Valthalak dungeon set questline is worth one more. Since November 13 Balnazzar in Stratholme counts for Master Spelunker. See the [Legacy guide](/guides/legacy-system) for the full list.
