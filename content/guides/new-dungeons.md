Forever adds nine five-player dungeons between level 13 and 60, placed to fill the gaps where Classic left you questing alone. Four have been previewed in full; the other five have been announced with their level ranges and locations. They sit alongside the Classic dungeons.

## The Hall of Thanes

The burial vaults of Ironforge's ancient thanes, broken open by Dark Iron looters and engineers. The restless dead have woken with them. Four bosses: [[npc:261306]], [[npc:261311]], [[npc:261316]] and [[npc:261319]].

**Getting in:** the chain starts with Earthseer Farsen on the hill by Gol’Bolar Quarry, through [[quest:96392]] and [[quest:96390]]. Spies there drop the Old Iron Map that leads to [[quest:96393]]. So far the quests are for the Alliance; Horde players have reported none yet.

**Rewards:** three blue weapons for level 9 and up: [[item:279894]], [[item:279896]] and [[item:279895]].

## Ruins of Lordaeron

The shattered throne city above the Undercity, overrun by a necromancer's invasion of banshees, flesh golems and fallen champions. Six bosses: [[npc:250483]], [[npc:250631]], [[npc:250657]], [[npc:250660]], [[npc:256035]] and [[npc:256097]].

**Quests:** both factions. Horde players get [[quest:92421]] and [[quest:92422]]; Alliance players get [[quest:92415]], [[quest:95189]] and [[quest:95250]].

**Rewards:** [[item:251533]] for strength classes and [[item:251534]] for casters, both blue at level 15.

## Whelgar’s Excavation

A titan dig site in the Wetlands where the Explorers’ League dug too deep. Dragonmaw saboteurs, highland beasts and an ancient relic guardian wait below. Four bosses: [[npc:260322]], [[npc:260325]], Highland Horror and the [[npc:260326]]. It is listed in the game files as Excavation Site: Wetlands.

**Quests:** twelve, split between the factions, with Dragonmaw Rumors, Open the Maw and Songblade Search open to both.

**Rewards:** thirteen blue items at level 24. Highlights include [[item:271767]] for healers, [[item:271766]] for melee and the tanking belt [[item:271768]].

## City of Dalaran

The violet city with its great barrier down. Nine encounters run from an Arcane Anomaly at the gates to a Mana Wraith in the depths. Quest givers include the Kirin Tor, a new reputation.

**Quests:** eight, with Starving Arcane open to both factions and Heart of Disruption on each side.

**Rewards:** sixteen blue items at level 24, the most of any new dungeon so far, including [[item:279841]], [[item:279840]] and [[item:251962]].

## The announced five

- **[The Drowned City](/dungeons/drowned-city)** (35 to 40): sunken troll ruins off the Stranglethorn coast, contested by jungle trolls and naga.
- **[Krol’dok Stronghold](/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold)** (40 to 55): an ogre fortress in the new Riverglades zone, built as an outdoor dungeon with no instance portal. Everyone in the zone can walk in.
- **[Alcaz Prison](/dungeons/alcaz-prison)** (48 to 53): the island prison where King Varian Wrynn was held.
- **[Blackmaw Hold](/dungeons/blackmaw-hold)** (55 to 60): a furbolg stronghold in Azshara whose tribe has fallen to corruption.
- **[The Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace)** (58 to 60): titan structures above Un’Goro Crater, the last new dungeon on the road to 60.

## Legacy points from dungeons

The Legacy system awards a point for defeating the final boss of every dungeon in each of three bands: levels 15 to 25, 26 to 45 and 46 to 60. Completing the Lord Valthalak dungeon set questline is worth one more. See the [Legacy guide](/guides/legacy-system) for the full list.
