All twenty-seven talent trees were rebuilt for Forever. Of the 465 talents, 117 are new and 295 work differently from Classic. Another 83 Classic talents are gone, many of them because their effect became a trainer spell or merged into another talent. The rules have not changed: one point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in total, and each row opens after five points in that tree.

Fourteen of the twenty-seven capstones are new talents. Ten more changed: Bloodthirst now also gives movement speed, Shadowform halves Shadow spell costs and doubles their crit damage, Elemental Mastery speeds up Lightning Bolt and Lava Burst and lifts Chain Lightning's cooldown, Moonkin Form doubles Omen of Clarity procs, and Holy Shield hits harder. Only Mortal Strike, Bestial Wrath and Adrenaline Rush work as you remember them.

## Five themes across every class

**Weapon talents adapt to your weapon.** Warriors lost Axe, Mace, Polearm and Sword Specialization and gained [[talent:warrior:Weaponmaster]]. Rogues lost four weapon specializations and gained [[talent:rogue:Hack and Slash]]. One talent, whatever you wield, so a weapon upgrade never forces a respec.

**Stats convert.** Several talents turn a stat your armor already carries into one your spec needs. Hunters get Attack Power from Intellect through [[talent:hunter:Careful Aim]], Enhancement shamans get both Attack Power and spell power from it through [[talent:shaman:Mental Dexterity]] and [[talent:shaman:Mental Quickness]], and Retribution paladins get spell damage through [[talent:paladin:Champion of the Light]].

**Every caster can regenerate while casting.** Paladins have [[talent:paladin:Reverence]], shamans [[talent:shaman:Mindfulness]], druids [[talent:druid:Reflection]], priests [[talent:priest:Meditation]], mages [[talent:mage:Arcane Meditation]] and hunters [[talent:hunter:Bestial Discipline]].

**Debuffs became personal.** [[talent:priest:Shadow Weaving]], [[talent:mage:Improved Scorch]], [[talent:mage:Winter's Chill]] and [[talent:warlock:Improved Shadow Bolt]] now buff only your own damage. Raids no longer need to stack a class to share its debuff.

**Core spells moved to the trainer.** Blessing of Kings, Consecration, Aimed Shot, Omen of Clarity, Nature's Grasp, Tactical Mastery and Divine Spirit are all trainer spells now, so those trees spend the points on something else. Lightwell and Magic Attunement are gone.

## Hit talents moved around

Several classes got their hit from a new place, which changes which off-tree points are mandatory:

- Mages need [[talent:mage:Elemental Precision]] for Fire and Frost, and [[talent:mage:Arcane Focus]] for Arcane.
- Warlocks get 5% hit to every spell from [[talent:warlock:Suppression]].
- Elemental shamans get theirs from [[talent:shaman:Tidal Focus]] in Restoration.
- Balance druids have [[talent:druid:Nature's Reach]]; Shadow priests [[talent:priest:Shadow Focus]].
- Paladins have [[talent:paladin:Precision]] for melee and [[talent:paladin:Divine Precision]] for Holy spells; Discipline priests have [[talent:priest:Holy Precision]].
- Rogues have [[talent:rogue:Precision]], Survival hunters [[talent:hunter:Surefooted]], and Fury warriors [[talent:warrior:Furious Precision]] for the off-hand.

## Using the calculator

The [talent calculator](/talents) is built for planning as well as theorycrafting:

- **Left-click** adds a point, **right-click** or **Shift-click** removes one. On a phone, tap a talent and use the plus and minus buttons.
- New talents carry a green marker and changed ones a gold marker. Hover any talent to see its current and next rank and, for changed talents, what it did in Classic.
- The **point order** strip under the trees records the order you spent points in. It doubles as a leveling plan and shows the level each point unlocks.
- Turn on **Talented** to model the Legacy perk that unlocks each talent point five levels early.
- **Copy link** saves the whole build, order included, in a URL you can share.

Every class guide links its recommended builds straight into the calculator.
