Every mage tree in Forever has a real rotation. Arcane stacks Arcane Blast and spends it on Arcane Wrath and free Missiles, Fire turns Fire Blast and crits into fast Pyroblasts, and Frost adds Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost and an Ice Barrier that raises Frost damage. Frostfire Bolt joins the trainer, and Orcs and the High Order Skyborne can now be mages. The builds below are worked out from the game's tooltips, with Blizzard's hotfixes up to November 13.

## What changed from Classic

- **Arcane Blast**, the new Arcane talent spell, gives you spell damage equal to 12% of your Intellect per cast and makes the next one cost 100% more, stacking four times for 18 seconds from the first cast. [[talent:mage:Arcane Wrath]], a 3.5-second cast on a 6-second cooldown, also gains 5% of your maximum mana as spell damage.
- **Missile Barrage**, now in the second row: Arcane Blast has a 40% chance, and Arcane Wrath, Fireball, Frostbolt and Frostfire Bolt 20%, to make your next Arcane Missiles free and twice as fast. It is spent when the channel starts and leaves Clearcasting alone.
- **Heating Up:** crits from Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast and Scorch take 25% off your next Pyroblast's cast, up to three stacks. Every Fire Blast adds a stack, two on a crit.
- **Ice Lance** deals 300% more damage to Frozen targets, on an 8-second cooldown shared with Fire Blast. **Fingers of Frost** gives your Chill effects a 15% chance to treat your next two spells as if the target were Frozen, bosses included.
- **Ice Barrier** adds 15% Frost damage for a minute and 5% more while the shield holds; Piercing Ice is gone. [[talent:mage:Frost Warding]] makes Ice Barrier and Frost Ward absorb 20% more, and Frost Ward now negates spells instead of reflecting them.
- **Crit:** [[talent:mage:Shatter]] gives every spell 50% crit against Frozen targets at three ranks, Arcane Mind makes Arcane crits deal double damage, [[talent:mage:Incineration]] gives Fire Blast, Ice Lance, Arcane Blast and Scorch 10% at five ranks, and [[talent:mage:Critical Mass]] is back to three ranks for 6%.
- **Personal debuffs:** [[talent:mage:Improved Scorch]] raises only your own Fire damage, and [[talent:mage:Winter's Chill]] feeds your next Cone of Cold: 20% damage and 7% of your spell damage per stack, up to five. [[talent:mage:Fire Power]] adds 3% per Fire effect of yours on the target, up to 12%.
- **Changed and moved:** [[talent:mage:Precision]] gives 2/4/6% hit with all spells and absorbed Arcane Focus; [[talent:mage:Arcane Resilience]] (armor equal to 75% of Intellect) moved to the first row and [[talent:mage:Improved Fire Blast]] to the third.
- **Low ranks** no longer cut talent proc chances, except Blizzard ranks learned more than 12 levels below you.
- **Renamed:** Improved Channeling (was Improved Arcane Missiles), Arcane Impact (Improved Arcane Explosion), Arcane Shielding (Improved Mana Shield), Incineration (Incinerate), [[talent:mage:Accelerant]] (Improved Fireball, now also for Arcane Wrath) and Precision (Elemental Precision).
- **Removed:** Magic Attunement, Piercing Ice and Arcane Focus. Detect Magic and Frost Blast left the spellbook, and since the hotfixes of November 3 no tooltip names Frost Blast. Blizzard's notes of November 2 cover Piercing Ice, Arcane Focus and Frost Blast, October hotfixes included; Magic Attunement and Detect Magic are simply missing from the client. Blizzard said before launch that it planned no big tuning before the raids open on December 9.

## Arcane

[[build:mage-arcane-raid]]

Arcane trades mana for burst. In this build [[talent:mage:Arcane Blast]] comes at 20, Arcane Wrath at 25 and [[talent:mage:Arcane Power]] at 40.

1. Open each 18-second window with Arcane Blast: four casts while your mana is above half, two below. Each stack adds 12% of your Intellect as spell damage to every spell. Four Blasts cost 150% of your base mana; the fourth alone costs as much as the first and third together.
2. Arcane Wrath on cooldown inside the window: 3.3 seconds with two points of Accelerant, instant with Presence of Mind.
3. Fire Blast on cooldown, every 6 seconds with Improved Fire Blast: each one adds a Heating Up stack, and at three stacks Pyroblast takes 1.5 seconds.
4. Arcane Missiles on every Missile Barrage proc: free, all five missiles in 2.5 seconds.
5. Arcane Missiles to fill. Spend Clearcasting on a Blast at three or four stacks.
6. Arcane Power with a fresh window: 30% more damage for 15 seconds, at 30% more mana.

[[talent:mage:Arcane Mind]] makes Arcane crits deal double damage, and its 10% Intellect feeds the stacks and Arcane Wrath. [[talent:mage:Arcane Subtlety]] cuts Arcane threat by 30%, which matters with Blessing of Salvation gone. [[talent:mage:Arcane Meditation]] keeps 50% of your mana regeneration going while you cast, and [[ability:mage:Mage Armor]] allows another 50%. Incineration adds 10% crit to Arcane Blast.

## Fire

[[build:mage-fire-raid]]

Fire turns crits into speed. In this build [[talent:mage:Pyroblast]] comes at 20, [[talent:mage:Heating Up]] at 25 and [[talent:mage:Combustion]] at 40.

1. Scorch until Improved Scorch has five stacks, 15% more Fire damage from you, and refresh it within 30 seconds.
2. Fire Blast on cooldown, every 6 seconds with Improved Fire Blast: a Heating Up stack per hit, two on a crit.
3. Pyroblast at two or three stacks: a 3-second cast at two, which already beats a Fireball, and 1.5 seconds at three.
4. Fireball as the filler.
5. Combustion on cooldown: each Fire hit adds 10% crit until three crits land.

Fire Power reaches 12% with four of your Fire effects on the target: Improved Scorch, the Fireball and Pyroblast burns, and [[talent:mage:Ignite]]. Improved Fire Blast means a third more guaranteed Heating Up stacks. Fire spends the most mana, so [[talent:mage:Master of Elements]] (30% of a crit's base cost back), Arcane Concentration, Arcane Meditation and Missile Barrage earn their points, and one Arcane Blast before the pull adds 12% of your Intellect as spell damage for 18 seconds. [[talent:mage:Burning Soul]] is the swap if you pull aggro: 30% less Fire threat, and 70% of hits no longer push back your casts. Frostfire Bolt hits for less than Fireball and adds nothing to Fire Power.

## Frost

[[build:mage-frost-raid]]

Frost is a really strong PvE pick. [[talent:mage:Ice Barrier]] adds about 20% Frost damage for one global cooldown every 30 to 60 seconds, and Frostbolt costs 290 mana to Fireball's 410 before [[talent:mage:Frost Channeling]] takes off 15%, so Frost holds its damage through a long fight. Fire can out-damage it in short ones. In this build [[talent:mage:Ice Lance]] comes at 20 and Ice Barrier at 40.

1. Keep Ice Barrier up: recast it when it breaks or before its minute runs out.
2. Frostbolt as the main spell. Each one adds a Winter's Chill stack.
3. On a [[talent:mage:Fingers of Frost]] proc, Ice Lance, then Frostbolt: both count as hitting a Frozen target, so Ice Lance deals 300% more and both get Shatter's 50% crit.
4. Cone of Cold at five stacks when you can stand close: double damage plus 35% of your spell damage, and 35% more from [[talent:mage:Improved Cone of Cold]].
5. Arcane Missiles on every Missile Barrage proc.

[[talent:mage:Ice Shards]] makes Frost crits deal double damage, Frost Channeling cuts Frost threat by 30%, and [[talent:mage:Arctic Reach]] makes Frostbolt, Ice Lance and the cone 20% longer. [[talent:mage:Missile Barrage]] gives each Frostbolt a 20% chance at free Missiles, and [[talent:mage:Arcane Concentration]] makes 10% of your spells free; since Blizzard's hotfixes of November 9 a spell that hits more than once, like Arcane Missiles, splits that chance across its cast and its missiles.

For dungeon trash and AoE farming, the Frost AoE build swaps Winter's Chill, Fingers of Frost, Missile Barrage and Magic Absorption for [[talent:mage:Improved Blizzard]], [[talent:mage:Frostbite]], [[talent:mage:Improved Frost Nova]] and [[talent:mage:Permafrost]]: since November 13 Blizzard's damage breaks Frost Nova's freeze, so the build works by the slow. Frost Nova to get out of melee, top-rank Blizzard where the pack will walk, slowed by 40% (50% with Permafrost), Cone of Cold as it comes out, then step back and Blizzard again.

## PvP

Frost is a really strong PvP pick: it has control, burst and defense at once. Ice Barrier returns every 30 seconds, Cold Snap gives you a second Ice Block, Frost Nova or Ice Lance, and Frostbite and Fingers of Frost set up shatters for free. Arcane and Fire hit harder in a short window and take Ice Block and Shatter from a 20-point Frost dip.

Two ranks of Precision give the 4% hit that stops your spells missing players. Crits do full damage in PvP, Frostbite's freezes now have diminishing returns, and Arcane Missiles checks line of sight only when the channel starts.

[[build:mage-frost-pvp]]

- **Combo:** Frost Nova or a Frostbite freeze, then Frostbolt and Ice Lance before it breaks. Fingers of Frost gives the same shatter without a freeze, and Cone of Cold with Winter's Chill stacks finishes when melee closes in.
- **Cooldowns:** Ice Barrier every 30 seconds, with no pushback while it holds; Frost Nova every 21; [[ability:mage:Blink]] every 15 to break a stun or root; [[ability:mage:Counterspell]] every 30; [[talent:mage:Ice Block]] and [[talent:mage:Cold Snap]] every 5 and 10 minutes.
- **Weak spot:** less burst than Arcane or Fire when nothing is frozen, and Cone of Cold needs melee range.

[[build:mage-arcane-pvp]]

- **Burst:** Arcane Power, Presence of Mind into Arcane Wrath, then Blasts and Missiles. Shatter works on Arcane spells and Arcane Mind makes their crits deal double damage, so Frost Nova into an instant Arcane Wrath is the kill combo.
- **Control:** [[talent:mage:Improved Counterspell]] adds a 4-second silence to Counterspell's 10-second lockout, and Missiles cannot be pushed back.
- **Weak spot:** no Ice Barrier or Cold Snap, and Blasts drain mana fast. Ice Block, Blink and Mana Shield, cheaper with [[talent:mage:Arcane Shielding]], are your defense.

[[build:mage-fire-pvp]]

- **Burst:** Fire Blast reaches 35 yards and builds Heating Up every 6 seconds. Frost Nova, Fire Blast, then a fast Pyroblast that Shatter makes likely to crit; Combustion for the kill.
- **Control:** [[talent:mage:Impact]] stuns on 10% of your Fire hits, [[talent:mage:Blast Wave]] dazes everything near you and Frostfire Bolt slows by 40%.
- **Weak spot:** long casts. Burning Soul stops 70% of pushback, but Pyroblast without Heating Up takes 6 seconds.

**Against common threats:**

- **Rogues:** Ice Barrier up before they open, Blink out of the stun, Frost Nova, then Polymorph or kite.
- **Warriors and hunters:** Frost Nova and Blink before they reach you and keep them slowed. Polymorph a hunter's pet and save Ice Block for Aimed Shot.
- **Warlocks:** Counterspell a Shadow cast to lock out Fear for 10 seconds; Remove Lesser Curse takes off a Curse or Bane. Demons cannot be polymorphed.
- **Healers:** Counterspell the healing school, Holy for priests and paladins, Nature for druids and shamans. Feed Grounding Totem a rank 1 Frostbolt.
- **Mages:** Counterspell their Polymorph or Frostbolt; Frost Warding gives Frost Ward a 20% chance to negate their Frost spells.

## Stat priorities

Spells stop missing a raid boss at 17% hit and players at 4%. Precision gives 6% with every school, so raiders need 11% more from gear and the PvP builds stop at two ranks. The [stats guide](/guides/stats-and-combat) has the details.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Arcane | Hit, spell power, Intellect, Critical Strike, Spirit, Haste |
| Fire | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect |
| Frost | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect |
| PvP, any spec | Stamina, spell power, Critical Strike, Intellect |

Arcane values Intellect more than the other specs: four Blast stacks turn 48% of it into spell damage, Arcane Wrath reads your maximum mana, and the pool pays for the Blasts. Arcane Meditation and Mage Armor keep Spirit working while you cast. With Arcane Mind and Ice Shards a crit deals double damage, and Fire turns crits into Ignite, Heating Up and refunds. Compare gear in the [item database](/items?class=mage).

## Leveling

Level as Frost: it is the safest spec and drinks least, so it is also the fastest.

[[build:mage-frost-leveling]]

- Frostbolt from range. When the mob arrives, Frost Nova, step back, then Ice Lance and Frostbolt while it is frozen. Ice Lance comes at 20 and Shatter at 26-28.
- From 38, Frostbolts stack Winter's Chill: finish with Cone of Cold. From 40, cast Ice Barrier before every pull.
- Rank 1 Frostbolt is a one-second cast with Improved Frostbolt and now procs Frostbite and Fingers of Frost at the full rate: a cheap slow for a second mob.
- For three or more mobs, slow them: Blizzard where they walk and Cone of Cold as they come out. Keep Frost Nova to get out of melee, since Blizzard's damage breaks its freeze since November 13. For steady AoE grinding, add Improved Blizzard as in the Frost AoE build.
- Arcane levels with pushback-proof Missiles and free procs, Fire with the biggest openers; both drink more. All three leveling builds are on the [mage class page](/classes/mage).
- With all five ranks of the Talented Legacy perk every talent comes five levels earlier: Ice Lance at 15, Ice Barrier at 35.

## Races

**PvE, Alliance**

1. **High Order Skyborne:** [[racial:Wind Blessed]] makes every cast 2% faster all fight: best for Fire and Frost.
2. **Human:** [[racial:Ruthless]] adds 1% crit and [[racial:The Human Spirit]] 10% Spirit, which Arcane turns into mana while casting; best for Arcane.
3. **Gnome:** [[racial:Eureka!]] adds 10% damage at 10% less cost for 8 seconds every 2 minutes, and [[racial:Expansive Mind]] 5% mana, which Arcane Wrath reads.

**PvE, Horde**

1. **Troll:** [[racial:Berserking]] adds 10% casting speed for 15 seconds, the strongest damage cooldown of the three.
2. **Orc:** [[racial:Blood Fury]] adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds every 2 minutes.
3. **Undead:** nothing for damage; [[racial:Touch of the Grave]] and [[racial:Cannibalize]] help while leveling.

**PvP, Alliance**

1. **Human:** [[racial:Will to Survive]] breaks a stun while Blink is on cooldown, and [[racial:Perception]] finds rogues before they open.
2. **Gnome:** [[racial:Escape Artist]] clears the snares Blink leaves on you, such as Hamstring and Crippling Poison.
3. **High Order Skyborne:** haste and [[racial:Walk on Air]], but nothing that breaks control.

**PvP, Horde**

1. **Undead:** [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks fear, charm and sleep, and Touch of the Grave heals you mid-fight.
2. **Orc:** [[racial:Hardiness]] shortens stuns by 20%, Blood Fury adds to a burst and [[racial:Shatter Curse]] strips Curses and Banes.
3. **Troll:** Berserking for burst, and [[racial:Rapid Regeneration]] heals 50% of your health behind a Polymorph.
