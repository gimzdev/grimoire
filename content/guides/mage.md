Every mage tree in Forever gets a proper rotation. Arcane stacks Arcane Blast and fires Missile Barrage procs while the bonus lasts, Fire turns crits into faster Pyroblasts with Heating Up, and Frost gets Ice Lance and Fingers of Frost for shatter combos against any target. Frostfire Bolt and Frost Blast join the trainer, Alliance mages get a teleport to Dalaran at 50, and Orcs and the High Order Skyborne can now be mages.

## What changed from Classic

- **Arcane Blast** is the new Arcane talent spell. Each cast gives you spell damage equal to 15% of your Intellect and makes the next Arcane Blast cost 175% more, stacking four times. The stacks last 15 seconds from the first cast.
- **Missile Barrage** is a two-rank talent. At two ranks it gives Arcane Blast a 40% chance and Arcane Wrath, Fireball, Frostbolt, Frost Blast or Frostfire Bolt a 20% chance to make your next Arcane Missiles free, twice as fast and half as long.
- **Heating Up** makes Pyroblast part of the Fire rotation: crits from Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast and Scorch take 25% off your next Pyroblast's cast time, stacking three times. Fire Blast adds a stack every time it hits, so a Fire Blast crit adds two.
- **Ice Lance and Fingers of Frost.** Ice Lance deals 200% more damage to Frozen targets, and Fingers of Frost gives your Chill effects a 15% chance to treat your next two spells as if the target were Frozen, which works on bosses that cannot actually be frozen.
- **Shatter now adds 50% crit** against Frozen targets at three ranks.
- **Improved Scorch and Winter's Chill are personal.** Improved Scorch raises the Fire damage *you* deal to the target, and Winter's Chill now feeds your own Cone of Cold: each stack makes your next one deal 5% more damage to the target and gain another 7% of your spell damage, up to five stacks. Neither buffs other mages any more.
- **Frostfire Bolt** is a trainer spell at 40: Frostfire damage, a 40% slow, and a short damage-over-time effect. [[talent:mage:Improved Fireball]] speeds it up as well.
- **Renamed:** Improved Arcane Missiles is now Improved Channeling, Improved Arcane Explosion is Arcane Impact, Improved Mana Shield is Arcane Shielding and Incinerate is Incineration.
- **Hotfixed in October:** Blizzard's server hotfixes of October 20 and 22 removed Piercing Ice, cut [[talent:mage:Elemental Precision]] from five ranks to three (6% hit at three), gave [[talent:mage:Ice Barrier]] a Frost damage bonus, reworked Winter's Chill, made [[talent:mage:Frost Warding]] strengthen Ice Barrier and Frost Ward, and moved four Frost talents within their rows.
- **Removed:** Magic Attunement and Piercing Ice, and Detect Magic left the spellbook.

## Arcane

[[build:mage-arcane-raid]]

Arcane trades mana for damage. [[talent:mage:Arcane Blast]] arrives at 34, one rank of [[talent:mage:Missile Barrage]] at 38 and [[talent:mage:Arcane Power]] at 50.

**Single target:**

1. Cast Arcane Blast three or four times. Each stack adds spell damage equal to 15% of your Intellect and costs more mana. The stacks last 15 seconds from the first cast, and other spells do not use them up.
2. Spend the rest of those 15 seconds on Arcane Missiles, ideally on a Missile Barrage proc when it is free and fires twice as fast.
3. Start again once the stacks fall off. Drop to fewer Arcane Blasts per cycle when your mana gets low.
4. Arcane Power and Presence of Mind together for burst: 30% more damage for 15 seconds, at 30% more mana.

[[talent:mage:Improved Channeling]] stops damage from interrupting Arcane Missiles and gives Arcane Blast a 70% chance to resist pushback. [[talent:mage:Arcane Mind]] adds 10% Intellect and doubles the critical damage bonus of Arcane spells, and [[talent:mage:Arcane Impact]] adds 6% Arcane crit. Clearcasting from [[talent:mage:Arcane Concentration]] is a free Arcane Blast at four stacks.

The Frost points take [[talent:mage:Frost Warding]], three ranks of Improved Frostbolt, [[talent:mage:Permafrost]] and Improved Frost Nova.

## Fire

[[build:mage-fire-raid]]

Fire is the crit spec. [[talent:mage:Heating Up]] arrives at 28 and [[talent:mage:Combustion]] at 40.

**Single target:**

1. Build five stacks of [[talent:mage:Improved Scorch]] on the target, 15% more Fire damage from you, and refresh it before it falls off.
2. Fireball as the filler.
3. Fire Blast on cooldown, 2 seconds shorter with [[talent:mage:Improved Fire Blast]]. Every Fire Blast that hits adds a Heating Up stack, two if it crits.
4. Pyroblast whenever Heating Up has stacked. At three stacks the cast is 75% shorter.
5. Combustion for burst: each Fire hit adds 10% crit until you land three critical strikes.

[[talent:mage:Ignite]] burns for 40% of every Fire crit, [[talent:mage:Master of Elements]] refunds 30% of a crit's base mana cost, and [[talent:mage:Critical Mass]] adds 10% crit. [[talent:mage:Incineration]] adds 6% crit to Fire Blast and Scorch. Fire's only hit talent is [[talent:mage:Elemental Precision]] in Frost, so the build always spends three points there.

## Frost

[[build:mage-frost-raid]]

Frost is the steady spec and the best one for leveling. [[talent:mage:Ice Lance]] arrives at 31 and [[talent:mage:Fingers of Frost]] at 37.

**Single target:**

1. Frostbolt as the main spell. Frostbolt, Frost Blast and empowered Ice Lances each add a stack of [[talent:mage:Winter's Chill]] to the target, up to five, and each stack makes your next Cone of Cold deal 5% more damage to it and gain another 7% of your spell damage.
2. Cone of Cold at five stacks, when you are close enough to reach the target.
3. When Fingers of Frost procs, cast Ice Lance twice. Each counts as hitting a Frozen target: 200% more damage and 50% more crit from [[talent:mage:Shatter]].
4. Cold Snap to reset Ice Barrier, Ice Block or Frost Nova.

[[talent:mage:Ice Shards]] doubles the critical damage bonus of Frost spells. [[talent:mage:Ice Barrier]] adds 3% Frost damage for a minute, another 3% while the shield holds, and lets you keep casting through damage while it holds: it took over the bonus Piercing Ice used to give. The points Piercing Ice and Elemental Precision freed go to [[talent:mage:Arctic Reach]] for range and [[talent:mage:Improved Cone of Cold]]. From Arcane, the build takes Arcane Concentration for free Frostbolts and [[talent:mage:Magic Absorption]] for resistances.

## Stat priorities

Blizzard has not published the spell hit cap against Forever's raid bosses; in Classic it was 16%. Arcane Focus covers 5% of that for Arcane, and Elemental Precision 6% for Fire and Frost.

| Spec | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Arcane | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect, Spirit |
| Fire | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect |
| Frost | Hit, spell power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect |

Every mage tree now rewards crit more than in Classic: Arcane Mind and Ice Shards double crit damage, and Fire feeds Ignite, Heating Up and Master of Elements. Arcane needs more Intellect and Spirit than the others to keep up with Arcane Blast's cost. Compare gear in the [item database](/items?class=mage).

## Leveling

Level as Frost. Frostbolt slows everything you hit, and the tree's early points cut its cast time and crit damage.

- Frostbolt until the target reaches you, Frost Nova, step back, and finish with Frostbolt or Fire Blast.
- From 31, Ice Lance on a Frost Nova'd target is a huge hit. Fingers of Frost turns any Frostbolt into that setup.
- Blizzard with Frost Nova and Cone of Cold handles packs once you are comfortable kiting.
- Frostfire Bolt at 40 works in any spec and slows by 40%.
- Teleport: Dalaran at 50 adds a new home port for Alliance mages. The class trainer does not sell it, and where it is learned is not known yet.
- With the Talented Legacy perk, Ice Lance comes at 26.

## Races

- **Human:** [[racial:Ruthless]] adds 1% crit to all spells and attacks, whatever you hold. [[racial:The Human Spirit]] adds 10% Spirit.
- **Gnome:** [[racial:Escape Artist]] breaks roots and snares.
- **Undead:** [[racial:Will of the Forsaken]] breaks fear, charm and sleep.
- **Troll:** [[racial:Berserking]] adds 10% casting speed for 10 seconds.
- **Orc:** new to the class. [[racial:Blood Fury]] adds 10% spell power for 15 seconds and [[racial:Shatter Curse]] removes curses and reduces magic damage taken by 5% for 8 seconds.
- **High Order Skyborne:** new to the class. [[racial:Read Leyline]] adds 50% health and mana regeneration for 15 seconds (100% once you are Attuned at a Ley Line), and [[racial:Wind Blessed]] adds 2% haste.
