A mid-week round for five classes, Riverglades and Tailoring.

## Classes

- **Paladin:** Judgement is a ranged attack. It uses your ranged hit chance and no longer triggers effects that need a melee hit. Since November 13, Judgement of Command is the exception (see that day).
- **Paladin:** the stored seals of Retribution's last talent no longer echo from Windfury Totem attacks. Blizzard's note calls the talent "Unsealed Fury"; in the game it is Unsealed Wrath.
- **Hunter:** Volley no longer triggers procs, and the channel stops when you move.
- **Druid:** Rend and Tear's 15% now counts the bleed of Pounce.
- **Priest:** Confounding Flash, the Gnome priest's racial, works in Shadowform, and so does Twilight Focus.
- **Shaman:** rank 2 of Flametongue Totem works.

## Riverglades

- The Twilight Enforcer's Smash hits much less hard, and Executioner Morg deals less damage.
- The Twilight Forgemaster's Mark of Flames adds 50 damage, not 1,000.

## Professions and items

- **Tailoring:** Red Mageweave Bag takes 5 bolts (was 4) and holds 14. Green Silk Pack takes 5 bolts (was 4) and Black Silk Pack 6 (was 5); both hold 12. Green Woolen Bag and Red Woolen Bag take 5 bolts (was 4) and hold 10.
- Bloody Claws of Attack add +1 Fist and Unarmed skill, and Earthen Silk Slippers no longer give too much spell damage.

## Quests and the world

- "Scarlet Diversions" (Western Plaguelands): the Command Tent works again.
- "The Giant in the Den" (Arathi Highlands): the Witherbark Mallet works again.
- "Freedom for All Creatures" (Feralas): the Cage Door resets.
- You can mount in the named areas of Shen'dralas.
- Blizzard turned off the Item Restoration service while it fixes a serious bug.

## What changed in Grimoire

- The Retribution builds and the Paladin guide say which Judgements are ranged and which hit gear covers them.
- The Feral leveling and PvP builds count Pounce's bleed for Rend and Tear; the Shadow leveling and Discipline PvP builds note Shadowform.
- The professions guide has the new bag recipes.
