Forever's first class tuning, with the weekly maintenance. Grimoire's trees, builds and add-on follow it since game build 1.60.1.70755.

## Hunter

- A Hunter's pet has 115% health (the 150% was a mistake).
- Arcane Shot and Summon Hawk cost 11% of base mana, and the hawk hits about 80% harder.

## Warlock

- Demons have about 15% more health, and the Imp's Firebolt hits harder at ranks 1 to 4.
- Soul Siphon counts only Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony. Wrack is reworked.
- Bane of Havoc triggers the global cooldown.

## Shaman

- Weapon imbues and totems work together: Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem both proc.
- Stormstrike deals 100% weapon damage every 8 seconds, Maelstrom Weapon gives 20% a stack and Mental Dexterity goes up to 100%.

## Druid and Warrior

- Nature's Swiftness also makes Wild Growth instant.
- Sweeping Strikes keeps its charges when no second target is hit.

## Quests

- Most quests from level 48 also reward gold.

## What changed in Grimoire

- The Warrior builds take Impale or Improved Bloodrage where Raging Blows was (gone from the game since November 3).
- Enhancement uses Windfury Weapon with Windfury Totem, its leveling bar too.
- Balance takes Nature's Majesty before Nature's Splendor, which needs two points in it in the game's own tree.
- The Warlock, Rogue, Paladin and Hunter notes follow the new numbers.
