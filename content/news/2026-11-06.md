The larger of the first two rounds: five classes, three dungeons, a Leatherworking block and many quests.

## Classes

- **Warlock:** the Voidwalker has about 40 more base health at every level. Soul Siphon no longer makes Wrack raise its own damage, which matters to every Affliction build.
- **Druid:** Shadowmeld no longer opens Ravage and Pounce to a Night Elf druid.
- **Orc:** some Warlock pet abilities gained too much from Orc Command; they no longer do.
- **Tooltips:** corrected for some Druid, Mage, Rogue and Warrior abilities.
- **Legacy:** Quick and the Dead, a Legacy talent, no longer fails now and then.
- **Cooldown Manager:** crowd control shows its real duration, diminishing returns and stun reductions included.

## Dungeons

- Maraudon: Zaetar's Spirit appears as it should.
- Blackmaw Hold and The Drowned City: creatures no longer leave the fight when they should not.
- A portal that failed to teleport now works.

## Professions and items

- Leatherworking: the Tanning Rack needs 125 skill, with its skill-ups moved to match.
- Searing Dagger now triggers only from damaging spells.

## Quests and the world

- About nine quests fixed, among them "Vorsha the Lasher", which hit everyone in the zone, and "Unending Torment", now level 15 for the Horde.
- Respawns, loot and spawns fixed in Dun Morogh, Durotar, Riverglades and Swamp of Sorrows.
- Other players and their targets are easier to see from far away.
- The Thunder Bluff barbershop is no longer too dark.

The same day, Blizzard announced class tuning for November 10: Hunter pets and Voidwalkers as tanks, Affliction's damage over time beside Wrack, and Strength for Enhancement Shamans.
