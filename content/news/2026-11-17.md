The week's maintenance (November 17, 18 in Europe) brought client build 1.60.1.70959 in place of 1.60.1.70938. The Auction House changes Blizzard withdrew on November 11 were due with it; its list had not put them back on November 18.

## Dungeons

- **Upper Blackrock Spire.** Its enemies had far less health and damage than intended. They are back to what Blizzard meant, and in its words the run again takes a full group of careful players. The Onyxia attunement goes through it: the [raids preview](/guides/raids-preview) has the steps.

## Classes and items

- **Paladin:** Soulforge Armor, the upgraded dungeon set for tanks, has Spell Power where it had Strength. Blizzard says it is leaning into Spell Power as the stat a Paladin tank deals damage with.
- **Shaman:** Windfury Totem attacks no longer use up Flurry's charges.
- **Priest:** a Shadow Priest can hold a Night Watchman's Torch.
- Might of Stormwind and Warchief's Blessing always end when you die, and no longer land on anything but players.
- [Whiteout Staff](/items/19101) and [Crackling Staff](/items/19102) have the same stats: 25 Stamina, 25 Intellect and 74 Spell Power from their weapon damage. Escape Plan has the range and damage it should.

## Battlegrounds

- The Battle for Alterac Valley quests ask for level 58: [[quest:7141]] and [[quest:7142]] asked for 51 in Classic.
- No more mounting up in the starting rooms of Warsong Gulch.

## Camping and Legacy

- A Cozy Sleeping Bag is set down where you choose, like a hunter's trap, and a Resting aura shows while you stand in its area. You can no longer sleep on it mounted.
- The Legacy perk Reagent Economy no longer stops working at random.

## Professions

- Blacksmithing: [[quest:2756]] gives the Orcish War Leggings plans as an item. If you never got them or lost them, Sumi in the blacksmithing hut nearby replaces them once the quest is done. Bronze Mace binds when equipped.
- Herbalism: Cultivation works on Mageroyal, and Find Herbs shows the wildflowers of [[quest:95975]] only while you are on the quest.
- Mining: miners can track Spectral Iron Ore in Shen'dralas, and it spawns more often.
- Skinning: a few creatures that were never meant to be skinned no longer can be. Paletusks can no longer be pickpocketed.
- Waylaid Crates: some conversions gave an obsolete turn-in item, and those crates can now be handed in for the right reward.

## Quests

- [[quest:6681]]: Mathias Shaw has the letter rogues were waiting for.
- [[quest:94483]] shows on your map.
- [[quest:275]]: more Fen Creepers in the Wetlands. [[quest:1153]]: more Gravelsnout Diggers and Surveyors.
- [[quest:95183]]: its rock piles are tracked by Find Minerals while you are on it, where it was Find Treasure.
- **[Shen'dralas](/world/16651):** a pylon you repair for [[quest:96018]] counts for party members nearby, the Depleted Crystal Heart of "Heart of Crystal" drops for the whole party, and Rokren runs more slowly in [[quest:93125]]. Bristleback Skullcrushers and Decrepit Pylon Protectors cast less often, and the necromancers' Summon Undead Ravager can be interrupted. [[quest:93604]] is no longer a dungeon quest: it is done in the elite area outside Razorfen Downs.
- Thousand Needles: a Pesterhide Snarler or a Needles Cougar no longer spawns inside a rock.

## Interface

- Your ignore list survives a logout.

## Grimoire 1.0.8, November 18

A map for every creature, quest and zone, with the add-on's arrow to any point of them, these hotfixes in the data, and QuestieDB 1.1.3 with the routes worked out again on it.

- **Where to find it.** Every creature that stands in the open world has a map on its page: [Kobold Vermin](/npcs/6) in Northshire, [Chicken](/npcs/620) in six zones, a tab for each. 6,884 creatures are placed, on 111,076 points. Point at a mark for its coordinates, click it to keep them, and "Look closer" when the marks crowd a corner of the zone. A creature of a dungeon links to the dungeon's own map.
- **Quest maps.** A [quest page](/quests/7) shows who gives the quest, who takes it and where what it asks for is found: the creatures and objects it names, and for an item the three likeliest to drop it.
- **Zone maps.** Each of the 50 zones and capitals drawn has its map on its page, [Elwynn Forest](/world/12#map) or [Stormwind City](/world/1519#map): flight masters, innkeepers, repairs and rares, and quest givers, vendors, trainers, banks, auction houses and stables to turn on. A mark opens its page. The ways into dungeons are in green.
- **How the maps are made.** They are Grimoire's own drawings, with no game art. A zone's land is the outline around every place where [QuestieDB](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB) and VMaNGOS put a creature, a patrol or an object, the land beside it is drawn fainter, and the place names are VMaNGOS's. The marks are QuestieDB's, on Forever's own maps: Classic's spawn points, and for Forever's creatures the places where Questie's players met them. [Riverglades](/world/16591#map), [Mount Hyjal](/world/616#map), [Shen’dralas](/world/16651#map) and [Zephras Isle](/world/16593#map) are drawn from those alone, so they have no place names yet.
- **The arrow in game.** Keep a point, click "Copy for the add-on" and paste the line in the game's chat, `/grim go 12 48.9 36.4 Kobold Vermin`: the add-on's arrow and the map pin lead you there. `/grim go off` goes back to your route. [Grimoire in game](/tools/addon) 1.0.8 has the command.
- **Hotfixes in the data.** The spell and talent pages carry the changes of November 16 and 17, 18 quests say what changed for them, the item pages of [Soulforge Armor](/items/22089), the two staves and [Taran Icebreaker](/items/2915) say what Blizzard changed, and the guides follow: [Priest](/guides/priest), [Shaman](/guides/shaman), [Warrior](/guides/warrior), [Druid](/guides/druid), [Rogue](/guides/rogue), [Paladin](/guides/paladin), [Professions](/guides/professions), [Fishing](/guides/fishing), [PvP](/guides/pvp), [New dungeons](/guides/new-dungeons) and the [raids preview](/guides/raids-preview), 21 days before the raids open.
- **QuestieDB 1.1.3.** Its traces of November 17 bring 1,733 of Forever's own quests (67 new to Grimoire) and the 74 creatures, 9 objects and 21 items they name. The [quest database](/quests) has 5,885 quests.
- **Routes.** All 56 [leveling routes](/tools/leveling) were worked out again on it. 535 of Forever's quests are on at least one route (505), between 166 and 270 on each. Together the routes grind 287.5 levels (293.4), and a median route takes 85.6 hours (86.5): 43 routes are shorter, 4 take as long and 9 are longer, by 1.3 hours at most. Eight routes took another of the engine's rules to stay within what they were as released, and the Orc and Troll Warrior step from Desolace into Shen’dralas at 36 for two quests of the chain that opens it.
