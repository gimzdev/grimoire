Five classes, a dungeon and a handful of quests. Blizzard's list has no entry for November 14 or 15. Most of these are fixes to how abilities work: the tooltips stay as they were, and no Grimoire build moves a point for them.

## Classes

- **Shaman:** Windfury Totem's extra attacks no longer ignore armor and have an internal cooldown, and they generate Rage as they should. Mutilate and Execute no longer get a second try at the totem, and Shield Slam, Shield Bash and a Holy Strike empowered by Shieldworn cannot trigger it. For Enhancement, Maelstrom Weapon can no longer benefit two casts, and Thundering Strikes gives its critical strike chance without a weapon equipped.
- **Warrior:** Cleave and Heroic Strike activate Unbridled Wrath and Reckless Swings, and they no longer use up Flurry's charges. The Revered Champion offers Legacy of Valor to the Warriors who qualify.
- **Priest:** Dark Inspiration no longer stacks when several Shadow Priests share a party. With the weekly maintenance (November 17, 18 in Europe), Blizzard's notes give Vampiric Embrace 10% of your Shadow damage as healing, where it gave 20%, and the Priest who casts it twice as much: nothing changes for a Priest alone, and the party gets half. On November 18 the client's own files still read 20% (wowforevertalents.com), so look at the healing you see in game.
- **Druid:** each rank of Wild Growth goes on cooldown as it should, and Genesis raises the damage of Pounce's bleed.
- **Rogue:** poisons can no longer go above their highest rank. Shank on a target without your combo points no longer removes the ones you have on another target, with or without Boot Knife.

## Dungeons

- [The Drowned City](/dungeons/drowned-city): Captain Dreadrise can be stunned, and the Abyssal Bubble he summons can no longer block, dodge or parry. The rare "Pyromancer" Braxton fights back when attacked.
- Sunken Temple: the Atal'alarion serpent statues of the Pit of Refuse no longer lock up when used in the wrong order.

## Items and professions

- Formula: Enchant Boots - Speed, the one bought with reputation, binds when picked up.
- Outrider's Gloves work, and The Violet Tragan no longer breaks crowd control.
- Camping: a Fishing Rack gives its lures to everyone, and the Bloated Fish caught with them drop more often. The [fishing guide](/guides/fishing) says where to fish at each skill.

## Quests

- [[quest:96334]]: the bandanas drop more often.
- [[quest:80411]]: Witch Doctor Zum'rah spawns as he should.
- [[quest:94888]]: a scout prisoner freed in Twilight's Shroud counts for the whole party.
- [[quest:3520]]: its Vale Screamers are on the coast of the Isle of Dread.
- [[quest:1878]], the one you can repeat, gives no experience any more.
- [[quest:95198]], in Theramore, can no longer get stuck.

## Battlegrounds

- Dying in the Gold Mine of Arathi Basin no longer sends you to a graveyard in Riverglades.
