The biggest round since launch. The live client is build 1.60.1.70938 since this day.

## Dungeons

- A kill gives no experience to anyone in the party when the enemy is trivial to one member. A high-level friend can no longer pull you through a dungeon for experience: the quests and your own kills at your level are what pay.
- The Drowned City: enemies other than bosses come back after 2 hours, not 1.
- The Deadmines: Sneed is thrown out of his Shredder as he should be when it is stunned at 0% health.
- Stratholme: Balnazzar counts for the Legacy Challenge "Master Spelunker".

## Classes

- **Mage:** Frost Nova's freeze breaks on Blizzard's damage and on damage from procs. Frost Nova then Blizzard no longer holds a pack.
- **Druid:** Wild Growth cannot be cast while moving and gains 6.6% of your healing power (was 3.3%). The client of this day, read by foreverchanges.pro, also has its three ranks sharing one cooldown; Blizzard has not posted that.
- **Paladin:** Judgement of Command is a melee attack again: melee hit, and it triggers on-hit effects. The other Judgements stay ranged.
- **Shaman:** Windfury Totem triggers from every melee ability. The main-hand strike of an ability can trigger it; the off-hand strike (Whirlwind, Mutilate) cannot.
- **Priest:** the Legacy perk The Quick and the Dead lowers Divine Spirit's cost as it should.
- **Warlock:** rank 1 of Siphon Life can no longer be cast while silenced. **Warrior:** Berserker Rage removes Sap.
- **Stuns:** stuns between players and creatures have diminishing returns again, both ways. A second stun on the same target soon after the first is shorter.

## Items

- Taran Icebreaker is a two-handed mace, as Grimoire's item page already shows it.
- Blessed Band of Light loses its hit. Until the raids open on December 9 you can trade it at Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay for a Channeler's Ring, which now gives spell power instead of Spirit. The trade cannot be undone.
- Gloves of Holy Might only activate from harmful spells and abilities that deal damage. The Darkspear signets give the same mana regeneration as the Alliance ones, and The Bad Hat, the Souvenir Sea Shell and the Everstaring Abomination Eye have corrected stats. Glimmering Mithril Insignia no longer needs Mail.

## Quests

- Five quest drops go to every party member on the quest: Deadwood Key, Drop of Stolen Life, Ring of the Fallen Soldier, Venom-dripping Heart and Blackthorne Intelligence.
- Pix Xizzix's weekly dungeon quests leave your quest log at the weekly reset.
- Fixes to "The Talisman of Kazdor", "A Quick Grocery Run", "Coyote Huntin'", "Crate Expectations", "Return to the Port", "Yashiro's Windwalk", "Gordunni Cobalt", "Gaseous Gold" and the Riverglades woodpiles, which only those on the quest can loot.
- The Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza lets many more players win each ruleset.

## Interface

- Your action bars no longer reset at logout once you have Secondary Talents, the second specialization Blizzard opens at level 40.

## Grimoire 1.0.6, November 14

Every build checked against these hotfixes and compared with the guides, Forever's own quests and zones on the leveling routes, the new dungeons' bosses, respec paths and four new guides, on the site and in the add-on.

- **Builds.** All 84 builds were checked on November 14 against the live trees, the tooltips and Blizzard's hotfixes, and every leveling, raid, tank and healing build was compared with the Icy Veins guide and Wowhead's or Mobalytics'. Each [build page](/builds) says what it was checked against and when, and links the guides to compare it with; 22 builds have two guides that make the same main choices. 24 orders changed, among them: Arms leveling takes Deflection, Improved Overpower at 15 and Sweeping Strikes at 30; Retribution leveling opens with Deflection and takes Pursuit of Justice at 20; Protection paladin leveling starts with Benediction and Pursuit of Justice; Beast Mastery hunters take Deadly Aspects at 10 and Pathfinding at 20; Marksmanship takes Mortal Shots; Combat rogues take Riposte at 20; Enhancement reaches Rage of the Farseer at 40; Affliction takes Pandemic at 22 and Malevolence at 27 and drops Amplify Curse and Curse of Exhaustion. The Frost AoE build and its action bar are built around the slow, and Wild Growth, Judgement, Windfury Totem, Pounce's bleed, creature stuns and Berserker Rage have new notes on the builds they touch.
- **Forever's quests.** Grimoire now reads [QuestieDB](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB) 1.1.1, the database behind Questie, under the GPLv3, with its traces of November 13 (credited on the [legal page](/legal)). The [quest database](/quests) has 1,665 of Forever's own quests, 902 of them new to Grimoire; 1,538 name their giver and 1,482 where they are handed in.
- **Routes.** The [leveling routes](/tools/leveling) take 488 of those quests, between 141 and 239 on each route. Every route has steps in [Riverglades](/world/16591) (at 41 to 45) and [Mount Hyjal](/world/616) (at 55 to 59), and the ten Skyborne routes start on [Zephras Isle](/world/16593). There the steps follow the places QuestieDB traced on each zone's own map; distances and times are estimates, because no open data gives the size of those maps; and quests QuestieDB does not place completely are not on the routes. A trip through several zones is one step ("Go to Badlands, through Hillsbrad Foothills, Arathi Highlands, ..."), and a grind step says how much of the level is left; 499 of the 955 grind steps offer a dungeon whose quests give part of that level. Medians of the 56 routes: 64 "Go to" steps (was 97.5, with every zone crossed as its own step), 12 continent changes (was 16), 7 visits that hand in fewer than three quests (was 23), 17 grind steps (was 19.5), 88.7 hours (was 101.1). Rather than kill its way through most of a level, a route now crosses the sea to the quests of that level: fewer levels of grinding (a median of 8.7, in 17 grind steps), more boats and zeppelins (a median of 8 a route) and fewer hours.
- **Forever's zones.** QuestieDB has 172 quests in Riverglades, most of them for levels 38 to 44, 35 in [Shen’dralas](/world/16651) (40 to 43), 138 in Mount Hyjal (56 to 60) and 149 on Zephras Isle. The route notes on Riverglades and Mount Hyjal say how many of their quests the route takes and why the others are on none; the [leveling guide](/guides/leveling) says how each zone is routed. Shen’dralas has no steps: its way in follows Desolace's centaur reputation quests, which the routes leave out.
- **New dungeons and raids.** Each new dungeon and both new raids list their bosses, 62 in all, where Blizzard names them or two independent sites agree, and each page says who names them; no open database has their abilities or loot. The [new dungeons guide](/guides/new-dungeons) says where each entrance is. [The Hall of Thanes](/dungeons/hall-of-thanes), [Ruins of Lordaeron](/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron), [Whelgar’s Excavation](/dungeons/whelgars-excavation), [City of Dalaran](/dungeons/city-of-dalaran) and [The Shaper’s Terrace](/dungeons/shapers-terrace) list 45 quests between them (8, for two, before). The [raids preview](/guides/raids-preview) gives each raid's bosses, entrance and what to do before December 9.
- **Respec paths.** [Discipline leveling](/builds/priest/discipline-leveling) turns into [Shadow leveling](/builds/priest/shadow-leveling) at 40, and 21 leveling builds turn into their raid or tank build at 60. Their build pages mark the level in the point table and give the price: 1 gold for a first reset, 5 for a second, never more than 50, or Secondary Talents for 50 gold to keep both. In the [talent calculator](/talents), the level buttons follow a build through its respec.
- **Four new guides:** [Hunter pets](/guides/hunter-pets) (the family for leveling, dungeons, raids and PvP, the 18 families' abilities, and 474 tameable beasts by level and zone), [Professions 1 to 300](/guides/professions-1-to-300) (a recipe for each stretch of skill in Leatherworking, Tailoring, Blacksmithing, Engineering, Enchanting and Alchemy, where every pattern past 225 comes from, and Skinning by beast level), [Fishing by skill](/guides/fishing) (the fishing level and the catch of 48 zones and subzones) and [Sayge and the Darkmoon Faire](/guides/darkmoon-faire) (the answers for each of the eight fortunes, and how the add-on's timer counts). Their tables come from the VMaNGOS database and Grimoire's data, with Forever's changes applied.

The add-on, [Grimoire in game](/tools/addon), has the same builds, routes, dungeons, respecs and guides in 1.0.6. After a reset at your class trainer it follows the new build.
