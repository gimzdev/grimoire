The first hotfixes came a day after launch, mostly for the starting zones. Like every hotfix, they need no download.

## Quests and creatures

- In Durotar, Yarrog Baneshadow respawned more slowly than he should; he no longer does.
- For "Escorting Erland", Deathstalker Erland now comes back much sooner.
- The text of "The Sisterhood of Elune" was corrected.

## Battlegrounds

- Plainsrunning, the Tauren racial, changed for flag carriers.
