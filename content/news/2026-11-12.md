Two notices, no new fixes.

## Shaman

The Shaman changes in the hotfixes of November 10 were late to deploy. They reached every region on November 12 at 10:45 a.m. Pacific time: weapon imbues and weapon totems work together, Stormstrike deals 100% weapon damage every 8 seconds, Mental Dexterity turns 33%, 66% and 100% of your Intellect into Attack Power, and Maelstrom Weapon gives 20% a stack at every rank and procs 6 to 10 times a minute by rank. Grimoire's trees have shown them since November 10.

## Auction House

The Auction House changes of November 11 were taken out again. Blizzard puts them back with the weekly maintenance of November 17 (November 18 in Europe). As its list first described them: the smallest deposit drops from 1 silver to 1 copper, auction mail stops coming from "Stormwind Auction House" for everyone, and mail that arrived after about 2 minutes takes the full 60 again. Until then the smallest deposit is 1 silver.

## What changed in Grimoire

- The Enhancement builds say when the changes went live, and the leveling build reaches Rage of the Farseer at 40.
