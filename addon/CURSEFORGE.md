## Description

**A leveling guide, talent builds and dungeon maps for WoW Forever, inside your game.** Grimoire is the add-on of [wowgrimoire.app](https://wowgrimoire.app/?utm_source=curseforge). Type `/grim`.

Free, with no ads and no paywall. Questions, ideas and bugs: [Discord](https://discord.gg/gY5PsymH). [Support on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/gimzdev)

### Leveling

- **Your own route to 60** for your race and class, Skyborne included: an arrow, the distance, the walking time and the experience each step gives (in Riverglades, Mount Hyjal and on Zephras Isle the distance and time are estimates: no open data gives the size of their maps). Steps tick themselves, and the route's quests are taken and handed in for you (hold Shift to do it yourself).
- **On your world map**: your next places, and a mark at every giver with a quest you can take now.

### Talents, spells and bars

- **84 builds**, a leveling, PvE and PvP build for every spec, point by point: each level-up tells you where the point goes. Make your own, or paste a wowgrimoire.app link.
- **Action bars** to match each build, set up in one click and undone in one.
- **What to train now**, marked at your trainer.

### Gear and gold

- **Upgrade scores** in item tooltips, the quest reward to take, and your best upgrade for each slot with where it drops.
- **Vendor and auction prices** in tooltips, and a **selling tab** at the auction house that prices what is in your bags. Every post is one click of yours.

### Dungeon and raid maps

- **A map inside every Classic dungeon and raid**, where Forever shows none, with each boss where it stands. Hover a boss for its abilities and best loot; click it for everything. Wings and floors get their own map.
- **Before you go**: the dungeon's quests, ticked from your quest log, with an arrow to each giver.

### And more

- Rare alerts, a kill bar that turns gold when your DoTs will finish the target, a raid buff check, your Legacy challenges across the account with a perk planner, **Sayge's fortune timer** for the Darkmoon Faire, and every Grimoire guide, readable in game.
- **Light** or **Complete**: just the guide window, tooltips and level-up notes, or everything.

### Good to know

- Item stats, drop chances and boss abilities are Classic's (patch 1.12) wherever Forever has published nothing of its own, with Forever's published stat rules applied.
- It sends nothing anywhere and saves only your settings, builds and progress.
