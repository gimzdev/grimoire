# Changelog

## 1.0.1

Game data updated to Blizzard's server hotfixes of October 20 and 22 (talentsforever.com export of October 23).

- **Mage:** Piercing Ice is gone, Elemental Precision has three ranks for 6% hit, Ice Barrier adds Frost damage, Winter's
  Chill feeds your Cone of Cold, Frost Warding strengthens Ice Barrier and Frost Ward, and four Frost talents moved
  within their rows. Grimoire's Fire and Frost builds are rebuilt for the new trees: Fire puts its two freed points in
  Burning Soul and Improved Flamestrike, Frost takes Arctic Reach and Improved Cone of Cold.
- **Paladin:** Champion of the Light turns 60% of your Intellect into spell damage.
- **Warlock:** Amplify Curse adds 20% to Curse of Exhaustion.
- **Training:** Hunter's Mark (rank 4) and Holy Fire (rank 1) carry their new values.
- **Racials** (Blizzard's changes of October 27):
  - Human: Ruthless gives 1% crit (was 2%), The Human Spirit 10% Spirit (was 5%).
  - Dwarf: Artisan's Precision, 1% hit with spells and attacks, replaces Earth Fury.
  - Orc: Command, 5% Hunter and Warlock pet damage, replaces Savage.
  - Tauren: Endurance is 5% Health only. Plainsrunning builds 2% a step up to 60%, and no longer speeds your mount.
  - Skyborne: Wind Blessed gives 2% haste (was 3%). Read Leyline adds 50% regeneration for 15 sec, 100% for an hour
    once Attuned at a Ley Line, and not while Innervate is on you. Skysight adds 15% movement speed for 15 sec, 30% for
    an hour once Charged at an elemental convergence. Attuned and Charged last through death.
- The Mage and Paladin guides follow the changes, and every class guide's race advice follows the new racials.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- **Leveling guide**: your own route to 60 for your race and class, class quests included, in a slim window with an
  arrow, the distance, the walking time and the experience each quest gives, your level's progress under it. Steps
  tick themselves, and the route's quests are taken and handed in for you (hold Shift to do it yourself).
- **On your world map**: your next places, and a quest mark at every giver with a quest you can take now.
- **Gear**: upgrades for your build in item tooltips, the quest reward to take, and your best upgrade for each slot.
- **Dungeons and raids**: bosses in order with their abilities and loot, and all of each dungeon's quests.
- **Rare alerts**, and a **kill bar** on your target's health that fills as your DoTs cover what it has left (the idea
  comes from Does It Die by JoeGG; Grimoire's code is its own).
- **Talents and training**: Grimoire's builds point by point, your own builds, and what to train.
- **Raid check** and **Legacy**: your group's buffs, the challenges across your account and a perk planner.
- **Light or Complete**: just the essentials, or everything. In Options, or `/grim light` and `/grim complete`.

Credits for the game data are in `NOTICE.md`.
